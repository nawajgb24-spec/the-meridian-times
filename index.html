Composed during his influential tenure in the United States from 1892 to 1895, the work masterfully fuses European classical tradition with inspirations from American folk idioms, including spirituals and Native American melodies. Its grand premiere in 1893 at Carnegie Hall solidified its place as an immediate triumph, celebrated for its memorable themes, dramatic scope, and profound emotional resonance.
