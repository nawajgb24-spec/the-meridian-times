Decades of Vigilance: The Fight Against the New World Screwworm

The United States Department of Agriculture's Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS) leads a long-standing, globally recognized effort to prevent the re-establishment of the New World Screwworm (Cochliomyia hominivorax). This parasitic fly, once a scourge of livestock and wildlife across the Americas, was eradicated from North and Central America through the innovative Sterile Insect Technique (SIT). Despite its success, constant surveillance, robust diagnostic capabilities, and the critical Panama barrier zone remain essential to protect animal health and prevent devastating economic and ecological impacts.
