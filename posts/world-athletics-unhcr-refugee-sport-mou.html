World Athletics and UNHCR Forge Partnership to Empower Refugees Through Sport

World Athletics and the UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR) have formalized their commitment to working with refugees globally through the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) in December 2023. This strategic partnership aims to leverage the unifying power of athletics to promote protection, well-being, and social inclusion for displaced communities. The agreement, signed by World Athletics President Sebastian Coe and UNHCR Assistant High Commissioner for Operations Raouf Mazou, marks a significant step in integrating sport into humanitarian efforts, building upon existing initiatives and setting a framework for future collaborative projects designed to provide opportunities and support for refugee athletes worldwide.

### Introduction MONACO — World Athletics, the international governing body for athletics, has officially solidified its commitment to humanitarian efforts by signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the United Nations Refugee Agency (UNHCR). This significant agreement, concluded in December 2023, establishes a formal framework for collaboration, aiming to harness the transformative power of sport to support refugees globally. The partnership seeks to integrate athletics into broader humanitarian initiatives, providing a platform for protection, well-being, and social inclusion for displaced populations. The MOU underscores a shared belief that sport can serve as a vital tool for resilience, healing, and community building, offering a sense of normalcy and opportunity amidst challenging circumstances. ### Background World Athletics has a history of engaging with social responsibility through its ‘Athletics for a Better World’ programme, which addresses various global challenges including peace, health, and gender equality. This initiative has often included projects aimed at supporting vulnerable communities, aligning naturally with the needs of refugees. UNHCR, established in 1950, is mandated to lead and co-ordinate international action to protect refugees and resolve refugee problems worldwide. The agency champions the use of sport as a critical component in its protection and education programmes, recognizing its ability to foster physical and mental health, promote integration, and provide a sense of purpose for those uprooted from their homes. Previous collaborations between sports organizations and UNHCR have demonstrated the tangible benefits of such partnerships. Sport offers a universal language and a structured environment that can help refugees, particularly children and youth, cope with trauma, develop life skills, and build friendships in new communities. ### Timeline **Ongoing Engagement:** For several years, World Athletics, through its various programmes and federations, has informally supported initiatives benefiting refugee athletes and communities. This has included advocating for the inclusion of refugee athletes in international competitions and providing assistance to those seeking to continue their athletic careers. **Early Collaborations:** Prior to the formal MOU, World Athletics and UNHCR had engaged in discussions and smaller-scale cooperative projects. These interactions often focused on specific events or targeted support mechanisms, laying the groundwork for a more comprehensive alliance. **December 2023:** The formal Memorandum of Understanding was signed at the World Athletics headquarters in Monaco. World Athletics President Sebastian Coe and UNHCR Assistant High Commissioner for Operations Raouf Mazou officiated the signing, marking a new chapter in their collaborative efforts. ### Latest Developments The recently signed MOU outlines a multi-faceted approach to collaboration. Key objectives include promoting access to athletics for refugees, providing technical support and resources for the development of sports programmes in refugee settings, and advocating for the rights and recognition of refugee athletes on a global stage. Specific areas of cooperation encompass the development of grassroots athletics programmes within refugee camps and host communities, the training of coaches and volunteers from refugee backgrounds, and the provision of equipment. The partnership also aims to facilitate pathways for talented refugee athletes to compete at national and international levels, thereby offering them opportunities for personal growth and empowerment. Furthermore, the MOU emphasizes joint advocacy efforts to raise awareness about the challenges faced by refugees and the positive role sport can play in their lives. This includes leveraging World Athletics’ global platform to share stories of resilience and achievement from refugee athletes. ### Analysis This formal partnership between World Athletics and UNHCR represents a strategic alignment of missions. For World Athletics, it reinforces its commitment to social responsibility and expands the reach of its 'Athletics for a Better World' programme into critical humanitarian domains. It allows the organization to contribute meaningfully to global issues beyond the competitive arena. For UNHCR, the MOU provides access to World Athletics’ expertise, infrastructure, and global network, enhancing the agency's capacity to deliver impactful sports-based interventions. The technical knowledge and organizational capabilities of an international sports federation can significantly bolster UNHCR's existing programmes, leading to more structured and sustainable athletic opportunities for refugees. The agreement also highlights a growing trend among major international sports organizations to formalize their engagement with humanitarian causes. It acknowledges that sport is not merely a form of entertainment but a powerful tool for social development, peacebuilding, and individual empowerment, particularly for vulnerable populations. ### Public Response The signing of the MOU has been met with positive sentiment from within both the sports and humanitarian sectors. Official statements from World Athletics and UNHCR emphasized the mutual benefits and shared vision for empowering refugees through sport. Sebastian Coe, President of World Athletics, underscored the universal appeal of athletics and its capacity to offer hope and structure. Raouf Mazou of UNHCR highlighted sport's role in promoting physical and mental health, education, and social cohesion among displaced people. While broad public response has yet to be extensively gauged, such partnerships generally receive support from advocacy groups and NGOs working with refugees, who often seek more resources and attention for the well-being and integration of displaced individuals. Specific comments from refugee communities or individual athletes regarding the new agreement remain unconfirmed. ### Industry Impact The formalization of this partnership is expected to set a precedent for other international sports federations. It demonstrates a clear model for how global sports bodies can systematically integrate humanitarian objectives into their strategic planning and operations, moving beyond ad-hoc charity to structured, long-term collaboration. This could encourage other sports organizations to review and enhance their engagement with refugee support, potentially leading to more widespread adoption of sports-for-development programmes in humanitarian contexts. It may also facilitate greater resource allocation towards providing athletic opportunities for displaced populations across various sports disciplines. Furthermore, the collaboration could foster cross-sector learning, allowing humanitarian agencies to better understand the operational aspects of sports development and enabling sports federations to gain deeper insights into the complex realities of humanitarian work. This synergy has the potential to innovate programme design and delivery. ### Future Outlook Looking ahead, the MOU establishes a foundation for a range of collaborative projects. Initial steps are likely to focus on assessing specific needs in key refugee-hosting regions and identifying existing World Athletics programmes or resources that can be adapted for refugee communities. Long-term goals include developing sustainable athletics ecosystems within refugee settings, ensuring that programmes are culturally sensitive and responsive to local contexts. There is also an emphasis on creating measurable impacts, tracking the participation, well-being, and skill development of refugee athletes. The success of this partnership will depend on sustained commitment, effective resource mobilization, and continuous dialogue between World Athletics and UNHCR. The aim is not just to provide temporary relief but to empower refugees with skills, confidence, and a sense of belonging that can contribute to their long-term integration and self-reliance. ### Conclusion The MOU between World Athletics and UNHCR represents a significant and forward-thinking alliance. By formalizing their commitment, both organizations are poised to make a profound difference in the lives of refugees, utilizing the inherent values of athletics – discipline, teamwork, perseverance, and fair play – to foster hope and opportunity. This partnership not only elevates the profile of refugee issues within the global sports community but also provides a concrete pathway for structured, impactful interventions. As the world grapples with increasing displacement, such collaborations underscore the crucial role that all sectors, including sports, can play in building a more inclusive and resilient global society.
