Chinese Taipei Dominates Mexico with No-Hitter in LLWS Game 20

Chinese Taipei delivered a commanding 10-0 victory over Mexico in Game 20 of the 2023 Little League Baseball World Series. Pitcher Fan Chen-Jun threw a no-hitter, leading his team to a mercy-rule win in just four innings. This decisive performance secured Chinese Taipei's advancement in the International Bracket, while Mexico's tournament journey concluded.

### Introduction Williamsport, Pennsylvania — The 2023 Little League Baseball World Series (LLWS) witnessed a dominant performance in Game 20, as the Asia-Pacific representative, Chinese Taipei, secured a resounding 10-0 victory over Mexico. The contest, held on August 21 at Howard J. Lamade Stadium, concluded in a mere four innings due to the mercy rule, highlighting Chinese Taipei's exceptional talent and strategic execution. The decisive win was anchored by an outstanding no-hitter from pitcher Fan Chen-Jun, who silenced Mexico's bats while his offense consistently plated runs. This result not only marked a significant moment in the tournament but also propelled Chinese Taipei further into contention within the competitive International Bracket. ### Background The Little League Baseball World Series, an annual tournament for children aged 10 to 12, represents the pinnacle of youth baseball. The 2023 edition featured 20 teams, divided into U.S. and International brackets. Each team embarks on a journey through regional qualifiers to earn their coveted spot in Williamsport. The tournament format is a modified double-elimination, allowing teams a chance to recover from an early loss, though consecutive defeats lead to elimination. Chinese Taipei has historically been a powerhouse in the LLWS, boasting numerous championships. Their return to Williamsport in 2023 was met with anticipation, given their reputation for disciplined play and strong fundamental skills. Fan Chen-Jun was exceptional from the outset, displaying pinpoint control and an impressive array of pitches. He consistently retired Mexican batters, preventing any base runners through the first two innings, maintaining the 3-0 lead. The third inning saw Chinese Taipei extend their advantage. They added two more runs to their tally, bringing the score to 5-0. This further solidified their control over the game, placing increased pressure on the Mexican squad to generate offense against a dominant pitcher. As the game moved into the bottom of the fourth inning, Chinese Taipei continued their relentless attack. With a 5-0 lead, they produced an explosive offensive surge, scoring five additional runs. This brought their total to 10 runs, reaching the threshold for the Little League mercy rule. With Chinese Taipei holding a 10-run lead after four complete innings, the game was called. The final score stood at 10-0, marking a swift and conclusive end to Mexico's LLWS campaign. ### Latest Developments The immediate aftermath of Game 20 saw Chinese Taipei celebrating a significant milestone.
