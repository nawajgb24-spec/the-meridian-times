## ASUS Earns Spot on TIME's Prestigious 2026 World's Best Companies List **Taipei, Taiwan** – ASUS, the renowned multinational computer hardware and electronics company, has reportedly been named to TIME magazine's 2026 World's Best Companies List. The prestigious inclusion signals a significant milestone for the Taiwanese tech giant, acknowledging its consistent excellence across key performance indicators and its strategic vision for the future. The announcement, arriving well in advance of the 2026 list's formal release, highlights ASUS's global standing. It recognizes the company's sustained efforts in innovation, financial robustness, and commitment to corporate responsibility, factors consistently evaluated by TIME in collaboration with data research firm Statista. ### Background ASUS was founded in 1989 in Taipei, Taiwan, by a team of former Acer engineers. From its inception, the company quickly established itself as a leading manufacturer of motherboards, becoming a foundational component supplier for the burgeoning personal computer industry. Over more than three decades, ASUS has expanded its product portfolio dramatically. Today, it is recognized globally for a diverse range of computer hardware and electronics, including high-performance laptops, gaming equipment under its Republic of Gamers (ROG) brand, monitors, smartphones, and networking solutions. Its enduring philosophy has revolved around delivering innovative technologies and premium quality. This approach has allowed ASUS to carve out significant market share in various highly competitive segments, solidifying its reputation as a technology pioneer. ### Timeline **1989:** ASUS (ASUSTeK Computer Inc.) is founded in Taipei, Taiwan. The company initially focuses on developing and manufacturing motherboards. **Early 1990s:** ASUS quickly gains industry recognition for its high-quality motherboards, establishing itself as a reliable partner for PC component manufacturers worldwide. **Late 1990s - Early 2000s:** The company diversifies its product lines, venturing into graphics cards, optical drives, and expanding its presence in the broader PC components market. **2007:** ASUS launches the revolutionary Eee PC, a series of netbooks that significantly impacted the portable computing market by offering affordable, compact, and functional devices, earning widespread consumer appeal. **2008:** A strategic restructuring sees ASUS spinning off its manufacturing operations into Pegatron and Unihan Technology, allowing ASUS to focus primarily on brand and product development. **2010s:** The company reinforces its commitment to gaming innovation with the Republic of Gamers (ROG) brand, which becomes a dominant force in the high-performance gaming hardware sector. ASUS also expands its presence in the smartphone and convertible laptop markets. **2020s:** ASUS intensifies its focus on emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI) integration in PCs, sustainable product design, and professional creator workstations, aligning with evolving industry demands and environmental concerns. **2026 (Reported):** ASUS is named to TIME's World's Best Companies List, a testament to its consistent growth, employee welfare, and commitment to ESG principles. ### Latest Developments While the official publication of TIME's 2026 World's Best Companies list is still some time away, the early announcement of ASUS's inclusion signals a forward-looking assessment of the company's trajectory and sustained performance.
