Alison dos Santos Shatters 400m Hurdles World Record in Zurich

Brazilian athlete Alison dos Santos has made history at the Zurich Diamond League, breaking the men's 400m hurdles world record with an extraordinary time of 45.80 seconds. This unprecedented performance marks the first time an athlete has run under 46 seconds in the event, solidifying his place in athletics history.

Zurich, Switzerland – Brazilian sensation Alison dos Santos has rewritten the history books, delivering an awe-inspiring performance at the Zurich Diamond League to set a new world record in the men's 400m hurdles. Dos Santos stormed across the finish line in an astonishing 45.80 seconds, becoming the first human to ever dip below the 46-second barrier in the demanding event. The historic run on Sunday evening at the Letzigrund Stadium sent shockwaves through the athletics world, confirming Dos Santos's status as a true generational talent.
