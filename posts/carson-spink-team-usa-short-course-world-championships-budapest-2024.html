Carson Spink Named to Team USA for Short Course World Championships

American swimmer Carson Spink has secured a coveted spot on Team USA, poised to compete at the 2024 World Aquatics Swimming Championships (25m), commonly known as the Short Course World Championships. The prestigious international event will be held in Budapest, Hungary, from December 10-15. Spink, known for his prowess in backstroke and butterfly, earned his selection following a rigorous qualification process, marking a significant milestone in his competitive career. His inclusion on the national roster provides a fresh opportunity for him to test his skills against an elite global field in the 25-meter pool format, distinct from the Olympic long course racing.
