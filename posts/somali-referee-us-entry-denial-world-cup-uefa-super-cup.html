Somali Referee Denied US Entry for World Cup Event Despite Officiating UEFA Super Cup

Abdirizak Ali Artan, a highly regarded Somali international football referee, has reportedly been denied a visa to enter the United States for a FIFA World Cup-related event. The denial comes despite his recent appointment to officiate the prestigious UEFA Super Cup, a testament to his elite status in international football. This incident underscores the significant challenges and inconsistencies faced by sports professionals from certain nations in navigating global travel regulations, sparking a broader discussion on the intersection of geopolitics and international sport.
