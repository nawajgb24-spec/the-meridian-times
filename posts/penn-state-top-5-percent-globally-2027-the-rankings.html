### Penn State Secures Top 5% Global Rank in 2027 THE University Report **University Park, PA** — Penn State University has cemented its position among the world's leading academic institutions, securing a coveted spot within the top 5% globally in the newly released 2027 Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings. The achievement underscores the university's consistent excellence across a comprehensive range of performance indicators, signaling its robust standing on the international stage. This distinguished ranking places Penn State in an elite category, recognized for its contributions to education, research, and global engagement. The Times Higher Education rankings are widely considered one of the most influential and closely watched assessments of university performance worldwide, providing a benchmark for academic quality and institutional reputation. ### Background The Times Higher Education World University Rankings is an annual publication that evaluates universities across the globe. Its methodology is based on five core pillars: Teaching (the learning environment), Research (volume, income, and reputation), Citations (research influence), International Outlook (staff, students, and research collaboration), and Industry Income (knowledge transfer). These pillars comprise 13 carefully calibrated performance indicators, designed to provide a comprehensive and balanced comparison. For the 2027 rankings, THE assessed institutions based on a rigorous compilation of data, typically spanning several academic years prior to the publication date. This extensive data collection ensures that the rankings reflect sustained performance rather than short-term fluctuations. Universities self-report data, which is then verified, alongside analyses of academic reputation surveys and citation databases. Penn State, a large public research university with its main campus at University Park, has a long-standing tradition of academic excellence and a significant research footprint. Its historical performance in global rankings has consistently placed it among the top institutions, reflecting its scale, diverse academic offerings, and commitment to impactful research. ### Timeline The journey to a top 5% global ranking is a culmination of years of strategic planning and operational execution.
