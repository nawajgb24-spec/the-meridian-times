UNL's 194 Researchers Named Among World's Most Influential Scientists

The University of Nebraska-Lincoln (UNL) has seen 194 of its affiliated scientists recognized in Stanford University's prestigious list of the world's top 2% most-cited researchers. This significant acknowledgment underscores UNL's growing global research impact, with faculty members demonstrating exceptional influence across a broad spectrum of scientific disciplines through their highly cited publications. The list, compiled by Professor John P.A. Ioannidis and his team, utilizes comprehensive data from Elsevier's Scopus, measuring both career-long and recent annual citation impact. This achievement positions UNL as a significant contributor to international scientific advancement and a hub for leading-edge research.

The University of Nebraska-Lincoln (UNL) has received significant international recognition, with 194 of its affiliated faculty and researchers named among the world's most influential scientists. This prestigious acknowledgment comes from an updated database compiled by Stanford University, which identifies the top 2% of scientists globally based on their career-long and single-year citation impact. The inclusion of such a substantial number of "Huskers," as UNL affiliates are known, underscores the institution's burgeoning influence in global scientific research and its commitment to producing high-impact scholarship across a diverse array of disciplines. The comprehensive database, publicly accessible and widely regarded within the academic community, was developed by Professor John P.A. Ioannidis and his team at Stanford University. It systematically ranks scientists based on a composite indicator that considers several citation metrics, including the total number of citations, h-index, co-authorship adjustments, and a measure of citations from self-citations. This rigorous methodology draws upon an extensive dataset from Elsevier's Scopus, one of the largest abstract and citation databases of peer-reviewed literature. The list aims to provide a standardized, objective measure of scientific influence, moving beyond simple citation counts to account for various factors that contribute to a researcher's overall impact. Scientists are categorized into 22 primary scientific fields and 174 sub-fields, ensuring a nuanced comparison of contributions within specific areas of expertise. Inclusion in this exclusive top 2% is a testament to sustained productivity and the profound influence a researcher's work has had on their respective fields, either over their entire career or specifically within the most recent complete calendar year. The Stanford University database was first conceptualized and released by Professor Ioannidis and his colleagues in 2019. Its initial publication marked a significant step forward in providing a transparent and comprehensive ranking system for scientific impact, quickly becoming a benchmark in academic assessments. Since its inception, the list has undergone periodic updates, typically on an annual basis, to integrate the latest citation data and refine its methodological approach. Each update allows for the dynamic tracking of scientific influence, reflecting new discoveries and the evolving landscape of research. The most recent significant release, which utilized citation data up to the end of 2022, is the specific iteration where 194 UNL-affiliated researchers garnered recognition. This consistent updating ensures the list remains current and relevant, capturing the ongoing contributions of scientists worldwide.
