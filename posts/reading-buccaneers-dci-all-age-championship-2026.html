The DCI All-Age Class represents the evolution of what was formerly known as Drum Corps Associates (DCA), an independent circuit for all-age drum and bugle corps. In 2022, DCA fully integrated into the DCI umbrella, bringing its rich history and passionate community under a unified national structure.
