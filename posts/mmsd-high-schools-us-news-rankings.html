MMSD High Schools Earn Top Marks in U.S. News & World Report Rankings

Six high schools within the Madison Metropolitan School District (MMSD) have received notable recognition in the latest 'Best High Schools' rankings published by U.S. News & World Report. This achievement places multiple MMSD institutions among the top educational providers at both state and national levels, underscoring the district's commitment to academic excellence and student preparedness. The annual report evaluates schools based on comprehensive criteria, including college readiness, student performance on state assessments, and graduation rates, providing a widely recognized benchmark for educational quality.

These factors collectively aim to capture a holistic picture of a school's ability to serve its student population effectively and prepare them for post-secondary opportunities.
