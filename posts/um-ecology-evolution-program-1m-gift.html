Visionary $1 Million Gift Elevates University of Miami's Ecology and Evolution Program

The University of Miami (UM) has announced a significant $1 million gift aimed at bolstering its highly regarded Ecology and Evolution Program. This substantial philanthropic contribution is poised to enhance research capabilities, expand educational opportunities, and reinforce the program's international standing in a critical scientific field. The gift underscores the growing recognition of the program's impact on understanding and addressing pressing global environmental challenges.
