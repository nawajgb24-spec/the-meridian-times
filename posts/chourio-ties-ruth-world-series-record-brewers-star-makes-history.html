### Chourio Joins Ruth in World Series History: Brewers Star Ties Iconic Record **MILWAUKEE, WI** — In a moment that resonated deeply within the annals of Major League Baseball, Milwaukee Brewers outfielder Jackson Chourio etched his name into the sport’s record books during Game 1 of the 2024 World Series. The young phenom recorded his first career World Series hit, an accomplishment made all the more significant by its historical parallel to one of baseball's most legendary figures: Babe Ruth. Chourio's achievement at just 20 years of age places him in an exclusive category, effectively tying a historical benchmark held by Ruth as one of the youngest players ever to register a hit on baseball's grandest stage. This rare feat has immediately sparked conversations about Chourio's prodigious talent and his potential future impact on the game. ### Background Jackson Chourio entered the 2024 MLB season as one of baseball's most highly anticipated prospects. Babe Ruth's own early career trajectory, though from a vastly different era, also featured a precocious World Series appearance. In 1916, Ruth, then primarily a dominant left-handed pitcher for the Boston Red Sox, made his mark on the World Series stage. It was in Game 2 of that series against the Brooklyn Robins that Ruth, at 21 years and 246 days old, registered his first World Series hit. That hit foreshadowed a career that would redefine baseball, first as an ace pitcher and later as an unparalleled slugger. The comparison between Chourio and Ruth, therefore, extends beyond mere statistics, touching upon the rare phenomenon of players making a significant World Series impact at an age when most are still honing their craft in collegiate or minor league circuits.
