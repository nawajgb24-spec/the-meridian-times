## USA's U20 Men's 4x100m Relay Secures Gold, Championship Record in Cali **Cali, Colombia** – The United States U20 Men's 4x100m relay team, propelled by a strong anchor leg from Elijah Taylor, clinched a gold medal and set a new Championship Record of 39.06 seconds at the World Athletics U20 Championships. This landmark performance unfolded in Cali, Colombia, in August 2022, securing a dominant victory for the American quartet. The team, comprising Brandon Miller, Ashton Schwartzman, Matthew Boling, and Elijah Taylor, demonstrated exceptional cohesion and speed against a formidable international field. Held biennially, the competition acts as a crucial stepping stone for athletes aspiring to transition to senior-level international competition, including future Olympic Games and World Athletics Championships. Historically, the United States has maintained a strong presence in youth sprinting and relay events, consistently producing athletes who go on to achieve distinction at the highest levels of the sport. The 4x100m relay, demanding precision in baton exchanges alongside raw speed, often highlights a nation's collective sprinting prowess. The global benchmark for this event, the absolute World U20 Record in the men's 4x100m relay, stands at an impressive 38.62 seconds, also set by a USA team in 2016. The 2022 team's achievement of 39.06 seconds, while not surpassing this ultimate mark, still registered as a Championship Record and the fastest time globally for U20 athletes that season, underscoring its significant caliber. ### Timeline The American campaign in Cali began with confidence and precision. The heats for the men's 4x100m relay took place on August 4, 2022. The USA team navigated their preliminary race successfully, recording a time of 39.54 seconds. This performance demonstrated early form and secured their place in the eagerly anticipated final. The ultimate test arrived on August 5, 2022. Under the intense pressure of a championship final, the USA quartet executed their race with remarkable efficiency and blistering speed. Each leg contributed to the momentum that built towards the climactic finish, setting the stage for Taylor's final sprint. ### Latest Developments In the final, the USA team faced strong competition from Jamaica, Japan, and other nations, all vying for global youth supremacy. Brandon Miller, leading off, established a solid start, quickly followed by Ashton Schwartzman, who maintained the pace through the second leg. Matthew Boling, a widely recognized talent, powered through the third leg, demonstrating his formidable acceleration and setting up the anchor. The crucial final handoff went to Elijah Taylor, who unleashed a powerful closing sprint. Taylor held off his competitors, crossing the finish line in 39.06 seconds to secure the gold medal. This time was officially recognized as a new Championship Record for the World Athletics U20 Championships, surpassing previous marks for the event. Furthermore, it stood as the world U20 leading time for the 2022 outdoor season, solidifying the team's standing as the premier youth relay squad globally for that year.
