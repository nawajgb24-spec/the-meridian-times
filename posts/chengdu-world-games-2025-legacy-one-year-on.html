Chengdu Forges Enduring Legacy One Year Ahead of The World Games 2025

One year before The World Games 2025, Chengdu is actively implementing strategies to ensure a lasting legacy beyond the sporting event. This article details the city's progress in urban development, infrastructure, green initiatives, and smart city integration, highlighting its proactive approach to leveraging the Games for long-term benefit and enhanced global standing.

### Introduction Chengdu, a city in southwest China, is preparing to host The World Games 2025. With approximately one year remaining until the event, the city is intensifying its efforts to build an enduring legacy that extends beyond the competition dates of August 7-17, 2025. This proactive approach aims to integrate the Games into Chengdu's broader urban development strategy, focusing on sustainable growth, enhanced infrastructure, and an elevated international profile. The focus is not merely on hosting a successful sporting event but on utilizing it as a catalyst for long-term city transformation. Chengdu's ambition is to showcase its modernity, cultural richness, and commitment to environmental sustainability on a global stage, ensuring tangible benefits for its residents and future generations. ### Background The World Games are an international multi-sport event, sanctioned by the International Olympic Committee, featuring disciplines not included in the Olympic Games. Chengdu secured the rights to host the 12th edition of The World Games in May 2019, marking a significant milestone for the city and the International World Games Association (IWGA). Hosting such an event offers cities an opportunity to accelerate urban planning and development initiatives. For Chengdu, a rapidly modernizing metropolis known for its pandas, spicy cuisine, and burgeoning technology sector, The World Games 2025 represents a strategic platform to reinforce its 'Parks City' concept and smart city ambitions. ### Timeline The journey to The World Games 2025 began officially with Chengdu's successful bid in May 2019. This marked the start of a six-year preparatory phase, encompassing extensive planning, infrastructure development, and organizational setup. The subsequent years have seen continuous progress in venue construction and renovation, logistical arrangements, and public engagement initiatives. As of mid-2024, Chengdu stands approximately one year out from the opening ceremony. This period is characterized by the finalization of venue readiness, extensive volunteer recruitment and training, and the roll-out of targeted urban improvement projects designed to be completed in time for the Games. ### Latest Developments In the past year, Chengdu has accelerated several key initiatives. Construction and renovation of primary venues, such as Dong'an Lake Sports Park and Phoenix Mountain Sports Park, have progressed significantly, with many facilities nearing completion. These venues are being designed not just for event use but also for post-Games community access and large-scale public engagement. Beyond sports infrastructure, the city has implemented smart city solutions, including advanced transportation management systems and integrated digital platforms aimed at enhancing visitor experience and operational efficiency during the Games. Green urban development continues to be a priority, with new parks and ecological corridors being established across the city, aligning with Chengdu's 'Parks City' vision. ### Analysis Chengdu's strategy for The World Games 2025 is a case study in pre-emptive legacy planning. Rather than solely focusing on event delivery, the city has strategically interwoven the Games into its long-term development agenda. This approach positions the event not as a standalone spectacle but as an accelerator for existing urban goals, particularly in sustainability, livability, and international connectivity. The investment in infrastructure, smart technology, and green spaces is designed to yield dividends for decades, improving the quality of life for residents and enhancing the city's appeal to businesses and tourists. The emphasis on a 'Parks City' and smart urban governance demonstrates a commitment to modern, sustainable development principles. ### Public Response The local population in Chengdu has shown considerable engagement with the upcoming Games. There has been an enthusiastic response to calls for volunteers, reflecting civic pride and a desire to contribute to the city's international showcase. Local media campaigns and community events have helped to foster a sense of anticipation and collective ownership. Residents have also expressed positive views regarding the urban improvements initiated in preparation for the Games, particularly the expansion of green spaces and upgrades to public facilities. The opportunity to host a major international event is widely seen as a chance to elevate Chengdu's global standing and promote local culture. ### Industry Impact The World Games 2025 are already generating significant economic activity. The construction sector has seen a boom due to venue development and urban renewal projects. The hospitality and tourism industries are preparing for an influx of international visitors, leading to investments in hotels, restaurants, and related services. Furthermore, the technology sector is benefiting from the push for smart city solutions and digital infrastructure required for event management and seamless urban operation. The Games also provide a platform for local businesses to engage with international partners and explore new market opportunities, contributing to a diversified economic landscape. ### Future Outlook Looking beyond 2025, Chengdu aims to solidify its position as a hub for sports, culture, and innovation. The completed venues are planned for conversion into multi-functional facilities, serving both elite sports training and broader community recreation. The expanded green infrastructure will continue to enhance urban livability and environmental quality. Lessons learned from hosting The World Games, particularly in smart city management and sustainable event planning, are expected to inform future urban development projects. The enhanced international profile and experience in global event hosting are anticipated to attract further international collaborations, investments, and tourism, ensuring the legacy extends far into the future. ### Conclusion Chengdu's journey towards The World Games 2025 is characterized by a strategic, forward-looking approach to legacy building. One year out, the city's commitment to urban transformation, technological integration, and environmental stewardship is evident. The Games are serving as a powerful catalyst, driving development that is set to benefit Chengdu's residents and its international standing long after the final medal is awarded. The proactive integration of the event into the city's long-term vision demonstrates an understanding that true legacy is built well before the spotlight fades, ensuring a vibrant and sustainable future for this dynamic Chinese metropolis.
