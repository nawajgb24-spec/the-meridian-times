Complete New World Translation Released in Polish Sign Language

Jehovah's Witnesses have completed the New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures in Polish Sign Language (PJM), making the entire Bible accessible to deaf individuals in Poland. This landmark achievement, officially released in July 2023, culminates years of dedicated translation work aimed at converting complex biblical concepts into a native visual language. The initiative underscores a global commitment to linguistic diversity and religious accessibility for the deaf community, setting a precedent for comprehensive sign language scriptural resources.

## Complete New World Translation of Holy Scriptures Released in Polish Sign Language **WARSAW, Poland** – The entire New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures has been released in Polish Sign Language (PJM), marking a significant milestone for the deaf community in Poland. This comprehensive translation, produced by Jehovah's Witnesses, makes the full text of the Bible accessible to individuals who communicate using Polish Sign Language, a distinct visual language with its own grammar and syntax. The completion of this monumental project, officially finalized in July 2023, represents years of meticulous effort by dedicated translation teams. It underscores a global commitment to linguistic diversity and religious accessibility, providing PJM users with direct access to a complete biblical text in their native language. ### Background on the Translation The New World Translation (NWT) is a Bible translation published by the Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society of Pennsylvania, the legal entity used by Jehovah's Witnesses. First released in English in phases starting in 1950, with the complete Bible in 1961, the NWT is known for its focus on a literal translation approach and its use of the divine name, Jehovah. Since its initial English release, it has been translated into hundreds of languages worldwide. For decades, Jehovah's Witnesses have prioritized the translation of their religious literature, including the Bible, into a vast array of languages, encompassing both spoken and sign languages. This global effort is rooted in their belief that everyone should have the opportunity to understand the scriptures in their own tongue, fostering spiritual growth and personal study. Translating the Bible into a sign language presents unique challenges compared to translating into a written or spoken language. It involves converting abstract concepts, poetic expressions, and historical narratives not merely word-for-word, but concept-for-concept, into a visually dynamic format. This requires a profound understanding of both the ancient biblical texts and the intricate nuances of the target sign language. ### Project Timeline The journey to a complete Polish Sign Language Bible has been a progressive one, reflecting a long-term strategic endeavor by Jehovah's Witnesses. While the official completion and release of the full New World Translation in PJM occurred in July 2023, parts of the Bible and other religious publications have been available in PJM for many years prior. Initial efforts focused on translating smaller publications, tracts, and sections of the Bible, such as individual books of the Christian Greek Scriptures (New Testament) or portions of the Hebrew Scriptures (Old Testament). These incremental releases allowed the PJM-speaking deaf community to gradually access religious material in their native language, building anticipation for the complete work. Specialized translation teams, often comprising both deaf and hearing individuals fluent in Polish Sign Language, worked diligently on the project. The process involved extensive research, linguistic analysis, and numerous revisions to ensure accuracy, clarity, and naturalness within the PJM context. The culmination of these efforts in July 2023 marked the historic availability of the complete NWT in this vital sign language. ### Latest Developments: July 2023 Release The formal announcement of the complete New World Translation in Polish Sign Language took place through an online program in July 2023. This event gathered deaf individuals and their families from across Poland, as well as an international audience, to celebrate the achievement. The digital nature of the release allowed for immediate global dissemination. Unlike printed Bibles, sign language translations are primarily distributed in video format. This ensures that the visual nature of PJM, including facial expressions, body language, and spatial grammar, is fully conveyed. The entire NWT in PJM is accessible through digital platforms provided by Jehovah's Witnesses, including their official website and the 'JW Library Sign Language' app. This release not only includes the scriptural text but also incorporates features designed to aid study, such as introductions to Bible books, cross-references, and an index of Bible words. These supplementary materials are also translated into PJM, offering a comprehensive study tool for deaf individuals. ### Analysis of Significance The release of the complete NWT in Polish Sign Language represents more than just a translation; it is an act of linguistic empowerment and religious inclusion. For the deaf community, access to information in their native sign language is fundamental to full participation in society. Religious texts, often rich in complex narratives and theological concepts, have historically been difficult to access for deaf individuals relying solely on interpreted spoken language or written texts that may not align with their primary language modality. This PJM Bible provides an authentic, direct, and culturally resonant way for deaf individuals to engage with the scriptures. It helps bridge the communication gap that often exists between deaf and hearing communities, facilitating independent study and deeper spiritual understanding. The dedication required for such a large-scale sign language translation highlights the significant resources and commitment invested by the publishing organization. ### Public Response and Community Impact The response from the deaf community in Poland and beyond has been overwhelmingly positive. Many individuals have expressed profound gratitude and emotional relief at receiving the entire Bible in their native sign language. Testimonials often highlight the sense of connection and clarity that comes from understanding complex spiritual messages without linguistic barriers. For deaf congregations and study groups of Jehovah's Witnesses in Poland, this translation provides a unified and authoritative resource for communal worship and private meditation. It fosters a stronger sense of identity and belonging within their faith community, enabling more profound discussions and shared learning experiences. The accessibility helps to address long-standing challenges of spiritual education for deaf individuals. ### Industry Impact and Precedent While specific 'industry' metrics for religious sign language translations are not widely tracked, this release sets an important precedent within the broader field of Bible translation and religious publishing. It underscores the growing recognition of sign languages as full and complete languages, deserving of comprehensive scriptural resources. Jehovah's Witnesses have been at the forefront of sign language Bible translation, having produced numerous complete or partial Bibles in other sign languages globally. This PJM release further solidifies their position as a leading organization in this specialized area. It may encourage other religious bodies and linguistic organizations to invest more significantly in similar comprehensive sign language projects, recognizing the vast spiritual and educational needs of deaf populations worldwide. ### Future Outlook The completion of the Polish Sign Language Bible is not an end but a new beginning for the PJM-speaking deaf community. It is expected to facilitate deeper personal study, more effective religious instruction, and greater engagement with biblical teachings. The ongoing support and distribution through digital platforms ensure its continued accessibility and relevance. Looking ahead, the commitment to providing accessible religious content in sign languages is likely to continue. While the complete Bible is now available, supplementary study aids, commentaries, and educational videos in PJM are expected to be produced and updated regularly. This ensures that the deaf community not only has the text but also the tools to understand and apply it in their daily lives. ### Conclusion The release of the complete New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures in Polish Sign Language marks a monumental achievement in religious translation and accessibility. By providing the entire Bible in a native, visual format, Jehovah's Witnesses have empowered the PJM-speaking deaf community with a direct and profound connection to the scriptures. This landmark effort reflects a deep understanding of linguistic diversity and a steadfast commitment to inclusivity, setting a standard for future sign language translation projects worldwide.
