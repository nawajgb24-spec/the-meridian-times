Australia Edges New Zealand in Gripping World Cup Semi-Final Showdown

The 2022 Rugby League World Cup semi-final between Australia and New Zealand delivered an intense and memorable contest, culminating in a narrow 16-14 victory for the Kangaroos. Broadcast live to a global audience, the match at Elland Road cemented its place as a classic encounter in the storied rivalry between the two rugby league powerhouses. This article delves into the game's context, the immediate aftermath, tactical insights, and its lasting impact on both nations and the sport.

### Introduction The 2022 Rugby League World Cup semi-final clash between Australia and New Zealand proved to be one of the tournament's most captivating encounters. On November 11, 2022, at Elland Road in Leeds, England, the two rugby league giants delivered a fierce contest that saw Australia emerge victorious with a narrow 16-14 scoreline. The match, broadcast live to millions worldwide, underscored the enduring rivalry and the high stakes of international rugby league. This pivotal knockout game not only determined a finalist but also provided a dramatic display of skill, resilience, and tactical prowess from both the Kangaroos and the Kiwis. This set them on a collision course for the semi-finals. On November 4, 2022, both teams successfully progressed through their quarter-final fixtures, Australia overcoming Lebanon and New Zealand defeating Fiji. This confirmed the semi-final pairing at Elland Road. The match itself, on November 11, unfolded with a tense first half, seeing both teams exchange tries and penalties.
