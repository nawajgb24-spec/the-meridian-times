Meridian's Sarah Jenkins Set to Compete at Bench Press World Championships

Meridian powerlifter Sarah Jenkins is preparing to represent her city and country at the International Powerlifting Federation (IPF) Bench Press World Championships in Pilsen, Czech Republic, this October. Jenkins, a celebrated local athlete and national champion in the Women's 76 kg category, carries the hopes of her community as she vies for an international title. This article details her journey, rigorous preparation, the significant local support, and the broader implications of her participation for the sport of powerlifting.
