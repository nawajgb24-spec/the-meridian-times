Terry Stotts' Crucial Role in USA Basketball's FIBA World Cup Qualification

Terry Stotts, a respected NBA coaching veteran, brought his extensive experience to the forefront as an assistant coach for the U.S. Men's National Team during the challenging 2017-2019 FIBA World Cup Qualifiers. Working alongside head coach Jeff Van Gundy, Stotts played a vital role in guiding a roster largely composed of G-League and international professionals through a rigorous qualification schedule. This article examines his contribution, the unique challenges faced, and the broader implications of NBA coaching talent contributing to international basketball's foundational stages.
