These two nations have historically contested the majority of championship finals, fostering an intense and compelling rivalry that captivates lacrosse enthusiasts worldwide. This expansion has led to more diverse and exciting tournament play. ### Timeline The history of the World Lacrosse Championship began in 1967 with the inaugural tournament in Toronto, Canada.
