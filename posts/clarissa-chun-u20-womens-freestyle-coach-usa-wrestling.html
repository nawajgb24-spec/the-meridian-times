Clarissa Chun, a highly decorated Olympic medalist and respected coach, has been officially named the Women's Freestyle Coach for the U.S. team competing at the upcoming U20 World Championships. Her appointment by USA Wrestling signifies a strategic move to bolster the development of emerging talent within women's wrestling. Chun brings a wealth of competitive experience and a proven track record as a mentor, poised to guide the nation's top young female athletes on the international stage.
