Jaydon Stegall Secures Silver for U.S. at World Athletics U20 Championships

Jaydon Stegall, representing the United States, played a pivotal role in the men's 4x100m relay team that secured a silver medal at the 2022 World Athletics U20 Championships in Cali, Colombia. This achievement underscores his growing prominence in junior athletics and contributes to the U.S. team's strong performance on the international stage. The U.S. squad finished with a time of 39.33 seconds, narrowly missing gold to the Japanese team.
