It has historically served as a launching pad for numerous footballing legends, including Diego Maradona, Lionel Messi, and Paul Pogba, who all garnered attention during their U-20 World Cup campaigns. Participating teams qualify through continental youth championships, ensuring that only the strongest national youth sides earn a spot. The tournament's structure, involving group stages followed by knockout rounds, mirrors that of the senior FIFA World Cup, preparing players for the rigours of top-tier international football. Its mandate is not only to crown a world champion at the U-20 level but also to foster the growth and development of football globally by highlighting promising talent. ### Timeline The typical timeline for a FIFA U-20 World Cup cycle begins well in advance of the main tournament.
