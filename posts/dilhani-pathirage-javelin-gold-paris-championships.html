Pathirage's Historic Javelin Gold: A Triumph in Paris, Not Budapest

Sri Lankan para-athlete Dilhani Lahandapurage Pathirage has etched her name into history with a groundbreaking gold medal in the Women's Javelin Throw F46 event.
