Illini Bluffs Star Sarah Althaus Commits to Valparaiso Volleyball Program

Sarah Althaus, a standout middle blocker from Illini Bluffs High School, has formally committed to the Valparaiso University women's volleyball program, an NCAA Division I team within the Missouri Valley Conference. This commitment, formalized in November 2022, marks a significant athletic and academic achievement for the central Illinois athlete, signaling her transition to collegiate-level competition. Her journey highlights the recruitment pathways for talented athletes from smaller high school programs into highly competitive university sports.

Glasford, Illinois – Sarah Althaus, a prominent middle blocker from Illini Bluffs High School, has officially committed to the Valparaiso University women's volleyball program. The commitment signifies Althaus's transition to NCAA Division I collegiate athletics within the competitive Missouri Valley Conference (MVC), marking a notable achievement for both the athlete and her high school program. Althaus formalized her commitment in November 2022 by signing her National Letter of Intent, securing her place as part of Valparaiso's 2023 recruiting class. This decision culminates years of dedication to the sport and positions her to compete at one of the highest levels of collegiate volleyball. Throughout her career at Illini Bluffs High School, Sarah Althaus established herself as a dominant force at the net. Standing at a reported 6 feet 2 inches, her athletic frame and skill set allowed her to excel both defensively as a blocker and offensively as a hitter. She consistently led her team in key statistical categories, including kills and blocks, demonstrating her foundational importance to the team's success. Althaus garnered significant individual recognition during her high school tenure. She was a multi-year selection for the Prairieland Conference All-Conference team and received various All-Area accolades from regional media outlets. Her consistent performance contributed to Illini Bluffs' competitive seasons, often leading them deep into IHSA regional tournament play. Her journey to Division I commitment began with early exposure to club volleyball, where she honed her skills against high-level competition and attracted the attention of collegiate scouts. The recruitment process involved evaluations, campus visits, and discussions with coaching staff from multiple institutions before she ultimately decided on Valparaiso University. Her official signing day in November 2022 represented a pivotal moment, affirming her verbal commitment and solidifying her future with the Valparaiso Beacons. Following her graduation from Illini Bluffs High School in Spring 2023, Althaus matriculated to Valparaiso University, ready to begin her collegiate athletic and academic pursuits in the Fall 2023 semester. In her freshman season at Valparaiso University (2023), Althaus saw her first collegiate action. She appeared in 13 matches and played in a total of 18 sets. During these appearances, she recorded 4 kills and contributed 3 blocks, indicating her initial integration into the team's system and gaining valuable experience at the Division I level. Her early contributions suggest a foundational role as she continues to develop within the program. Althaus brings a significant physical presence and a developing skill set to the Valparaiso Beacons. Her height and reach are assets at the middle blocker position, crucial for both stopping opposing attacks and providing an offensive option. Her transition to the faster pace and higher intensity of Division I volleyball is an ongoing process, typical for many freshmen at this level. Valparaiso University’s volleyball program, under Head Coach Jolene Haney, has a history of competitiveness within the Missouri Valley Conference. The MVC is recognized as a strong mid-major conference for volleyball, featuring teams that frequently contend for NCAA Tournament berths. Althaus's addition is part of the program's strategy to maintain and enhance its position within this challenging league. The commitment generated considerable positive sentiment within the Illini Bluffs community. Local news outlets covered her signing, and school officials, coaches, and teammates expressed pride in her accomplishment.
