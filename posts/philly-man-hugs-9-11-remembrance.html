Philly Man Honors 9/11 Friend with Enduring Hugs

Every September 11th, Philadelphia resident Glenn Murphy takes to the streets, offering free hugs to strangers. This heartfelt gesture is his annual tribute to his friend, firefighter Anthony Raffa, who perished in the North Tower collapse during the 2001 terrorist attacks. What began as a personal act of remembrance has evolved into a widely recognized tradition, embodying a message of human connection, comfort, and resilience in the face of profound loss. Murphy's consistent presence has offered solace to countless individuals, transforming a day of collective grief into an opportunity for unexpected kindness and shared humanity, perpetuating the legacy of his friend through simple yet powerful acts of compassion.

Glenn Murphy, a local resident, stands offering free hugs to strangers, his gesture a poignant memorial to his friend, Anthony Raffa, a firefighter who lost his life in the World Trade Center collapse in 2001. This annual act of kindness has transcended a personal tribute, becoming a symbol of solace and connection for many navigating the enduring legacy of that tragic day. Murphy's presence, often accompanied by a simple sign explaining his purpose, invites a moment of shared humanity. His initiative seeks to replace the feelings of grief and isolation associated with 9/11 with an embrace of community and compassion, embodying a unique form of remembrance that prioritizes human interaction and mutual support. ### Background: A Friendship Forged, A Legacy Honored Glenn Murphy and Anthony Raffa shared a bond that predated the devastating events of September 11, 2001. His bravery, like that of many others, ultimately cost him his life when the North Tower collapsed. Murphy recounts the profound impact of Raffa's death, describing the deep personal grief and the collective shock that rippled across the nation. In the years following the attacks, he grappled with how to best honor his friend's memory, seeking an act that would not only remember Raffa's sacrifice but also inject a positive, connecting force into a day often overshadowed by sorrow. ### Timeline: From Personal Grief to Public Embrace **September 11, 2001:** Anthony Raffa, 39, perishes in the line of duty at the World Trade Center.
