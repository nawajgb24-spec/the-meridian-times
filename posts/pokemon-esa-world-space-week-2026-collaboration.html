World Space Week, an annual international event designated by the United Nations General Assembly, is observed from October 4th to 10th. It commemorates key milestones in space exploration: the launch of the first artificial satellite, Sputnik 1, on October 4, 1957, and the signing of the Outer Space Treaty on October 10, 1967. The week-long celebration aims to educate and inspire the public about the benefits of space science and technology for humankind. The European Space Agency, an independent intergovernmental organisation, has been at the forefront of European space research and development since its establishment in 1975. ESA’s extensive portfolio includes missions in Earth observation, space science, human spaceflight, launch services, navigation, and technology development. A cornerstone of its mission is public engagement, particularly in promoting science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) education to cultivate future generations of innovators. Pokémon, a global entertainment phenomenon since its debut in 1996, encompasses video games, trading card games, animated series, films, and merchandise. Managed internationally by The Pokémon Company International, the franchise is renowned for its widespread appeal across diverse age groups and cultures. Beyond its entertainment value, Pokémon has often been associated with educational themes, encouraging exploration, discovery, and problem-solving within its fictional universe. Historically, The Pokémon Company International has engaged in various partnerships aimed at public outreach and education, aligning with its brand values of adventure and wonder.
