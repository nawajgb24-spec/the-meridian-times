World Cup Final Kick-Off Delayed by Extended Pre-Match Ceremony

The highly anticipated 2022 FIFA World Cup final between Argentina and France experienced a notable delay in its scheduled kick-off, primarily due to an elaborate and extended closing ceremony. Held at Lusail Stadium in Qatar, the pre-match spectacle featured a host of international musical performances, designed to encapsulate the tournament's themes and cultural diversity. While the event captivated many, its prolonged duration pushed back the start of the final match. Contrary to some speculation, official reports did not confirm the involvement of actor Tom Cruise in any speech or performance at the ceremony, with the delay solely attributed to the comprehensive artistic program.
