Bayonne Secures Little League World Series Berth After Metro Region Victory

Bayonne, New Jersey's Little League team has earned a coveted spot in the Little League World Series in Williamsport, Pennsylvania, after triumphing over Darien, Connecticut, in the Metro Region Tournament final. The historic 4-1 victory on August 4, 2023, marks Bayonne as the first team from New Jersey to claim the Metro Region title since its inception in 2022. This achievement sparks community pride and sets the stage for an anticipated national appearance in youth baseball's premier event.

Bayonne, New Jersey, has etched its name into the annals of youth baseball, securing a coveted berth in the Little League World Series. The Bayonne Little League team achieved this distinction by defeating Darien, Connecticut, in a hard-fought Metro Region Tournament championship game. The 4-1 victory, which took place on August 4, 2023, in Bristol, Connecticut, propels the New Jersey squad to Williamsport, Pennsylvania, where they will compete on a global stage. The Little League World Series, an annual baseball tournament held in Williamsport, Pennsylvania, brings together teams of 10-to-12-year-old boys from around the world. Established in 1947, it is one of the most recognized and celebrated youth sporting events globally, capturing national and international attention each summer. Teams qualify for the World Series by advancing through district, sectional, state, and then regional tournaments. The United States is divided into multiple regions, each sending its champion to Williamsport. The international bracket features champions from various countries and continents. The Metro Region is one of the newest additions to the Little League regional structure, having been established in 2022. It comprises champions from New Jersey, New York, and Connecticut. Its creation expanded the U.S. bracket from eight to ten teams, allowing more states to have dedicated paths to Williamsport.
