Kiwis Star Jahrome Hughes Driven by 2022 World Cup Heartbreak

New Zealand's premier halfback, Jahrome Hughes, has voiced his profound determination to atone for the Kiwis' 2022 Rugby League World Cup semi-final exit. The Melbourne Storm playmaker’s resolve to secure international silverware for his nation highlights the lingering impact of that narrow defeat and underscores a renewed commitment within the New Zealand camp to chase future glory on the world stage.

## Kiwis Star Jahrome Hughes Driven by 2022 World Cup Heartbreak A palpable sense of unfinished business continues to motivate Jahrome Hughes, the dynamic halfback for the New Zealand Kiwis rugby league team. His resolve to ‘make amends’ now serves as a powerful personal and collective catalyst for the national side. The 27-year-old playmaker, a linchpin for both the Kiwis and his club, the Melbourne Storm, carries the memory of that campaign with a professional intensity. Under the guidance of then-coach Michael Maguire, the team boasted a formidable roster of NRL stars, including Hughes, aiming to challenge the dominance of their trans-Tasman rivals, Australia, and hosts England. New Zealand’s journey through the group stages was marked by strong performances, showcasing a blend of attacking flair and defensive resilience. They navigated their way to the knockout rounds with considerable confidence, building momentum and generating significant belief among their passionate fanbase both at home and abroad. The quarter-final saw the Kiwis overcome a spirited Fiji side, a victory that set the stage for a highly anticipated semi-final clash against the Australian Kangaroos.
