Duplantis, Jefferson-Wooden, Sawe Headline 2026 World Athletics Athlete of the Year Nominees

World Athletics has announced that pole vault sensation Mondo Duplantis, sprint specialist Melissa Jefferson-Wooden, and long-distance formidable Sabastian Sawe will headline the nominees for the prestigious 2026 Athlete of the Year awards. This early recognition underscores their consistent dominance and groundbreaking performances across various disciplines, positioning them as leading contenders for the sport's highest individual honor. The nominations highlight a diverse array of athletic excellence, promising an exciting and competitive awards season for global track and field.

The global athletics community is abuzz following the announcement that three of the sport’s most compelling figures—Mondo Duplantis, Melissa Jefferson-Wooden, and Sabastian Sawe—will headline the nominees for the 2026 World Athletics Athlete of the Year awards. This prestigious recognition places the Swedish pole vault maestro, the American sprint sensation, and the Kenyan long-distance formidable at the forefront of the quest for track and field’s ultimate individual accolade. The early unveiling of these athletes as key contenders for the 2026 honors underscores their sustained excellence and impact within their respective disciplines. Each has demonstrated a remarkable ability to consistently perform at the highest level, breaking records, securing major titles, and captivating audiences worldwide. Their inclusion signals another year of compelling competition and athletic achievement. The World Athletics Athlete of the Year award is widely regarded as the most esteemed individual prize in track and field. Established in 1988, it celebrates athletes who have delivered the most outstanding performances throughout the competitive season, considering factors such as world records, major championship victories, and overall consistency. Past recipients represent a pantheon of athletics legends, solidifying the award’s significance in the sport’s history. The selection process typically begins with a longlist of nominated athletes, compiled by an international panel of athletics experts. This list is then subject to a three-way voting process, involving the World Athletics Council, the World Athletics Family (member federations, committee members, meeting directors, athlete representatives, etc.), and the general public. Each group’s vote carries a specific weight, leading to a shortlist of finalists before the ultimate winners—one male and one female—are announced. Historically, the nomination cycle commences in late summer or early autumn, following the conclusion of the major championship season. For the 2026 awards, this initial headlining announcement sets an early tone, indicating strong performances by these athletes during the 2026 competitive calendar. A full list of nominees, comprising both male and female athletes across various disciplines, is anticipated to be released in the coming months, followed by the shortlisting and voting phases, culminating in the awards ceremony in December 2026.
