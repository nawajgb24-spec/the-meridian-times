Race Across the World Star Jo Gardiner Dies Year After Son Alan

Jo Gardiner, who captivated audiences on BBC's 'Race Across the World' alongside her son Alan, has died at the age of 72, approximately one year after Alan's passing from pancreatic cancer. The mother-son duo's journey on the 2023 series highlighted their enduring bond and resilience, leaving a significant impression on viewers. Their story, marked by Alan's terminal diagnosis after filming, resonated widely, and tributes have poured in following Jo's death.

The mother-son duo garnered widespread affection during their appearance on the 2023 edition of the popular competition, captivating viewers with their resilience and profound bond. ### Background Jo and Alan Gardiner became household names after featuring in the third series of 'Race Across the World', which aired in March 2023. The show sees pairs navigate thousands of miles across various continents without the use of air travel, credit cards, or mobile phones, relying solely on a cash budget equivalent to airfare for the same journey. Their adventure took them across Canada, where they demonstrated a remarkable partnership and determination. Their participation took on a poignant dimension when it was revealed that Alan had been diagnosed with pancreatic cancer shortly after filming concluded. Despite his deteriorating health, Alan was determined for the series to be broadcast, sharing their story with the public. His passing in February 2023, just weeks before the show began airing, added a layer of profound sadness to their inspiring on-screen journey. ### Timeline Filming for the third series of 'Race Across the World' occurred in 2022. The family shared the news, prompting a renewed wave of public mourning and remembrance. ### Latest Developments Jo Gardiner's death was confirmed by her family, who issued a statement acknowledging her passing and celebrating her life.
