Idaho 7th Grader Sets World Record with Monumental Sturgeon Catch

Thirteen-year-old Jayce Fickle from Idaho has achieved a world record, officially recognized by the International Game Fish Association (IGFA), for catching a white sturgeon measuring 9 feet, 11 inches. The monumental catch, made in May 2023 on the Snake River, places Fickle in the angling history books as a junior angler and underscores the region's robust conservation efforts for the ancient species.

### Introduction In a remarkable display of angling prowess, 13-year-old Jayce Fickle, then a 7th grader from Idaho, has been officially recognized by the International Game Fish Association (IGFA) for setting a world record. Fickle’s catch of a massive white sturgeon, measuring an astonishing 9 feet, 11 inches, occurred in May 2023 on the famed Snake River. This achievement marks a significant milestone in both Fickle's young life and the annals of sport fishing. The colossal freshwater fish, estimated to weigh over 500 pounds, represents a new benchmark for junior anglers globally. The record-setting event highlights the unique opportunities available in the Pacific Northwest for encountering ancient and formidable aquatic species, while also drawing attention to critical conservation practices. ### Background Jayce Fickle, like many children in Idaho, grew up with a passion for the outdoors and fishing. His family often embarked on angling excursions, fostering his skills and appreciation for the natural environment of the region. The Snake River, particularly within the deep canyons of Hells Canyon, is renowned for its population of white sturgeon, North America's largest freshwater fish. These ancient fish can live for over 100 years and grow to immense sizes, making them a sought-after challenge for experienced anglers. Strict catch-and-release regulations are in place to protect the species, which has faced historical population declines. Anglers pursuing sturgeon in these waters often rely on expert guides familiar with the river's currents and the sturgeon's habits. ### Timeline **May 28, 2023:** Jayce Fickle, accompanied by his father and guided by Joe Fredericks of Hammer Down Excursions, set out on a fishing trip on the Snake River in Hells Canyon. The group was specifically targeting white sturgeon, a species known for its formidable fighting capabilities. **Late Morning, May 28, 2023:** Jayce hooked into what immediately felt like a substantial fish. The fight commenced, described by those present as an intense and prolonged battle requiring considerable strength and determination from the young angler. **Approximately 30 Minutes Later:** After a strenuous struggle, Fickle managed to bring the colossal sturgeon alongside the boat. Guide Joe Fredericks and Jayce's father worked together to secure the fish for measurement and photography, ensuring its safety and minimizing stress. **Post-Catch:** With precise measurements taken and photographic evidence gathered, the sturgeon was carefully released back into the Snake River, adhering to the mandatory catch-and-release protocols essential for the species' conservation. The length of 9 feet, 11 inches was immediately recognized as exceptional. **Following Weeks:** The necessary documentation, including witness statements, photographs, and the IGFA’s specific forms, were meticulously prepared and submitted to the International Game Fish Association for official world record consideration. **Late 2023:** After a thorough review process, the IGFA officially certified Jayce Fickle's catch, recognizing it as a new Junior Angler All-Tackle Length world record for white sturgeon. It was also noted as potentially the longest white sturgeon ever recorded by the IGFA for length in any category. ### Latest Developments Since the official certification by the IGFA, Jayce Fickle's achievement has garnered significant attention from the angling community and mainstream media. His story has been featured in various outdoor publications and news outlets, celebrating his accomplishment and inspiring young anglers worldwide. The IGFA's recognition solidifies his place in fishing history, particularly given the size and the species involved. The organization's rigorous verification process ensures that all record claims meet stringent criteria, adding weight to Fickle's extraordinary catch. His achievement serves as a testament to both his skill and the health of the Snake River's sturgeon population. ### Analysis Fickle's record-breaking catch is significant on multiple fronts. For a junior angler to land a fish of this magnitude underscores not only personal skill but also the increasing accessibility of advanced fishing techniques and equipment. White sturgeon are notorious for their power and endurance, making any successful catch a triumph, let alone one of this size by a young individual. Furthermore, the achievement highlights the success of long-term conservation efforts for white sturgeon. Decades of protective measures, including catch-and-release mandates and habitat preservation, have allowed these impressive fish to thrive and reach such extraordinary sizes. The presence of record-sized sturgeon indicates a healthy ecosystem capable of sustaining top predators. ### Public Response The public response to Jayce Fickle's world record has been overwhelmingly positive. Local communities in Idaho have also shown immense pride in Fickle's representation of their state's natural heritage. ### Industry Impact Jayce Fickle's record catch could have several positive impacts on the sport fishing industry, particularly in regions known for sturgeon fishing like Idaho and the Pacific Northwest.
