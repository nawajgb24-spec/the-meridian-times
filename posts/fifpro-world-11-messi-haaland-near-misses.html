FIFPRO World 11: Records, Debuts, and Close Contenders in Elite Selection

The 2023 FIFA FIFPRO World 11 selections have once again highlighted the pinnacle of global football talent, as chosen by thousands of professional peers. Lionel Messi extended his record for consecutive inclusions, while newcomers like Erling Haaland and Aitana Bonmatí marked their debut. The exclusive nature of the award means specific vote details are not disclosed, but an examination of the selections and prominent absentees provides insight into who garnered significant support and who narrowly missed a coveted spot among the world's elite.

## FIFPRO World 11: Records, Debuts, and Close Contenders in Elite Selection London, UK – The football world recently turned its attention to The Best FIFA Football Awards 2023, where the eagerly anticipated FIFA FIFPRO World 11 for both men and women were unveiled. This unique accolade, decided by the votes of professional footballers worldwide, represents the ultimate peer recognition in the sport. The selections for 2023 once again showcased a blend of established legends and rising stars, sparking considerable discussion about who secured the most votes and which exceptional talents found themselves just outside the chosen elite. The FIFPRO World 11 stands as a distinctive honour, differing from other individual awards by placing the selection entirely in the hands of active professional players. It is an acknowledgment by one's peers, often regarded as one of the most prestigious team selections in global football. For the 2023 edition, thousands of male and female professional footballers from across the globe cast their ballots, selecting their preferred eleven players based on performances between August 19, 2022, and August 20, 2023. The genesis of the FIFPRO World 11 dates back to 2005. Initially established by FIFPRO, the global union for professional football players, the award gained further prominence in 2015 when it became an integral part of The Best FIFA Football Awards. This integration solidified its status as a benchmark for excellence, presented alongside other major FIFA individual honours. The consistent annual presentation of this award provides a unique chronological record of the sport's most dominant performers. The 2023 selections, announced on January 15, 2024, in London, brought several significant developments. For the men, Lionel Messi made history with his 17th consecutive inclusion, a testament to his sustained dominance at the highest level. He was joined by debutants Erling Haaland, Jude Bellingham, and Vinicius Jr., signaling a generational shift alongside stalwarts like Kevin De Bruyne and Kylian Mbappé. The Men's World 11 was predominantly composed of players from Manchester City, reflecting their treble-winning season. On the women's side, the 2023 World 11 saw first-time inclusions for European champions Aitana Bonmatí, Mary Earps, Alex Greenwood, and Lauren James. Bonmatí's inclusion was widely anticipated following her stellar season, which also saw her win the Ballon d'Or Feminin. The Women's World 11 featured a strong contingent from England's Lionesses, indicative of their performance at the FIFA Women's World Cup. These debutants highlight the evolving landscape of women's football and the emergence of new top-tier talents. While FIFPRO does not release detailed vote counts for individual players, the consistent selection of figures like Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo (in previous years), and now Kevin De Bruyne, suggests they frequently receive a significant proportion of the votes in their respective positions. Their sustained elite performance year after year cements their status as players widely considered by their peers to be among the very best. The inclusion of three Manchester City defenders and Rodri in the Men's XI, all critical to their team's success, indicates strong peer recognition for their collective and individual contributions. The question of who 'narrowly missed out' inevitably arises with an award of this calibre. Without public vote tallies, assessing those on the cusp relies on observing players who had exceptional seasons but did not secure a place. For instance, in the men's category, players like Bukayo Saka, Antoine Griezmann, or Julian Alvarez, all of whom had highly impactful seasons for club and country, were considered strong contenders by many. In defence, figures like Ronald Araújo or Eder Militão, despite strong individual performances, faced stiff competition from the dominant Manchester City quartet. Similarly, for the women, players such as Kadidiatou Diani, Fridolina Rolfö, or Debinha, who were pivotal for their clubs and national teams, were part of the wider conversation but ultimately not selected, demonstrating the immense depth of talent at the elite level. The public response to the 2023 World 11 selections was, as always, a mix of affirmation and debate. Social media platforms and sports forums buzzed with discussions regarding the inclusions, particularly the record-breaking appearance of Messi and the strong representation from Manchester City. While many lauded the selections as fair reflections of the season's outstanding performers, some critics pointed to specific omissions, arguing for other players who they believed deserved a spot based on their contributions. This discourse underscores the subjective nature of such awards, even when decided by peers. Being named in the FIFPRO World 11 carries significant industry impact. For players, it solidifies their standing as a global elite, enhancing their marketability, sponsorship appeal, and overall career trajectory. Clubs with multiple players in the World 11 benefit from increased prestige and a perception of housing world-class talent, which can aid in attracting further talent and commercial partnerships. The award serves as a powerful endorsement of a player's quality and influence, recognised by the very individuals who understand the game best. Looking ahead, the 2023 selections offer glimpses into the future landscape of elite football. The emergence of young talents like Jude Bellingham and Lauren James indicates a changing guard, while the consistent presence of veterans like Messi underscores the longevity of true greatness. Future iterations of the World 11 will likely see continued evolution, with new stars emerging and established figures striving to maintain their places. The ongoing global expansion of football, particularly in the women's game, suggests an ever-widening pool of talent for professional players to consider in their votes. In conclusion, the 2023 FIFA FIFPRO World 11 once again delivered a definitive statement on the premier talents in men's and women's football. It is a testament to the hard work, skill, and dedication of those who grace the pitches globally, as judged by their fellow professionals. While the exact numerical support for each player remains an internal FIFPRO detail, the selections themselves provide clear indicators of who the world's players consider to be the most impactful and exceptional, celebrating both unparalleled consistency and groundbreaking new arrivals to the sport's highest echelons.
