FIDE Quietly Shifts Gukesh-Sindarov World Championship Dates

The International Chess Federation (FIDE) has discreetly revised the dates for the eagerly awaited World Championship match between challenger D. Gukesh and reigning champion Sindarov, moving the high-stakes encounter from an anticipated late 2024 schedule to early 2025. This subtle recalibration, confirmed through quiet updates rather than a formal announcement, marks a significant logistical adjustment for one of the sport's most prestigious events and raises questions about FIDE's communication strategy.
