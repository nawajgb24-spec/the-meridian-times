Ngetich Shatters Half Marathon World Record at Copenhagen 2026

Kenyan long-distance runner Agnes Jebet Ngetich has achieved a monumental feat at the World Athletics Road Running Championships Copenhagen 2026, setting a new women's half marathon world record. Clocking an astonishing 1:02:45, Ngetich decisively broke the previous mark, solidifying her status as a global athletics phenomenon. Her emotional dedication of the win to her mother resonated widely, underscoring a powerful personal triumph alongside athletic excellence. The record-breaking performance not only elevates Ngetich's career but also reaffirms Kenya's enduring legacy in elite road running.

Copenhagen, Denmark – Agnes Jebet Ngetich of Kenya has written a new chapter in the annals of athletic history, shattering the women's half marathon world record at the World Athletics Road Running Championships Copenhagen 2026. Her remarkable performance saw her cross the finish line in an astounding 1 hour, 2 minutes, and 45 seconds, eclipsing the previous global benchmark. Ngetich's achievement represents a significant milestone in women's long-distance running, demonstrating a formidable blend of speed, endurance, and strategic execution. The new record was met with immediate acclaim from the international athletics community and a wave of national pride in Kenya.
