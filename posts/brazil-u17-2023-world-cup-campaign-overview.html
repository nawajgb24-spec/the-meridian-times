The FIFA U-17 World Cup is a biennial international football tournament contested by the men's national under-17 teams of FIFA's member associations. It was first held in 1985 in China as the FIFA U-16 World Championship before being re-branded to its current format. Brazil holds a distinguished record in the competition, having lifted the trophy four times (1997, 1999, 2003, 2019), placing them among the most successful nations alongside Nigeria. Participation in the tournament offers young players invaluable experience on a global stage, exposing them to diverse playing styles and high-pressure situations. For a football-mad nation like Brazil, success at this level is often seen as a barometer for the health of its youth development system and a preview of the senior national team's future potential.
