Wisconsin's American Club Recognized Among World's Elite Hotels

The American Club in Kohler, Wisconsin, has achieved significant international recognition, earning a place in Travel + Leisure's 2024 World's Best Awards. This historic luxury resort was named among the 'Top 100 Hotels in the World' and secured the 'No. 1 Resort in the Midwest' title. The accolade underscores the property's enduring commitment to exceptional service, its unique heritage, and its comprehensive array of world-class amenities, including renowned golf courses and a luxury spa. The ranking elevates Wisconsin's profile in the global luxury travel market and marks a notable achievement for a property located outside traditional major tourist hubs.

### Wisconsin's American Club Recognized Among World's Elite Hotels **Kohler, Wisconsin** – The American Club, a distinguished luxury resort nestled in the village of Kohler, Wisconsin, has garnered international acclaim, securing a prominent position in Travel + Leisure magazine's 2024 World's Best Awards. This recognition places the historic property among the 'Top 100 Hotels in the World' and designates it as the 'No. 1 Resort in the Midwest,' highlighting its exceptional standards in global hospitality. The annual Travel + Leisure World's Best Awards are determined by reader surveys, which evaluate hotels, resorts, cities, islands, cruise lines, spas, and airlines on various criteria, including facilities, location, service, food, and overall value. The inclusion of The American Club on such a prestigious global list marks a significant achievement for a property located in a smaller Midwestern community. ### Background The American Club's journey to global recognition is rooted in a unique history that began over a century ago. The building, constructed in 1918 by the Kohler Co., initially served as a dormitory for single immigrant men employed at the company's factories. It provided comfortable lodging, English language classes, and a taste of American culture, reflecting the company's progressive vision for its workforce. After decades of service as an employee residence, the building underwent an extensive transformation in the late 1970s. Recognizing the potential to repurpose the historic structure, Kohler Co. embarked on a project to convert the dormitory into a luxury hotel, aiming to create a unique destination that embodied quality and service. The conversion carefully preserved the architectural integrity and historical charm of the original Tudor-style building. This meticulous renovation laid the groundwork for its new identity as a premier hospitality establishment, retaining elements of its past while embracing a future in luxury travel. ### Timeline **1918:** The American Club is originally constructed by Kohler Co. to serve as a dormitory for its predominantly European immigrant male workforce, providing housing, meals, and educational opportunities. **Late 1970s:** Kohler Co. leadership initiates plans for a comprehensive renovation and transformation of the historic dormitory building, recognizing its potential for a new purpose. **1981:** Following extensive renovation work, The American Club officially reopens its doors as a luxury hotel. This marked its debut into the hospitality industry, offering refined accommodations and services. **Various Years (1980s-Present):** Over the subsequent decades, The American Club steadily expands its offerings and reputation. Key developments include the establishment of championship golf courses like Blackwolf Run and Whistling Straits, and the creation of the Kohler Waters Spa, enhancing its appeal as a comprehensive destination resort. **2004, 2010, 2015:** Whistling Straits, one of The American Club's golf courses, hosts the prestigious PGA Championship multiple times, bringing international attention to the property and the region. **2021:** Whistling Straits further elevates its global profile by hosting the Ryder Cup, one of golf's most significant international team competitions, drawing visitors and media from around the world. **2024:** Travel + Leisure magazine announces its annual World's Best Awards, recognizing The American Club among the 'Top 100 Hotels in the World' and as the 'No. 1 Resort in the Midwest,' cementing its status as a top-tier global destination. ### Latest Developments The 2024 Travel + Leisure World's Best Awards represent the most recent significant accolade for The American Club. The announcement, released earlier this year, highlights the resort's consistent ability to deliver an exceptional guest experience, as perceived by a global readership of discerning travelers. The ranking places The American Club in an exclusive group of properties worldwide, often alongside hotels in major metropolitan areas or iconic international destinations. Hotel management has noted that such awards are a testament to the dedicated staff and the unique offerings that define The American Club. The property emphasizes personalized service, meticulous attention to detail, and a commitment to maintaining its historic charm while providing modern amenities. ### Analysis The inclusion of The American Club in Travel + Leisure's World's Best Awards is significant for several reasons.
