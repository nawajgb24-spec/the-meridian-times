Caracas to Host World Junior U20 Chess Championship in 2026

The International Chess Federation (FIDE) has officially announced that the World Junior U20 Chess Championship will commence in Caracas, Venezuela, in 2026. This decision positions the Venezuelan capital as the stage for one of the most significant youth tournaments in the global chess calendar, drawing promising talents from across the world. The championship, a crucial event for the development of future grandmasters, is expected to bring international attention to the local chess scene and the region.
