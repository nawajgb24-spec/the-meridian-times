## Agnes Ngetich Shatters Women-Only World Half Marathon Record in Valencia **VALENCIA, SPAIN** — Kenyan long-distance runner Agnes Jebet Ngetich delivered a historic performance at the Valencia Half Marathon on January 14, 2024, shattering the women-only world record with an extraordinary time of 1:03:00. Her achievement, pending ratification by World Athletics, marks a significant milestone in the annals of road running. Ngetich's dominant run surpassed the previous women-only record of 1:03:51, held by Ethiopia's Yalemzerf Yehualaw since 2021. The atmosphere in Valencia was charged as Ngetich maintained a blistering pace, demonstrating formidable strength and tactical precision throughout the 21.0975-kilometer course. ### Background to a Record-Breaking Season Agnes Ngetich has rapidly emerged as a formidable presence in elite athletics. Her recent string of successes highlights a focused trajectory towards global dominance in road running. Prior to her Valencia triumph, Ngetich had already made international headlines with another world record-breaking performance. In October 2023, she showcased her talent at the inaugural World Road Running Championships in Riga, Latvia. There, Ngetich secured gold and established a new world record in the women's 10K, crossing the finish line in 28:46. This earlier achievement underscored her exceptional form and hinted at her potential for further records. The distinction between 'women-only' and 'mixed-gender' records is crucial in road running. Women-only races prohibit male pacemakers, often resulting in slightly slower times than mixed-gender events where women can benefit from drafting behind men. Ngetich's 1:03:00 in a women-only setting is therefore particularly noteworthy, as it was achieved without such assistance, emphasizing her individual prowess. ### A Season of Milestones: Chronological Progression Ngetich's journey to the half marathon world record has been characterized by consistent improvement and high-stakes performances over a relatively short period. These early experiences provided the endurance and tactical acumen necessary for long-distance success. **October 1, 2023:** At the World Road Running Championships in Riga, Latvia, Ngetich delivered a breakout performance. This victory established her as a prominent figure in global athletics and a force to be reckoned with on the road. **January 14, 2024:** Building on her Riga success, Ngetich lined up at the Valencia Half Marathon. This represented a progression in distance and a further affirmation of her exceptional talent. ### Latest Developments and Immediate Impact Following her historic run, Ngetich expressed joy and gratitude, acknowledging the challenging yet rewarding nature of the race.
