For decades, the event has been synonymous with Portsmouth Raceway Park (PRP) in Lucasville, Ohio, a track revered for its challenging layout and vibrant atmosphere. The DTWC consistently serves as one of the highest-paying and most anticipated races on the LOLMDS schedule, often concluding the championship season. Throughout its history, the DTWC has witnessed countless memorable moments, cementing its status as a must-win for any aspiring dirt late model champion. Its legacy at PRP has fostered a unique connection with the local community and a loyal fan base that travels from across the country to attend. West Virginia Motor Speedway, known affectionately as 'The Speedplant,' offers a different but equally compelling narrative. Located in Mineralwells, West Virginia, this 5/8-mile high-banked dirt oval has a formidable reputation for producing high-speed, thrilling racing. WVMS has, at various times, hosted significant national touring events and possesses the infrastructure to accommodate large crowds. While WVMS has a rich history of its own, including periods of re-opening and renovations under different management, it has not traditionally been the venue for the Dirt Track World Championship. Its larger, faster configuration contrasts with PRP's more modest size, suggesting a different racing dynamic if it were to host the DTWC. ### Timeline The Lucas Oil Dirt Track World Championship has followed a consistent pattern since its inception in 1981, typically holding its annual event at Portsmouth Raceway Park.
