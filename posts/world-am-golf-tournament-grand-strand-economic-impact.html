World Am Golf Tournament Fuels Multi-Million Dollar Boost for Grand Strand

The Myrtle Beach World Amateur Handicap Championship, a cornerstone event in the Grand Strand's tourism calendar, annually injects tens of millions of dollars into the local economy. Attracting thousands of amateur golfers from around the globe, the tournament drives substantial revenue for hospitality, retail, and golf course sectors, solidifying its role as a vital economic engine for the South Carolina coast.

The Myrtle Beach World Amateur Handicap Championship, widely known as the World Am, consistently delivers a significant economic boon to the Grand Strand region of South Carolina. This annual amateur golf tournament draws thousands of participants, translating into substantial spending on lodging, dining, retail, and entertainment across the coastal area. For nearly four decades, the World Am has distinguished itself as one of the largest and most prestigious amateur golf events globally. Its unique format, which handicaps golfers to ensure fair competition across various skill levels, broadens its appeal and encourages a diverse international turnout. **Background** The World Am was conceived in 1984 with the vision of creating an inclusive, high-caliber event for amateur golfers. The tournament sought to capitalize on the Grand Strand's extensive array of golf courses and its established reputation as a premier golf destination. From its inception, the focus was on providing a competitive yet social experience. The format typically involves four rounds of net stroke play, with golfers placed into flights based on their handicaps. Flight winners then advance to a championship round. This structure ensures that players of all abilities have a realistic chance to compete and succeed, contributing to the tournament's enduring popularity. Beyond the competition, a significant draw is the 'World's Largest 19th Hole' — a nightly gathering offering food, drinks, live entertainment, and golf exhibitors. This social component fosters camaraderie and further encourages participant engagement throughout their stay. **Timeline** **1984:** The inaugural Myrtle Beach World Amateur Handicap Championship is held. The event quickly establishes a foundation for a unique, large-scale amateur golf competition. **Late 1980s - 1990s:** The tournament experiences steady growth in participant numbers and international recognition, cementing its position as a staple in the amateur golf calendar. The Grand Strand's golf infrastructure expands to accommodate demand. **2000s - Present:** The World Am consistently attracts 3,000 to 3,500 golfers annually, with peak years seeing higher numbers. It becomes a critical component of the region's late-summer tourism, managed by organizations like Golf Tourism Solutions (formerly Myrtle Beach Golf Holiday). **Latest Developments** The most recent iterations of the World Am have demonstrated its sustained appeal and economic power. The 2023 tournament, for instance, once again welcomed thousands of golfers from all 50 U.S. states and over 20 countries, underscoring its broad geographic reach. Organizers continue to refine the event, incorporating technological advancements for scoring and participant engagement, while maintaining the core elements that have defined its success. The consistent participation levels indicate a strong post-pandemic return and ongoing interest in golf tourism. Economic impact assessments for recent tournaments affirm the event's significant contribution, with estimates consistently placing the direct and indirect financial injection into the Grand Strand economy in the tens of millions of dollars annually. These figures highlight the tournament's crucial role in supporting local businesses. **Analysis** The economic impact of the World Am is multifaceted, extending far beyond the golf courses themselves. Participants typically stay in the area for approximately seven to eight nights, driving substantial demand for accommodations ranging from hotels to rental properties. This influx of visitors directly stimulates the local food and beverage sector, with golfers patronizing restaurants, bars, and grocery stores throughout their stay. Retail establishments, including pro shops, souvenir stores, and general merchandise outlets, also experience a notable increase in sales. Furthermore, the tournament generates significant revenue for the Grand Strand's extensive network of golf courses, not just through tournament fees but also from practice rounds and additional casual play. Transportation services, entertainment venues, and other local businesses benefit from the increased tourist traffic, creating a substantial ripple effect across the entire regional economy. **Public Response** The local business community largely views the World Am as an indispensable annual event. Hoteliers, restaurateurs, and retailers consistently report increased bookings and sales during the tournament week, recognizing it as a critical period for revenue generation, particularly after the peak summer season. Residents, while accustomed to the seasonal influx of tourists, generally express support for the event due to its positive economic ramifications. The Grand Strand's tourism officials and local government entities actively promote and support the World Am, understanding its vital role in bolstering the region's brand as a premier golf and leisure destination. Participants frequently praise the tournament's organization, the quality of the courses, and the welcoming atmosphere of the Grand Strand. Many are repeat attendees, citing the combination of competitive golf, social events, and vacation opportunities as key motivators for their return. **Industry Impact** The golf industry within the Grand Strand is profoundly impacted by the World Am. With dozens of courses utilized for the tournament, the event ensures a period of high occupancy and revenue for operators. This boosts course maintenance budgets, allows for facility upgrades, and supports employment for golf professionals, marshals, and groundskeepers. The broader hospitality industry, encompassing hotels, motels, resorts, and vacation rentals, experiences a surge in occupancy rates. This demand often extends to ancillary services such as cleaning, maintenance, and guest services, supporting a wide range of local jobs. Moreover, the tournament provides a significant marketing platform for the entire Grand Strand. The exposure gained from thousands of out-of-market visitors, many of whom return for future golf trips or family vacations, reinforces the region's status as a top-tier destination. **Future Outlook** The future of the World Am appears robust, with organizers continuously seeking ways to enhance the participant experience and solidify its economic contribution. Maintaining its relevance will involve adapting to evolving golfer preferences, embracing technological innovations, and ensuring the continued high quality of the Grand Strand's golf courses and hospitality infrastructure. Challenges may include economic fluctuations impacting leisure travel budgets, competition from other golf destinations, and environmental considerations for course management. However, the tournament's established brand and loyal following suggest a strong foundation for continued success. Its role as a significant driver of economic activity for the Grand Strand is expected to persist. The consistent ability to attract a large, dedicated demographic of golf enthusiasts ensures that the World Am will remain a cornerstone of the region's tourism economy for the foreseeable future. **Conclusion** The Myrtle Beach World Amateur Handicap Championship stands as a testament to the power of sports tourism. Far more than just a golf tournament, it is an annual economic engine that generates substantial revenue, supports thousands of jobs, and significantly boosts the profile of the Grand Strand. Its sustained popularity and demonstrated financial impact underscore its critical importance to the regional economy, solidifying its place as a premier event on the international amateur golf circuit.
