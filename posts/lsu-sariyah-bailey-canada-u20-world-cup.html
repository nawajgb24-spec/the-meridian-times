LSU's Sariyah Bailey Named to Canada's FIFA U-20 Women's World Cup Squad

LSU Soccer defender Sariyah Bailey has been selected to represent Canada at the upcoming FIFA U-20 Women's World Cup in Colombia. This significant achievement marks a pivotal moment for the young athlete and brings international recognition to the LSU women's soccer program. The tournament, running from August 31 to September 22, 2024, will see Bailey join Canada's 21-player squad as they compete in Group A against hosts Colombia, Ghana, and Australia. Her inclusion underscores her rising profile in international youth soccer and highlights the increasing pipeline of talent from collegiate athletics to global competitions.
