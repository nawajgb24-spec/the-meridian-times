Gustafson 'Super Pumped' for Potential Clark Showdown at FIBA World Cup 2026

Megan Gustafson, the Spanish national team star and University of Iowa alumna, has expressed considerable anticipation for a potential face-off against fellow Hawkeye graduate Caitlin Clark and Team USA at the FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup in 2026. Known for her powerful post-play, rebounding prowess, and scoring ability, Gustafson earned numerous accolades, including the Naismith College Player of the Year and the Wade Trophy in her senior season (2019). Following her collegiate success, she embarked on a professional career that has seen her play in the WNBA and various European leagues, ultimately leading her to gain Spanish citizenship and represent Spain internationally. Caitlin Clark, hailing from West Des Moines, Iowa, followed a path of unprecedented success at the University of Iowa from 2020 to 2024. She redefined women's collegiate basketball with her record-breaking scoring, extraordinary long-range shooting, and exceptional court vision. Clark became the NCAA's all-time leading scorer for both men and women, garnering national attention and significantly boosting viewership for women's basketball. Her transition to the professional ranks and her selection to the USA national team underscore her status as one of the sport's global icons. The FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup stands as the premier international competition for women's basketball, contested every four years. It brings together the world's top national teams, showcasing elite talent and national strategies.
