Spain, conversely, has steadily ascended to become a formidable European powerhouse, known for its tactical discipline, cohesive team play, and resilient spirit. Their program has earned silver medals at both the FIBA World Cup in 2014 and the Olympic Games in 2016, consistently challenging the global elite. The narrative of these two basketball titans includes several memorable confrontations on the biggest stages. Their rivalry intensified at the 2010 FIBA World Championship, where the USA emerged victorious in a hard-fought semifinal. Four years later, they met again in the 2014 FIBA World Cup Final, with the USA once more prevailing to claim the gold. Their paths crossed again in the gold medal game of the 2016 Rio Olympics. More recently, the teams clashed in the quarterfinals of the 2022 FIBA World Cup, a testament to their sustained presence at the highest echelons of the sport.
