USWNT Edges Scotland in Tense FIH Hockey World Cup Clash

The United States Women's National Field Hockey Team secured a hard-fought victory against Scotland in a nail-biting encounter at the FIH Hockey World Cup in Terrassa, Spain. The 2-1 win saw the USWNT hold off a resilient Scottish challenge, earning crucial points in their tournament campaign. This article details the match, its significance, and the broader implications for both teams and the sport.
