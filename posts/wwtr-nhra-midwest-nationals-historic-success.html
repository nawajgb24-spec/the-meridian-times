World Wide Technology Raceway Celebrates 'Historic Success' of 15th NHRA Midwest Nationals

World Wide Technology Raceway (WWTR) has declared the 15th Annual NAPA Auto Parts NHRA Midwest Nationals a 'historic success,' marking a significant milestone for the premier motorsports venue. The three-day event, held from September 29 to October 1, 2023, attracted substantial crowds, showcased elite drag racing, and reinforced its substantial economic contribution to the St. Louis metropolitan area. Under owner Curtis Francois's leadership, WWTR has continuously invested in facility upgrades and fan experience, solidifying the Midwest Nationals as a critical stop on the NHRA Camping World Drag Racing Series and a testament to sustained growth and community engagement.

MADISON, Ill. – World Wide Technology Raceway (WWTR) recently concluded its 15th Annual NAPA Auto Parts NHRA Midwest Nationals, hailing the event as a 'historic success.' The three-day spectacle, held from September 29 to October 1, 2023, solidified the venue's reputation as a premier motorsports destination, drawing significant crowds and generating substantial economic activity for the St. Louis region. The event, a crucial stop on the NHRA Camping World Drag Racing Series’ Countdown to the Championship, featured high-stakes competition across Top Fuel, Funny Car, Pro Stock, and Pro Stock Motorcycle categories. Its successful execution underscores WWTR’s consistent growth and its commitment to delivering a top-tier experience for both competitors and fans. The genesis of World Wide Technology Raceway as a major motorsports hub dates back to 2011, when entrepreneur Curtis Francois acquired the former Gateway International Raceway. Recognizing the facility’s potential, Francois embarked on a comprehensive revitalization project, investing millions into infrastructure improvements, track surfaces, and spectator amenities. This visionary leadership transformed the venue into a multi-purpose motorsports complex capable of hosting NASCAR, INDYCAR, and NHRA events. The NAPA Auto Parts NHRA Midwest Nationals quickly became a cornerstone of WWTR’s annual calendar, building a loyal following and cementing its status as a vital component of the NHRA circuit. The NHRA Midwest Nationals has a rich history at the Madison, Illinois, track. While the event itself has existed longer in different iterations, its consistent presence and growth at WWTR began after Francois's acquisition. Each year has seen incremental improvements, from enhanced spectator areas to state-of-the-art timing systems and a meticulously maintained drag strip. Over the past 15 years, the event has evolved, reflecting both the advancements in drag racing technology and WWTR's continuous dedication to facility excellence. The 2023 edition was a culmination of these efforts, building on a foundation of sustained investment and strategic partnerships that have elevated its profile nationally. The 15th annual event proved to be a thrilling display of drag racing prowess. In Top Fuel, Clay Millican secured an emotional victory, his first in five years, defeating Doug Kalitta in the final round. His win was a popular one, celebrated by fans and fellow competitors alike. Ron Capps continued his consistent performance in Funny Car, clinching another victory, further solidifying his position as a dominant force in the category. His win over Matt Hagan showcased the intense rivalry at the pinnacle of the sport. Pro Stock saw five-time champion Erica Enders navigate a competitive field to claim victory, reinforcing her reputation as one of drag racing's most successful drivers. Gaige Herrera extended his remarkable season in Pro Stock Motorcycle, continuing his dominant run with another win. The declaration of 'historic success' is attributed to several factors. Consistent high attendance, particularly for a post-pandemic era event, highlights the enduring appeal of drag racing and WWTR’s effective marketing and fan engagement strategies. The seamless operation of a large-scale event, coupled with positive feedback from attendees and participants, contributed to this assessment. Furthermore, the quality of competition, with tight races and record-breaking potential, ensures that the event remains compelling. WWTR's ability to host a demanding national championship race while maintaining a family-friendly atmosphere is a testament to its operational efficiency and strategic planning. The public response to the 15th Annual NAPA Auto Parts NHRA Midwest Nationals was overwhelmingly positive. Fans lauded the exciting on-track action, the accessibility of the pit area, and the overall event atmosphere. The economic benefit to the St. Louis metropolitan area, including hotels, restaurants, and local businesses, was significant, with thousands of visitors contributing to regional commerce. Community engagement initiatives, often a part of WWTR’s events, also fostered goodwill. The track's efforts to integrate itself into the local fabric, rather than simply being a venue, have cultivated a supportive base that contributes to the event's ongoing success. From an industry perspective, the NHRA Midwest Nationals at WWTR plays a crucial role in showcasing the sport’s vitality. It provides a platform for manufacturers, sponsors, and teams to connect with a passionate fanbase, driving interest in automotive performance and technology. The event's robust sponsorship with NAPA Auto Parts further exemplifies its commercial appeal and stability. The continued success of the event contributes to the overall health of the NHRA circuit, attracting new fans and solidifying its position within the broader landscape of American motorsports. It also highlights the importance of well-managed, privately owned facilities in sustaining professional racing. Looking ahead, World Wide Technology Raceway is poised for continued growth. Plans for future investments and enhancements remain a priority for Curtis Francois and his team. The track aims to maintain its reputation for excellence, ensuring the NHRA Midwest Nationals remains a premier destination for drag racing enthusiasts for years to come. The partnership between WWTR and NHRA is expected to strengthen, building on the mutual success achieved. Focus will likely remain on optimizing fan experience, exploring new technological integrations, and reinforcing the event's economic and cultural impact on the region. The 15th Annual NAPA Auto Parts NHRA Midwest Nationals stands as a testament to the dedication of World Wide Technology Raceway’s leadership and staff, the enduring appeal of drag racing, and the vibrant support of the St. Louis community. Its designation as a 'historic success' is a recognition of not just one event, but over a decade of consistent effort and strategic development that has elevated WWTR to the forefront of national motorsports.
