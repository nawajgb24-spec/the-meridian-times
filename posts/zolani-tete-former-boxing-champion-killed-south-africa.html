Former Boxing Champion Zolani Tete Killed in South Africa

Former IBF and WBO bantamweight world champion Zolani Tete, 35, was shot and killed in his hometown of Mdantsane, Eastern Cape, South Africa, on September 9, 2023. Tete, known as 'Last Born,' had a distinguished career marked by multiple world titles and a record-breaking knockout. His death has sent shockwaves through the global boxing community, prompting a swift murder investigation by South African police, which led to immediate arrests. The incident underscores ongoing concerns about violent crime in the region.
