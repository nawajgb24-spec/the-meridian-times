Launched to acknowledge and celebrate outstanding achievements in the hospitality sector, the initiative quickly established itself as a definitive global guide for discerning travelers and industry professionals. The ranking process involves a rigorous and independent methodology overseen by The World's 50 Best Hotels Academy. This influential body comprises 580 anonymous experts, including leading hoteliers, renowned travel journalists, and seasoned luxury travelers, each selected for their extensive knowledge and experience in the global hotel scene. Their votes are confidential, ensuring impartiality. Members cast their votes for seven of their best hotel experiences from the previous two years, with a maximum of three votes allowed for hotels within their own region. The criteria for judgment are broad, encompassing everything from innovative design and exceptional service to unique guest offerings, culinary excellence, and a hotel's contribution to its local community and environment. ### Timeline The journey to each year's definitive list is a multi-stage process.
