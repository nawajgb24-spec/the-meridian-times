Taffety Punk Gears Up for World Premiere of 'Freddie: A Girl'

Washington D.C.'s Taffety Punk Theatre Company is set to unveil the world premiere of its new original play, 'Freddie: A Girl.' Known for its distinctive 'Rough-Magic' aesthetic, the production promises to explore themes of identity and self-discovery through a contemporary lens. The premiere marks a significant moment for the company, continuing its tradition of thought-provoking and innovative theatrical works within the vibrant D.C. arts scene.
