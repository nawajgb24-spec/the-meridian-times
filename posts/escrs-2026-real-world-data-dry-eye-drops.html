Real-World Data Bolsters Support for Two Dry Eye Therapies at ESCRS 2026

The upcoming European Society of Cataract and Refractive Surgeons (ESCRS) 2026 conference is set to highlight compelling real-world data supporting the efficacy and safety of lifitegrast and perfluorohexyloctane for Dry Eye Disease (DED). These presentations are expected to provide crucial insights into how these therapies perform in diverse patient populations under routine clinical conditions, validating their roles in the evolving landscape of DED management. The findings are anticipated to inform future treatment strategies and reinforce the value of real-world evidence in ophthalmology.

## Real-World Data Bolsters Support for Two Dry Eye Therapies at ESCRS 2026 **Vienna, Austria —** The global ophthalmology community is keenly anticipating the European Society of Cataract and Refractive Surgeons (ESCRS) 2026 Congress, where significant real-world data concerning two leading dry eye disease (DED) treatments, lifitegrast and perfluorohexyloctane, is slated for presentation. These forthcoming revelations are expected to provide robust affirmation of the therapies' effectiveness and safety profiles in routine clinical practice, extending beyond the controlled environments of initial clinical trials. The presentations at ESCRS 2026 are poised to offer critical insights into patient outcomes, adherence, and tolerability across varied demographics and DED subtypes. Such real-world evidence is increasingly vital for clinicians, aiding in the nuanced decision-making required for personalized patient care and further shaping the therapeutic landscape for a condition that affects millions globally. ### Background Dry Eye Disease is a prevalent, multifactorial condition of the ocular surface, characterized by symptoms of discomfort, visual disturbance, and tear film instability. Its etiology often involves inflammation, hyperosmolarity of the tear film, and neurosensory abnormalities. Left unmanaged, DED can significantly impair quality of life and even lead to severe ocular surface damage. Treatment strategies for DED have evolved considerably over the past decades, moving beyond symptomatic relief with artificial tears to therapies addressing underlying causes. Immunomodulators, such as cyclosporine and lifitegrast, target the inflammatory components of DED. Lifitegrast, specifically, acts as a lymphocyte function-associated antigen-1 (LFA-1) antagonist, blocking the interaction between LFA-1 and ICAM-1, thereby reducing T-cell mediated inflammation on the ocular surface. More recently, non-steroidal options have emerged, including perfluorohexyloctane, which received regulatory approval for DED in 2023. This novel treatment works by forming a monolayer on the tear film, reducing evaporation and improving tear film stability, particularly beneficial for patients with evaporative DED, which constitutes a significant portion of DED cases. Real-world data refers to information collected outside of traditional randomized controlled trials (RCTs), often from electronic health records, claims data, patient registries, and observational studies. It complements RCTs by providing insights into how treatments perform in broader, more heterogeneous patient populations and real-world clinical settings, accounting for comorbidities, polypharmacy, and varying levels of adherence. ### Timeline The journey to robust real-world evidence for these therapies spans several years. The foundational understanding of DED as an inflammatory condition gained traction in the early 2000s, paving the way for targeted immunomodulatory treatments. Lifitegrast ophthalmic solution, known commercially as Xiidra, received its initial regulatory approval from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in 2016, following extensive randomized controlled trials demonstrating its efficacy and safety. Since its market introduction, lifitegrast has been prescribed to numerous patients, generating a substantial body of post-market real-world data. These real-world studies, initiated in the years following 2016, have sought to evaluate its performance in diverse patient cohorts, including those with varying severities of DED and co-existing ocular or systemic conditions. Perfluorohexyloctane ophthalmic solution, marketed as Miebo, represented a significant advancement in 2023 with its FDA approval, offering a new mechanism of action primarily for evaporative DED. The period between 2023 and 2025 has seen accelerated efforts to gather initial real-world insights into its effectiveness, patient experience, and tolerability in clinical practice. The findings from these ongoing and recently concluded studies form the basis of the presentations scheduled for ESCRS 2026. ### Latest Developments Preliminary information suggests that the real-world data for **lifitegrast** will affirm its sustained efficacy in reducing DED symptoms and signs, consistent with its pivotal trials. Studies indicate improved patient-reported outcomes, including reduced ocular discomfort, dryness, and foreign body sensation, across a wide spectrum of DED patients over extended treatment periods. Furthermore, the real-world evidence is expected to highlight its consistent safety profile, with adverse events largely mirroring those observed in controlled trials, predominantly mild and transient ocular irritation. The real-world data for **perfluorohexyloctane** is anticipated to underscore its rapid onset of action and significant benefits in improving tear film stability and reducing evaporative dry eye symptoms. Early observations from clinics suggest high patient satisfaction, particularly regarding its non-blurring formulation and ease of use. The data will likely demonstrate its effectiveness in improving objective measures like tear breakup time and overall ocular surface health in patients suffering predominantly from evaporative DED, providing valuable context for its place in the treatment paradigm. These real-world studies, many conducted across multiple international sites, emphasize the value of observing drug performance in less-controlled, everyday settings, offering a more complete picture of their clinical utility. They encompass a broader age range, varied DED etiologies, and often longer follow-up durations than typical RCTs, strengthening the evidence base for these therapies. ### Analysis The upcoming presentations are not merely confirmations but represent a maturation of understanding regarding these DED therapies. Real-world data fills critical gaps left by highly selective RCTs, which, while providing robust proof of concept, may not fully represent the heterogeneity of patients encountered in daily practice. This evidence is particularly valuable in DED, where patient response can vary significantly due to diverse underlying causes and co-morbidities. The affirmation of lifitegrast's efficacy and safety in real-world settings solidifies its position as a go-to immunomodulatory treatment for moderate to severe DED. For perfluorohexyloctane, the real-world findings will be crucial in establishing its niche, particularly for evaporative DED, potentially guiding clinicians on patient selection and combination therapy strategies. The data provides a more comprehensive view of how these drugs integrate into complex treatment plans and contribute to long-term patient management. ### Public Response The anticipated public response to the ESCRS 2026 findings is expected to be largely positive within the patient community and among ophthalmologists. For patients suffering from DED, robust real-world data offers reassurance regarding the effectiveness and tolerability of their prescribed treatments in practical, daily life situations. Patient advocacy groups are likely to welcome data that helps refine treatment pathways and improve access to effective care. Among eye care professionals, the data will likely spark lively discussions regarding updated treatment algorithms, particularly concerning the optimal sequencing or combination of therapies. While clinicians already rely on anecdotal evidence from their practice, formalized real-world data provides a stronger evidence base to support their clinical judgments and to guide discussions with patients about realistic expectations and potential outcomes. This could lead to a greater sense of confidence in prescribing these agents. ### Industry Impact For the pharmaceutical companies behind lifitegrast and perfluorohexyloctane, the presentation of strong real-world data at ESCRS 2026 represents a significant commercial and scientific validation. Such data can strengthen market positioning, reinforce prescribing patterns, and potentially influence reimbursement decisions by healthcare payers. It can also provide a competitive edge in a crowded DED market, demonstrating superior or consistent performance in varied clinical contexts. Furthermore, these findings may spur further investment in post-market surveillance and real-world evidence generation across the ophthalmology sector. Competitors developing new DED treatments will be evaluating these results closely to identify opportunities and challenges. The success of these real-world studies highlights a growing industry trend towards proving value beyond initial approvals, emphasizing long-term patient benefits and real-world applicability. ### Future Outlook The insights gleaned from the ESCRS 2026 presentations are expected to profoundly influence future DED management guidelines. As real-world evidence continues to grow, it is likely to be increasingly integrated into clinical decision-making tools and educational programs for ophthalmologists and optometrists. This could lead to more nuanced, evidence-based treatment algorithms that account for the diverse manifestations of DED. Further research will likely focus on comparative effectiveness studies leveraging real-world data, exploring which patients respond best to specific therapies or combinations. The long-term impact on patient quality of life, economic burden of DED, and optimal patient adherence strategies will also remain key areas of investigation. The future of DED treatment is moving towards highly personalized medicine, and real-world data is a crucial component in achieving this goal. ### Conclusion The ESCRS 2026 conference is set to be a pivotal event for the dry eye community, with real-world data for lifitegrast and perfluorohexyloctane offering compelling support for their continued utility. These presentations underscore the critical role of real-world evidence in validating and refining therapeutic strategies in ophthalmology. As clinicians gain a deeper understanding of how these medications perform in the varied landscape of patient care, the promise of more effective, tailored treatments for Dry Eye Disease moves closer to reality, ultimately benefiting countless individuals suffering from this chronic condition.
