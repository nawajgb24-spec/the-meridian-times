Australia Dominates Samoa to Secure Rugby League World Cup Final Berth

Australia's Kangaroos delivered a powerful performance against Toa Samoa in the Rugby League World Cup semi-final, securing a resounding 66-6 victory and a place in the tournament's final. The match, held at Emirates Stadium in London, saw stellar displays from multiple Australian players, including hat-tricks from Josh Addo-Carr and Valentine Holmes, with Reece Walsh also contributing a try amidst a comprehensive team effort.

Australia's formidable Kangaroos delivered a commanding performance against a spirited Toa Samoa side, securing their spot in the Rugby League World Cup final with an emphatic 66-6 victory. The semi-final clash, held on November 12, 2022, at London's iconic Emirates Stadium, showcased Australia's clinical efficiency and attacking prowess, overwhelming their Pacific rivals. From the opening whistle, Australia asserted their dominance, executing a relentless offensive strategy that left Samoa struggling to contain their formidable opponents. The Kangaroos' star-studded lineup demonstrated why they are perennial contenders, converting pressure into points with impressive regularity throughout the eighty minutes. While the fixture was initially anticipated to be a tighter contest, given Samoa's impressive run to their first-ever World Cup semi-final, Australia's experience and depth proved decisive. The Kangaroos scored a total of 13 tries, a testament to their fluid ball movement, strong forward carries, and incisive backline play. Among the standout performers, wingers Josh Addo-Carr and Valentine Holmes each crossed the try-line three times, recording hat-tricks that highlighted their blistering pace and predatory finishing. Fullback Reece Walsh, making his mark in a high-stakes encounter, also contributed a significant try, showcasing his agility and attacking flair as part of Australia's widespread offensive assault. Other notable try-scorers included Jack Wighton and Jeremiah Nanai, both bagging doubles, alongside Latrell Mitchell and Liam Martin. Nathan Cleary, Australia's halfback, was equally instrumental, successfully converting nine of the Kangaroos' tries, adding to the scoreboard pressure and maintaining Australia's significant lead. Toa Samoa, on the other hand, embarked on a historic journey, reaching the semi-finals for the very first time.
