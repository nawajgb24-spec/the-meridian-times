These bodies require rigorous documentation, including birth certificates, census records, and other identifying paperwork, to confirm a person’s age beyond doubt. The current record for the longest fully verified human lifespan belongs to Jeanne Calment of France, who lived to an astonishing 122 years and 164 days. Each new supercentenarian, therefore, contributes valuable data to the understanding of human biological limits. ### Timeline Born in approximately 1907, the unnamed individual has lived through an unparalleled period of human history.
