Will Ospreay Reveals Tony Khan Considered AEW World Championship Plans for 2025

Professional wrestling star Will Ospreay recently disclosed that All Elite Wrestling (AEW) CEO Tony Khan had entertained the idea of him becoming the AEW World Champion in 2025. This revelation, made during a public interview, sheds light on the high regard AEW holds for Ospreay, who signed with the promotion in late 2023 after a decorated career in New Japan Pro-Wrestling. The statement indicates a potential long-term vision for Ospreay at the pinnacle of AEW, sparking discussions among fans and industry observers about the company's future championship landscape and strategic booking decisions.

Will Ospreay, a prominent figure in the global professional wrestling scene, has revealed that All Elite Wrestling (AEW) CEO and General Manager Tony Khan considered making him the AEW World Champion in 2025. The statement, made during a recent interview, offers a rare glimpse into the long-term planning and strategic considerations at the highest levels of professional wrestling promotion. Ospreay's disclosure immediately drew significant attention, underlining his rapid ascent within AEW since his full-time debut in early 2024. His transition to AEW was among the most anticipated free-agent signings in recent memory. He rose to international prominence primarily through his extensive tenure with New Japan Pro-Wrestling (NJPW), a promotion renowned for its high-quality in-ring action and competitive environment. During his time in NJPW, Ospreay captured numerous accolades, including the IWGP Junior Heavyweight Championship, the IWGP Heavyweight Championship, and the IWGP United States Heavyweight Championship. His matches in NJPW against competitors such as Kazuchika Okada, Kenny Omega, and Shingo Takagi were widely acclaimed, earning him a reputation for delivering exceptional, high-stakes contests. These performances cemented his status as a top-tier talent, capable of headlining major events worldwide. His style became synonymous with a fast-paced, athletic approach that captivated audiences globally. As his NJPW contract neared its end in late 2023, speculation mounted regarding Ospreay's next move. Major promotions, including AEW and World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE), were reportedly vying for his services. His eventual decision to sign with AEW was viewed as a significant coup for the Jacksonville, Florida-based company, reinforcing its commitment to acquiring and showcasing top international talent. **Timeline** Will Ospreay's professional wrestling career began in 2012, quickly gaining traction in the UK independent scene before making his breakthrough internationally. In 2016, he debuted for New Japan Pro-Wrestling, where he would spend the better part of eight years, establishing himself as a multi-time champion and a main event performer. His NJPW run was characterized by a series of critically acclaimed matches and championship reigns across various divisions. By November 2023, amidst widespread rumors of his impending free agency, AEW officially announced Ospreay's signing at their Full Gear pay-per-view event. The announcement generated considerable excitement, signaling a major addition to the AEW roster. Ospreay, however, still had commitments to fulfill with NJPW, which meant a phased transition to his new promotion. He continued to appear for NJPW into early 2024, notably competing at Wrestle Kingdom 18 in January. His full-time in-ring debut for AEW occurred in February 2024 at the Revolution pay-per-view, where he defeated Konosuke Takeshita in a highly anticipated match.
