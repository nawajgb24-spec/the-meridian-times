Chinese Taipei Dominates Curacao in LLWS International Championship

Chinese Taipei defeated Curacao 9-1 in Game 37, the International Championship of the 2023 Little League World Series, securing their spot in the World Championship game. The decisive victory showcased Chinese Taipei's strong offense and pitching, ending Curacao's resilient run through the elimination bracket. This recap details the pivotal moments, the broader context of the tournament, and the implications for both teams.

The decisive contest, held at Howard J. Lamade Stadium, saw Chinese Taipei showcase a powerful offense and stellar pitching, earning them a coveted berth in the overall Little League World Series Championship game.
