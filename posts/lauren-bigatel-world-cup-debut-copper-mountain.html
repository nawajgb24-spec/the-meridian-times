Lauren Bigatel Makes World Cup Debut in Moguls at Copper Mountain

American freestyle skier Lauren Bigatel marked a significant milestone in her burgeoning career by making her FIS World Cup debut in moguls at Copper Mountain, Colorado, on January 16, 2022. The Park City, Utah, native, a member of the US Ski Team, competed in the women's moguls event, finishing 28th in the qualifying rounds. This pivotal moment underscores her progression through the ranks and signals her entry into elite international competition.
