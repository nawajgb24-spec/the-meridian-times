Each team earns its spot through a rigorous series of qualifying tournaments, starting at the grassroots level and progressing through district, sectional, state, and regional championships. This structure ensures that only the best-performing teams advance to the global stage. ### Timeline The journey to the 2026 Little League World Series is a multi-stage process that spans several months. Local league play, which forms the initial stage of qualification, typically commences in early 2026 across thousands of communities affiliated with Little League International. The culmination of the qualification process occurs with the regional tournaments, held in various locations across the U.S. and internationally throughout July and early August 2026. These regional events determine the final 20 teams that will travel to Williamsport. The 2026 Little League World Series itself is scheduled to take place in mid-to-late August, typically running for approximately ten days.
