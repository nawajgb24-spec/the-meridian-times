Montréal, Canada, is preparing to host the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI) Road World Championships 2026. Montréal’s selection as host is a testament to its deep connection with international cycling. The city is known for the annual Grand Prix Cycliste de Montréal (GPCQM), a UCI World Tour event with a challenging urban circuit featuring Mount Royal. This established pedigree provides a solid foundation for the World Championships' logistical demands. Canada previously hosted the UCI Road World Championships in Hamilton, Ontario, in 2003, showcasing its capability for world-class events. Montréal also hosted cycling events during the 1976 Olympic Games. The 2026 Championships will build upon this rich history, aiming to further elevate cycling's profile across North America. The journey to the 2026 UCI Road World Championships in Montréal commenced with a competitive bidding process. The UCI rigorously evaluates potential host cities on infrastructure, financial capacity, government support, and event experience. In September 2022, the UCI officially announced Montréal as the chosen host, confirming the dates as September 20-27, 2026. This launched a multi-year planning phase, involving close collaboration between the UCI, Cycling Canada, the Corporation du Grand Prix Cycliste de Montréal, and government bodies. Since the announcement, operational planning has intensified.
