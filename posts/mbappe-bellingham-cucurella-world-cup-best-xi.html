Examining World Cup Best XI Selections: Mbappé, Bellingham, and Cucurella's Presence

The selection of a World Cup Best XI is a highly anticipated post-tournament tradition, recognizing standout performers. While Kylian Mbappé and Jude Bellingham were widely acclaimed for their exceptional contributions to the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar, frequently featuring in various hypothetical 'Teams of the Tournament,' the potential inclusion of Marc Cucurella prompts a closer examination. Despite being part of Spain's squad, Cucurella did not play any minutes, making his presence in a performance-based Best XI for that tournament unconfirmed by major journalistic or official sports bodies.

### Introduction Following the conclusion of a FIFA World Cup, global attention often turns to the compilation of a 'Best XI' – an unofficial yet highly regarded selection of players deemed to have delivered the most outstanding individual performances throughout the tournament. These selections, often debated and discussed across media platforms and among fans, aim to encapsulate the finest talents who graced the global stage. Among the names frequently discussed for their impactful displays at the 2022 World Cup in Qatar are France's Kylian Mbappé and England's Jude Bellingham. However, the notion of a 'Best XI' featuring Marc Cucurella alongside these two raises specific questions, given his tournament participation. ### Background The tradition of selecting a 'Team of the Tournament' dates back decades, evolving from simple journalistic assessments to complex data-driven analyses. While FIFA occasionally presents an official 'Dream Team' or recognizes standout individuals through specific awards like the Golden Ball, Boot, or Glove, countless media outlets, football pundits, and fan communities also release their own interpretations. These selections are typically based on a player's on-field contributions, statistical performance, impact on their team's progress, and memorable moments. The 2022 World Cup in Qatar, culminating in Argentina's victory, provided a wealth of talent and memorable performances, prompting extensive discussions about who merited inclusion in such elite company. ### Timeline The 2022 FIFA World Cup ran from November 20 to December 18, 2022, showcasing 64 matches across various venues in Qatar. Immediately following the dramatic final between Argentina and France, and in the subsequent weeks, sports news organizations, football websites, and analytical platforms began publishing their respective 'Teams of the Tournament'. These lists aimed to capture the essence of the event by highlighting players who consistently excelled. Both Kylian Mbappé and Jude Bellingham quickly emerged as consistent fixtures in these post-tournament compilations, reflecting their significant contributions to their national teams' campaigns. ### Latest Developments In the aftermath of the 2022 World Cup, Kylian Mbappé's inclusion in virtually every 'Best XI' was undisputed. As the tournament's Golden Boot winner with eight goals, including a hat-trick in the final, his electrifying performances for France were central to their journey to the final. Similarly, Jude Bellingham, at just 19 years old, garnered widespread praise for his mature and influential midfield displays for England, contributing a goal and an assist and often being lauded as one of the tournament's breakthrough stars. His presence in numerous 'Teams of the Tournament' reflected his exceptional impact. However, a review of prominent post-2022 World Cup 'Best XI' selections from major sports media and official football bodies consistently shows no record of Marc Cucurella's inclusion. While Cucurella was indeed named in Spain's 26-man squad for the tournament, official records and match reports confirm that he did not play a single minute for the national team throughout their four matches in Qatar. Spain's primary left-back during the tournament was Jordi Alba, with Alejandro Balde also featuring. ### Analysis The criteria for selection into a World Cup Best XI are fundamentally rooted in on-field performance and impact. Players are chosen based on their statistical output, defensive solidity, offensive contributions, leadership, and overall influence on their team's success. Kylian Mbappé's case is straightforward: his goal-scoring prowess, pace, and ability to change games single-handedly made him a unanimous choice. Jude Bellingham's dynamic box-to-box play, ball-winning ability, and composure beyond his years earned him deserved recognition. These players met the established criteria through their visible and measurable contributions on the pitch. Conversely, Marc Cucurella's situation, while being a squad member, did not allow for any on-field contributions. Without any playing time, he could not meet the performance-based criteria required for inclusion in a 'Best XI'. Therefore, any claim of his presence in a legitimate 'World Cup Best XI' for the 2022 tournament, particularly one based on actual match performances, remains unconfirmed and inconsistent with the facts of his participation. ### Public Response The unveiling of World Cup 'Best XIs' inevitably sparks widespread public debate, often with passionate arguments for and against specific player inclusions. Fans scrutinize selections, comparing them against their own assessments and frequently creating alternative teams. When a player's inclusion is unexpected or perceived as unwarranted, especially if they had limited or no playing time, it typically generates significant discussion and often skepticism. The general public tends to prioritize tangible on-field impact and statistical evidence when evaluating who truly deserves to be recognized among the tournament's elite. ### Industry Impact Being named in a World Cup Best XI carries considerable prestige within the football industry. For players like Mbappé and Bellingham, such recognition further solidifies their status as global stars, enhancing their market value, increasing endorsement opportunities, and bolstering their appeal to top clubs. For clubs, having their players recognized among the world's best adds to their brand reputation and can influence future transfer negotiations. The impact extends beyond immediate financial gain, contributing to a player's legacy and shaping perceptions of their career trajectory. The absence of such recognition, especially for players who did not feature, naturally limits these associated benefits for that specific tournament. ### Future Outlook Looking ahead, Kylian Mbappé is expected to continue his trajectory as one of the dominant forces in world football, with future World Cups likely to see him at the forefront. Jude Bellingham's career continues to ascend, with his performances at club level consistently placing him among the elite midfielders globally, making him a strong contender for future international accolades. For Marc Cucurella, despite his non-playing role in 2022, his club career continues, and he remains a potential selection for future Spain squads. The landscape of international football is constantly evolving, with new talents emerging and established stars striving to maintain their form for the next global showpiece. ### Conclusion The 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar provided a platform for numerous footballers to showcase their talent, leading to the customary post-tournament selection of 'Best XIs'. Kylian Mbappé and Jude Bellingham unequivocally demonstrated the qualities required for such an honour, delivering standout performances that earned them widespread critical acclaim and frequent inclusion in various compilations. However, based on available records, Marc Cucurella, while a squad member, did not participate in any matches for Spain during the tournament. Consequently, his presence in any credible, performance-based 'World Cup Best XI' for Qatar 2022 remains unconfirmed by major sports reporting and is contrary to the established criteria of recognizing on-field contributions. The true 'Best XI' narratives are built on the tangible impact players make during the competition.
