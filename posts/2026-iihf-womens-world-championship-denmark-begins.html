It annually brings together the finest teams from around the world, creating compelling rivalries and memorable moments. Denmark has a notable history of hosting major international ice hockey events. In 2022, the country successfully organized the IIHF Women's World Championship in Herning and Frederikshavn, earning praise for its logistical efficiency and enthusiastic atmosphere. Prior to that, in 2018, Denmark hosted the IIHF Men's World Championship in Copenhagen and Herning, demonstrating its capacity to manage large-scale sporting spectacles with professionalism and flair. These experiences have positioned Denmark as a reliable and experienced host nation for the IIHF. The decision to award the 2026 Women's World Championship to Denmark underscored the IIHF's confidence in the nation's organizational capabilities and its passion for ice hockey.
