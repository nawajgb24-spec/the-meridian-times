The event is a direct pipeline to the senior ranks, with many U20 medalists transitioning to become Olympic and Senior World champions. Greco-Roman wrestling, one of the oldest and most traditional forms of the sport, distinguishes itself by prohibiting holds below the waist. This rule forces competitors to rely exclusively on upper-body strength, throws, and intricate holds, making it a highly technical and physically demanding style. It rewards explosive power, refined technique, and strategic thinking. Historically, nations such as Russia, Iran, Turkey, Armenia, Georgia, Azerbaijan, and various Eastern European countries have demonstrated consistent strength in Greco-Roman wrestling.
