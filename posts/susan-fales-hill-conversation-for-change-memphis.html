Susan Fales-Hill, the acclaimed co-creator and executive producer of the influential sitcom 'A Different World,' recently brought her 'Conversation for a Change' series to Memphis. Hosted by the Women's Foundation for a Greater Memphis, the event facilitated discussions on critical social topics, reflecting Fales-Hill's long-standing commitment to leveraging storytelling and dialogue for community empowerment and progress.
