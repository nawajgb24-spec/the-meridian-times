For any player to be considered a '2026 World Cup winner' at this juncture, it would require an unprecedented level of foresight, disregarding the myriad of variables that influence football outcomes. Arsenal's transfer strategy typically prioritizes players who fit Mikel Arteta's tactical system and possess the right character and potential for long-term growth.
