Milwaukee has clinched its first World Series title, with shortstop Cooper Pratt, a devout Latter-day Saint, emerging as a pivotal figure. His journey from draft pick to World Series hero, underpinned by his faith, has captivated fans and analysts alike, marking a significant moment for both the franchise and the athlete.
