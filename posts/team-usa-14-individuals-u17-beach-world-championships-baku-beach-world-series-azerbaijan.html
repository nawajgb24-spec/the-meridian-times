Team USA Sends 14 to U17 Beach Worlds, Baku World Series in Azerbaijan

Team USA is deploying a 14-member contingent to Azerbaijan for two key international beach volleyball tournaments: the U17 Beach World Championships and the Baku Beach World Series. This significant deployment highlights the nation's strategic focus on nurturing emerging talent and maintaining a strong presence on the global professional circuit. The delegation represents a concerted effort by USA Volleyball to provide young athletes with invaluable international competition experience while also challenging seasoned competitors on the world stage in Baku.
