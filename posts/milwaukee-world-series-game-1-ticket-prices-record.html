For a city like Milwaukee, which may not consistently host deep playoff runs, the opportunity to secure a World Series berth amplifies fan excitement. This system, while facilitating transactions, can also accelerate price increases, as speculative buyers and eager fans compete in an open market. Furthermore, the modern consumer's increased comfort with purchasing high-value items online contributes to the efficiency of this market.
