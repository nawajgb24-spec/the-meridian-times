If the series is tied at 2-2, the momentum becomes finely balanced, setting up a virtual best-of-three scenario. Both teams enter Game 5 with renewed focus, knowing the next victory provides a significant psychological edge. The trailing team faces immense pressure to win three consecutive games, a formidable challenge. Conversely, if Game 4 results in a 3-1 lead for the other team, the series narrative shifts dramatically, demanding resilience and flawless execution from the team facing elimination. In the rare instance of a team securing a 4-0 sweep, Game 4 becomes a historic moment of triumph and dominance.
