The Milwaukee Brewers secured a commanding victory over the Tampa Bay Rays in Game 3 of the World Series, establishing a 3-0 series lead. The win, fueled by dominant pitching, timely hitting, and a vibrant home crowd, places the Brewers on the precipice of their first championship. Four key takeaways emerged from the pivotal contest.
