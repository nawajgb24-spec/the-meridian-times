Evan Wilkerson Shatters World Record at Para Pan Pacific Championships Finale

Para-swimmer Evan Wilkerson delivered a historic performance, setting a new world record on the concluding day of the 2026 Para Pan Pacific Championships in Brisbane, Australia.
