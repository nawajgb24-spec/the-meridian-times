West Bengal Minister Rescues Drowning Dog During Chhath Puja

West Bengal Minister Aroop Biswas garnered widespread acclaim in November 2023 for rescuing a drowning dog from the Ganges River during Chhath Puja celebrations in Kolkata. The incident, captured on video, showed the Minister for Housing and Transport wading into the sacred river to save the struggling animal, an act of compassion that resonated broadly across social media and media outlets. The event highlighted the intersection of public service, animal welfare, and traditional festivities, drawing significant positive attention to Biswas and sparking discussions on empathy in public life.

## West Bengal Minister Rescues Drowning Dog During Chhath Puja **Kolkata, West Bengal** — West Bengal Minister for Housing and Transport, Aroop Biswas, earned widespread recognition in November 2023 for his spontaneous act of compassion: rescuing a drowning dog from the Ganges River. The incident, which occurred during the significant Chhath Puja celebrations in Kolkata, quickly gained traction across social media platforms, drawing praise for the minister's swift and humane intervention. The event unfolded as millions gathered along the riverbanks to observe the ancient Hindu festival. Biswas was reportedly present at one of the ghats, overseeing arrangements to ensure the smooth conduct of the religious rituals when he spotted the animal in distress. ### Background Chhath Puja is an ancient Hindu festival primarily observed in the Indian states of Bihar, Jharkhand, Uttar Pradesh, and West Bengal, as well as in Nepal. Dedicated to the Sun God (Surya) and Chhathi Maiya, the festival involves devotees offering prayers and making ritualistic holy baths in rivers and other water bodies. The Hooghly River, a distributary of the Ganges, becomes a focal point for thousands of devotees in Kolkata during this period. The festival, typically celebrated a few days after Diwali, is known for its arduous rituals, including fasting, standing in water for extended periods, and making offerings at sunrise and sunset. The presence of large crowds along the ghats is a characteristic feature of Chhath Puja, creating a bustling and often congested environment. Aroop Biswas is a prominent political figure in West Bengal, serving as the Minister for Housing and Transport in the state government.
