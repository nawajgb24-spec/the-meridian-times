Colette Jones Spearheads Global Campaign to Redefine Cleveland's Image

As Chief Marketing Officer for Destination Cleveland, Colette Jones is at the forefront of an ambitious initiative to reintroduce Cleveland to the world. Through strategic branding, dynamic campaigns, and focused storytelling, she and her team are working to transform perceptions and attract visitors, conventions, and talent to the city. This article explores the strategies, challenges, and impact of her efforts in reshaping Cleveland's global narrative and driving economic growth.

### Introduction In the competitive landscape of global cities vying for attention, investment, and tourism, Cleveland has mounted an assertive campaign to redefine its image. At the heart of this endeavor is Colette Jones, Chief Marketing Officer for Destination Cleveland, the city’s official destination marketing organization. Her role is pivotal in crafting and disseminating a compelling narrative about a city often misunderstood, turning what some might consider a 'Mission Possible' into a tangible reality for economic growth and cultural revitalization. Jones leads a comprehensive strategy designed to showcase Cleveland's vibrant cultural scene, burgeoning culinary landscape, robust innovation ecosystem, and unique lakeside appeal. Her efforts are not merely about attracting tourists; they are intrinsically linked to talent attraction, convention bookings, and overall economic development, aiming to position Cleveland as a dynamic, modern American city. ### Background For decades, Cleveland faced significant challenges to its public perception, often battling outdated stereotypes and the lingering effects of industrial decline. This historical context created a complex environment for any entity tasked with promoting the city. Destination Cleveland was established with the explicit mandate to overcome these perceptions and proactively market Greater Cleveland as a desirable place to visit, meet, and live. The organization’s work is multifaceted, encompassing everything from national advertising campaigns to hyper-local partnerships. It involves meticulous research into target audiences, developing compelling content, and leveraging media relations to highlight the city's resurgence. The goal is to correct misconceptions and tell the authentic story of a city that has undergone substantial transformation. ### Timeline The journey to rebrand Cleveland has unfolded over several key phases. Following a period of urban challenges in the late 20th century, the early 2000s saw a concerted push towards urban renewal, with significant investments in downtown infrastructure and cultural institutions. This laid the groundwork for more aggressive marketing efforts. In 2013, the completion of the Global Center for Health Innovation and the Cleveland Convention Center marked a significant boost to the city’s ability to host major events. Destination Cleveland, with Jones's strategic input, subsequently launched the 'This Is Cleveland' campaign. A major turning point occurred in 2016 when the city successfully hosted the Republican National Convention, which provided an unprecedented platform to showcase Cleveland to a global audience, directly challenging existing narratives and highlighting its capacity for large-scale events. ### Latest Developments Under Colette Jones’s direction, Destination Cleveland continues to evolve its promotional strategies. Recent efforts have focused on hyper-targeted digital campaigns, leveraging data analytics to reach specific demographic groups interested in Cleveland's distinct offerings, from its world-class medical institutions to its growing outdoor recreation scene along Lake Erie. The organization has intensified its focus on storytelling through various digital channels, including social media, podcasts, and video series. These initiatives aim to provide deeper, more authentic insights into Cleveland’s neighborhoods, local businesses, and diverse community, moving beyond generic tourist attractions to highlight the city's unique character and quality of life. ### Analysis Colette Jones's approach to 'selling Cleveland' involves a blend of direct marketing, public relations, and strategic partnerships. The core strategy revolves around identifying and amplifying the city's unique selling propositions: its rich cultural institutions, a surprisingly innovative culinary scene, passionate sports culture, and a legacy of manufacturing excellence now transitioning into advanced industries like bioscience and additive manufacturing. The challenge remains significant, as changing deeply ingrained perceptions requires sustained, consistent effort. The strategy focuses on authenticity, presenting Cleveland not as a flawless utopia, but as a city with genuine character, resilience, and a palpable sense of community. The 'Mission Possible' aspect truly resonates here, as it acknowledges the scale of the task while emphasizing the commitment to achieve it. ### Public Response The promotional campaigns led by Destination Cleveland have generally garnered positive reactions from local residents and businesses. Many Clevelanders express a sense of pride and validation as their city receives more positive national and international attention. Local businesses, particularly in the hospitality and retail sectors, often report tangible benefits from increased visitor traffic and convention revenue. Feedback from external audiences, including travel writers, convention planners, and potential talent, indicates a growing awareness and shifting perception of Cleveland. While some ingrained stereotypes persist, there is an observable trend towards recognizing the city's significant transformations and its appeal as a contemporary urban center. ### Industry Impact The strategic marketing efforts overseen by Jones and Destination Cleveland have had a measurable impact on the city's tourism and hospitality industries. Increased convention bookings contribute directly to the local economy through delegate spending on accommodation, dining, and entertainment. The broader tourism campaigns attract leisure travelers, boosting hotel occupancy rates and supporting local attractions. Cleveland’s approach often serves as a case study for other mid-sized American cities looking to revitalize their image. By focusing on specific assets and building a cohesive brand identity, Destination Cleveland demonstrates how strategic marketing can be a powerful engine for economic development, distinguishing Cleveland in a competitive national and international market. ### Future Outlook Looking ahead, Colette Jones and Destination Cleveland are poised to further refine their strategies, particularly in leveraging emerging digital platforms and data-driven insights. The emphasis will likely continue to be on telling Cleveland's authentic story, highlighting its innovation, diversity, and quality of life to a global audience. Efforts to attract and retain talent are expected to become an even more integrated part of the overall branding message. Continued challenges include adapting to evolving global travel patterns, sustaining funding for marketing initiatives, and maintaining the momentum of positive urban development. However, the foundational work has established a clear direction: to ensure Cleveland is recognized not just for its past, but for its vibrant present and promising future. ### Conclusion Colette Jones's leadership in marketing Cleveland to the world is a testament to the power of strategic vision and persistent effort. As Chief Marketing Officer for Destination Cleveland, she has been instrumental in spearheading an initiative that goes beyond mere promotion; it's about narrative reconstruction and economic activation. Her 'Mission Possible' continues to unfold, demonstrating that with focused intent, a city can indeed rewrite its global story and claim its place as a compelling destination for all.
