Breanna Stewart Lifts Team USA into FIBA World Cup 2026 Final Against France

Breanna Stewart delivered a commanding performance, scoring 35 points and securing the decisive basket, as Team USA narrowly defeated Australia 82-80 in a thrilling FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup 2026 semi-final in Doha, Qatar. Her heroics paved the way for a highly anticipated gold medal clash against France, who confidently overcame Belgium 75-68 in their own semi-final. The stage is now set for a showdown between two of women's basketball's global powerhouses.

DOHA, QATAR – Breanna Stewart’s remarkable performance powered Team USA to a hard-fought 82-80 victory over a resilient Australia in the semi-finals of the FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup 2026. The triumph, sealed by Stewart’s clutch scoring and dominant presence, propels the defending champions into the final, where they will face a formidable French squad. The highly anticipated gold medal match is now set for September 4, promising a compelling spectacle between two of the sport’s perennial contenders. Fans in Doha and around the globe are preparing for what is expected to be an enthralling conclusion to the tournament. Team USA entered the 2026 World Cup as the overwhelming favorite, aiming to extend their historic dominance in women’s international basketball. With a legacy built on consistent excellence, the American squad has been a benchmark for the sport globally for decades. Breanna Stewart, a cornerstone of the national team for years, arrived in Doha carrying the weight of high expectations. Her previous accolades, including multiple Olympic gold medals and league MVPs, underscore her status as one of the game's elite talents. France, meanwhile, has steadily solidified its position as a global basketball powerhouse. Their journey to the 2026 final marks a culmination of strategic development and the emergence of a talented generation of players, eager to challenge the established order. The tournament, hosted for the first time in Doha, Qatar, has captivated audiences with its blend of established rivalries and emerging narratives. The state-of-the-art Lusail Sports Arena has provided a fitting backdrop for the high-stakes drama unfolding on the court. The path to the semi-finals saw both Team USA and France navigate challenging group stages and quarter-final matchups with precision. Team USA maintained an undefeated record through their preliminary games, showcasing their depth and tactical versatility. France, similarly, demonstrated impressive form, recovering from an early group stage challenge to secure decisive victories in subsequent rounds. Their cohesive team play and strategic execution earned them a strong seeding for the knockout stages. On September 2, the Lusail Sports Arena hosted the two gripping semi-final clashes. First, France took on Belgium in a contest that showcased European basketball at its finest. France delivered a composed performance, securing a 75-68 win. Led by a balanced scoring effort and stifling defense, the French side controlled the tempo for much of the game. Their ability to execute under pressure allowed them to maintain a comfortable lead, never truly letting Belgium mount a significant comeback. The second semi-final saw Team USA face Australia, a rivalry steeped in history and intensity. The Opals, known for their tenacious defense and physical play, pushed the Americans to their absolute limit in a back-and-forth battle. From the opening tip, the game was a relentless exchange of baskets and defensive stops. Australia’s aggressive tactics disrupted USA’s offensive rhythm, preventing them from establishing a comfortable lead. The score remained tight throughout all four quarters. As the game entered its final minutes, the tension was palpable. With the score tied and possession in Australia’s favor, it appeared the Americans might be heading for an unexpected defeat. However, a crucial defensive stop by Team USA created a fast-break opportunity. Breanna Stewart seized the moment, driving to the basket and converting a contested layup to give USA an 81-80 lead with under 15 seconds remaining. She then calmly sank a free throw after being fouled, extending the lead to 82-80. Australia’s final attempt to tie or win the game fell short, igniting celebrations on the American bench. Stewart finished the game with a staggering 35 points and 12 rebounds, a testament to her unparalleled ability to perform in critical moments. Stewart's performance was not merely a statistical anomaly; it was a demonstration of her leadership and mental fortitude. In a game where Team USA struggled at times to find offensive consistency against a determined Australian defense, Stewart was the singular force that kept them afloat. Her ability to score from multiple areas on the court, coupled with her tenacious rebounding and defensive effort, proved insurmountable for Australia. She single-handedly shifted the momentum in critical junctures, preventing an upset. The victory underscores Team USA's enduring reliance on its star power, even as other nations continue to close the gap. While depth and team cohesion are vital, the ability of a player like Stewart to take over a game remains a defining characteristic of American success.
