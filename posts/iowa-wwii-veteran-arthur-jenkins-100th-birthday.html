Des Moines, Iowa – Arthur 'Art' Jenkins, a respected resident of Des Moines and a veteran of World War II, celebrated his 100th birthday on Tuesday, becoming a centenarian and a living testament to a pivotal era in global history. The event was characterized by warmth and reverence, with attendees sharing stories and offering their gratitude.
