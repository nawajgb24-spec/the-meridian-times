Laura Kraut and Baloutinue Victorious at Longines FEI Jumping World Cup™ Los Angeles

American Olympian Laura Kraut, riding her experienced mount Baloutinue, secured a commanding win at the Longines FEI Jumping World Cup™ Los Angeles held at Santa Anita Park. The victory marks a significant milestone in their bid to qualify for the prestigious World Cup Final, showcasing their precision, speed, and enduring partnership against a field of international competitors. The event, a key leg of the FEI Jumping World Cup™ North American League, drew considerable attention to elite equestrian sport in Southern California.

Laura Kraut, a prominent figure in American show jumping, brings decades of experience to the arena. Baloutinue, a 13-year-old Hanoverian gelding owned by St. Bride's Farm, has been her consistent partner in numerous top-tier competitions, known for his scope, carefulness, and speed.
