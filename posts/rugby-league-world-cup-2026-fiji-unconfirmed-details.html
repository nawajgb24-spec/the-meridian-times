The tournament itself has a rich history, dating back to 1954, and has grown in stature and global reach. Typically held every four years, it brings together the best rugby league nations to compete for the ultimate honour. England hosted the most recent edition in 2022 (delayed from 2021), delivering a highly successful event that showcased the sport's growing appeal. The road to the 2026 World Cup has seen unexpected twists. France was initially announced as the host nation for the tournament, which was then scheduled for 2025 before being pushed back to 2026. This decision was met with enthusiasm, promising a new frontier for rugby league in Europe. However, in May 2023, France regrettably withdrew its bid, citing an inability to guarantee the financial viability and government support required to stage a tournament of such magnitude. This withdrawal plunged the 2026 World Cup into uncertainty, prompting the International Rugby League (IRL) to swiftly initiate a process to identify alternative hosts and reconsider the tournament's structure. The IRL has since been engaged in intensive discussions with various potential host nations and stakeholders, exploring options that could ensure the continuity and success of the quadrennial event. As of early 2024, the International Rugby League has yet to officially name new host nations or unveil a revised tournament format.
