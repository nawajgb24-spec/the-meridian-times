Arlington Heights' Josh Pauls Named to US Para Ice Hockey World Championship Roster

Arlington Heights native and celebrated para ice hockey athlete, Josh Pauls, has once again secured a coveted spot on the U.S. National Sled Hockey Team roster for the upcoming World Para Ice Hockey Championship. Pauls, a four-time Paralympic gold medalist, brings a wealth of experience and leadership to the team as they prepare to defend their title on the international stage. His consistent performance and dedication have made him a cornerstone of the dominant U.S. program, inspiring athletes and fans alike within the adaptive sports community.

### Introduction Arlington Heights, Illinois, celebrates once again as local hero Josh Pauls secures his place on the U.S. National Para Ice Hockey Team roster. The decorated athlete, a four-time Paralympic gold medalist and a veteran presence, is set to compete in the highly anticipated World Para Ice Hockey Championship. Pauls' selection underscores his enduring commitment and elite skill in the adaptive sport, continuing a remarkable career that has inspired countless individuals. His inclusion on the national team for the prestigious World Championship reaffirms his status as one of the sport's most impactful figures. The upcoming tournament represents another opportunity for Pauls and Team USA to demonstrate their dominance on the international stage, building upon a legacy of unparalleled success. ### Background Josh Pauls’ journey to becoming a para ice hockey icon began under challenging circumstances. Born in 1992 without tibias and fibulas, both of his legs were amputated above the knee when he was just 10 months old. Growing up in Arlington Heights, Pauls attended Prospect High School, graduating in 2011, all while cultivating an extraordinary athletic career. His introduction to sled hockey came at age 10 through the Great Lakes Adaptive Sports Association (GLASA). The sport quickly became more than just a pastime; it transformed into a passion and a pathway to elite competition. Sled hockey, an adapted version of ice hockey, allows athletes with lower-body disabilities to play by sitting on a sled equipped with two blades, using two short sticks with picks on the ends to propel themselves and handle the puck. From his early days, Pauls exhibited a natural talent and an unwavering determination. His rapid progression through the ranks of adaptive sports was a testament to his innate ability and the relentless effort he poured into his training. This foundational period set the stage for a career defined by international medals and a profound impact on the sport. ### Timeline Pauls’ career timeline is marked by consistent achievement and leadership: * **1992:** Born without tibias and fibulas. * **1993:** Undergoes amputation of both legs above the knee. * **2002:** Begins playing sled hockey at age 10. * **2008:** Earns first selection to the U.S. National Sled Hockey Team. * **2010:** Captures his first Paralympic gold medal at the Vancouver Games. * **2011:** Graduates from Prospect High School in Arlington Heights, Illinois. * **2012:** Wins gold at the World Para Ice Hockey Championship. * **2014:** Secures his second Paralympic gold medal in Sochi. * **2015:** Adds another World Para Ice Hockey Championship gold to his collection. * **2018:** Achieves his third Paralympic gold medal at the PyeongChang Games. * **2019:** Claims gold at the World Para Ice Hockey Championship. * **2021:** Earns another World Para Ice Hockey Championship gold medal. * **2022:** Completes a historic quad of Paralympic golds at the Beijing Games. * **2023:** Wins another World Para Ice Hockey Championship gold and is named to the latest U.S. National Para Ice Hockey Team roster for the upcoming World Championship, continuing his active role as a key player and leader. ### Latest Developments The recent announcement confirming Josh Pauls' place on the U.S. National Para Ice Hockey Team roster for the upcoming World Para Ice Hockey Championship has generated significant interest. This selection positions Pauls to contribute his vast experience and formidable defensive and offensive capabilities to Team USA as they aim to secure yet another world title. Team USA is widely recognized as a powerhouse in para ice hockey, consistently performing at the highest level in international competitions.
