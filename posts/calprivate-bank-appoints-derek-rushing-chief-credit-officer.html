CalPrivate Bank Appoints Veteran Derek B. Rushing as Chief Credit Officer

CalPrivate Bank has named Derek B. Rushing as its new Executive Vice President and Chief Credit Officer. Rushing brings over 30 years of extensive experience from major financial institutions like City National Bank, Wells Fargo, and Union Bank. His appointment underscores CalPrivate Bank's commitment to strengthening its credit risk management framework amidst ongoing economic shifts and strategic growth initiatives.

## CalPrivate Bank Appoints Veteran Derek B. Rushing as Chief Credit Officer **LA JOLLA, CA –** CalPrivate Bank, a subsidiary of Private Bancorp of America, Inc. (OTCQX: PBAM), has announced the appointment of Derek B. Rushing as its new Executive Vice President and Chief Credit Officer. The strategic move brings a seasoned banking executive with over three decades of industry experience to a pivotal leadership role within the Southern California institution. Rushing's extensive background in credit risk management, commercial lending, and portfolio oversight is expected to significantly bolster CalPrivate Bank's capabilities. His appointment is aligned with the bank's ongoing commitment to maintaining a robust credit culture and supporting its growth trajectory. ### Background CalPrivate Bank was established with a focus on delivering personalized banking services to businesses and high-net-worth individuals across Southern California. Headquartered in La Jolla, the bank emphasizes relationship-based banking, offering a range of services including private banking, commercial lending, and wealth management. The role of Chief Credit Officer is central to any financial institution's stability and strategic direction. This executive is responsible for overseeing the entire credit function, managing risk, and ensuring the quality and integrity of the bank’s loan portfolio. Effective credit leadership is paramount, particularly in dynamic economic environments. ### Timeline Derek B. Rushing's career trajectory spans more than 30 years, during which he has accumulated significant experience across various facets of commercial banking and credit risk. His professional journey includes leadership positions at several prominent financial institutions. Before joining CalPrivate Bank, Rushing served as Executive Vice President and Chief Credit Officer at City National Bank. In this capacity, he was responsible for the strategic direction and operational oversight of the bank’s credit administration, playing a crucial role in its lending activities. Prior to his tenure at City National Bank, Rushing held senior leadership roles at Wells Fargo and Union Bank. His responsibilities at these institutions encompassed diverse areas such as commercial real estate, corporate banking, asset-based lending, and middle market commercial lending, honing his expertise in complex credit structures and risk mitigation. CalPrivate Bank itself has grown steadily since its inception, positioning itself as a key regional player.
