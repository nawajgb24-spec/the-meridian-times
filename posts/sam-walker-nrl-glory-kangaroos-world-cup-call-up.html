Sam Walker Caps Stellar Season with Kangaroos World Cup Call-Up

Sam Walker, the dynamic halfback for the Sydney Roosters, has achieved a remarkable dual milestone, topping off a career-defining NRL season that included a premiership victory and individual accolades with a highly anticipated call-up to the Australian Kangaroos squad for the upcoming Rugby League World Cup. This recognition solidifies his status as one of the sport's brightest talents and marks a significant chapter in his burgeoning career, positioning him on the international stage.
