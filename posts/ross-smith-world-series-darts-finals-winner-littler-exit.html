## Ross Smith Crowned World Series of Darts Finals Champion, Ends Littler's Clean Sweep Bid **Amsterdam, Netherlands** – Ross Smith has been crowned the champion of the World Series of Darts Finals, delivering a commanding performance to defeat Gerwyn Price 11-9 in a tense final held at AFAS Live in Amsterdam. Smith's triumph marks a significant milestone in his career, securing a major title on the Professional Darts Corporation (PDC) circuit. The final weekend also drew considerable attention to the aspirations of teenage prodigy Luke Littler, whose impressive run of form across the 2024 World Series circuit had fuelled speculation of a historic clean sweep. However, Littler's dream was ultimately dashed in a dramatic semi-final clash, where he was narrowly defeated by Price in a sudden-death leg. ### Background: The World Series of Darts Circuit The World Series of Darts is an international series of tournaments organised by the PDC, designed to showcase top darting talent globally. It typically comprises several events held in various international locations, culminating in the World Series of Darts Finals, where players compete for the coveted championship title and substantial prize money. This year's circuit had been particularly notable due to the emergence and sustained success of Luke Littler. Following his sensational debut at the World Darts Championship, Littler had carried his momentum into the World Series, clinching titles at the Bahrain Darts Masters, Dutch Darts Masters, Nordic Darts Masters, and Poland Darts Masters. His consistent victories had established a compelling narrative around a potential 'clean sweep' of the entire series, including the Finals. Ross Smith, a former European Champion, entered the Finals with a reputation as a powerful scorer and an increasingly consistent performer. His journey through the sport has been marked by periods of strong form, and he was regarded as a formidable opponent capable of challenging the sport's elite. Gerwyn Price, a former World Champion and a dominant force in darts, arrived in Amsterdam aiming to add another major title to his extensive collection, having demonstrated strong form throughout the season. ### Timeline: A Weekend of High Drama The World Series of Darts Finals weekend, spanning September 13-15, 2024, commenced with a field of 24 of the world's best darts players. Early rounds saw a mix of established stars and rising talents battling for progression, with several closely contested matches setting the stage for the latter stages. As the tournament progressed into the quarter-finals on Saturday, the intensity escalated. Luke Littler continued his impressive form, navigating his way to the semi-finals with solid performances that reinforced his status as a serious contender. Gerwyn Price also demonstrated his championship pedigree, consistently delivering high-level darts to secure his spot in the final four. Sunday's semi-finals delivered the weekend's most talked-about match. Luke Littler faced Gerwyn Price in a titanic struggle that went down to the wire. The match was a back-and-forth affair, with neither player able to establish a decisive lead. Ultimately, Price emerged victorious with an 11-10 win in a sudden-death leg, ending Littler's pursuit of the clean sweep and securing his place in the final. Ross Smith, meanwhile, had also navigated a challenging path to the final. His semi-final performance showcased his scoring power and composure under pressure, earning him a well-deserved shot at the title against Price. ### Latest Developments: Smith's Dominant Final Performance The final between Ross Smith and Gerwyn Price was a compelling encounter, characteristic of major darts championships. Smith started strongly, immediately putting pressure on the former World Champion. His clinical finishing and consistent heavy scoring proved to be key factors throughout the match. Price, known for his resilience, fought hard to stay in contention, often responding to Smith's surges with impressive legs of his own. The match remained tight for extended periods, with both players exchanging blows and showcasing their high-level abilities. However, Smith maintained a slight edge, consistently hitting crucial doubles when it mattered most. As the final legs approached, Smith managed to create a decisive break, moving ahead by two legs. He capitalised on this advantage, holding his nerve to close out the match 11-9, securing the biggest title of his career to date. The victory was a testament to his sustained focus and skill throughout the demanding tournament format. ### Analysis: A New Champion and a Lingering Narrative Ross Smith's victory at the World Series of Darts Finals is a significant achievement, cementing his position among the sport's elite.
