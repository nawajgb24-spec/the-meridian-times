## India's Path to Hockey World Cup 2026 Semi-Finals: A Dual Challenge Indian men's hockey faces a significant and multifaceted challenge as it eyes the FIH Hockey World Cup 2026, set to be co-hosted by Belgium and the Netherlands. The team's upward trajectory continued with a gold medal at the 2022 Asian Games in Hangzhou, which not only reaffirmed its continental dominance but also secured an automatic qualification spot for the Paris 2024 Olympics. These performances have instilled a renewed sense of belief and raised expectations among fans and pundits alike.
