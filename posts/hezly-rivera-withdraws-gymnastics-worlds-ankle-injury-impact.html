Olympic gold medalist Hezly Rivera has officially withdrawn from the upcoming Gymnastics World Championships, citing an ankle injury. Her unexpected absence marks a significant setback for the U.S. women's team, altering medal projections and requiring adjustments to the competitive lineup for one of the sport's most prestigious events. The withdrawal initiates discussions on team strategy, athlete recovery, and the broader implications for international gymnastics.
