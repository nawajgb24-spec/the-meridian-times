World Wide Technology Raceway Hosts Sellout 15th NHRA Midwest Nationals

The 15th Annual NAPA Auto Parts NHRA Midwest Nationals at World Wide Technology Raceway (WWT Raceway) in Madison, Illinois, drew a capacity crowd on Saturday, signaling robust public interest in professional drag racing. The event, a critical stop on the NHRA Camping World Drag Racing Series, showcased elite competition across various categories. This sellout underscores the enduring appeal of NHRA racing and its significant cultural and economic impact on the greater St. Louis metropolitan area.

Madison, Ill. — The 15th Annual NAPA Auto Parts NHRA Midwest Nationals at World Wide Technology Raceway (WWT Raceway) concluded its Saturday action with a confirmed sellout crowd, highlighting the sustained passion for drag racing in the St. Louis metropolitan area. Thousands of fans converged on the Madison, Illinois, facility to witness top-tier competition in the NHRA Camping World Drag Racing Series, reinforcing the event's standing as a major fixture on the national motorsport calendar. The sellout performance on Saturday, a prime day for qualifying rounds and early eliminations, speaks to the event's broad appeal. Spectators experienced the raw power and speed of Top Fuel dragsters, Funny Cars, Pro Stock, and Pro Stock Motorcycles, with drivers pushing their machines to the limits in pursuit of crucial championship points. The NHRA Midwest Nationals holds a significant place in the drag racing circuit. It represents a pivotal late-season event, often playing a decisive role in the championship battles across the professional categories. Teams arrive at WWT Raceway with high stakes, aiming to solidify their positions or make crucial gains as the season progresses towards its conclusion. World Wide Technology Raceway, formerly known as Gateway International Raceway, has a rich history of hosting premier motorsport events. Located just across the Mississippi River from St. Louis, Missouri, the multi-purpose venue features a 1.6-mile road course, a 1/4-mile drag strip, and an oval track. Its strategic location makes it accessible to a large fan base from both Illinois and Missouri. The track first began hosting NHRA national events in 1997, quickly establishing itself as a favorite among competitors and fans alike due to its modern facilities and fast track surface. Over the years, it has undergone significant upgrades and ownership changes, consistently enhancing the spectator experience and competitor amenities. NAPA Auto Parts assumed title sponsorship of the Midwest Nationals in 2009. This long-standing partnership, now celebrating its 15th year, has been instrumental in elevating the event's profile and ensuring its continued success.
