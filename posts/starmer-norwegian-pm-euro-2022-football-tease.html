Starmer Trolls Norwegian PM After Lionesses' Historic Euro 2022 Win

Following England's commanding 8-0 victory over Norway in the UEFA Women's Euro 2022, UK Labour leader Keir Starmer engaged in a light-hearted exchange with Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre. Starmer's 'hard luck' message, sent via social media, acknowledged the Lionesses' dominant performance while extending good-natured commiserations to his fellow Labour politician. The public interaction underscored the role of sport in fostering amicable international relations, even in moments of competitive defeat, drawing widespread media attention for its display of political sportsmanship.

Norway, a formidable team with a strong history in women's football, was widely expected to provide stiff competition. However, England produced a historic performance, scoring eight goals without reply.
