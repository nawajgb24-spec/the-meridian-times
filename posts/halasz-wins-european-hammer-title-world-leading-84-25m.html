The sport demands a unique combination of explosive power, balance, and intricate technique, often requiring athletes to spin multiple times before releasing the hammer. Historically, the event has seen numerous legendary figures push the boundaries of human performance. European athletes, particularly from Eastern Europe, have traditionally excelled in the hammer throw, establishing a strong legacy within the sport.
