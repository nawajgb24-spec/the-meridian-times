U.S. Women Defeat Spain, Set for FIBA World Cup Final Showdown Against France

The U.S. Women's National Basketball team has secured its place in the FIBA World Cup final after a 76-66 victory over Spain in a hard-fought semi-final. The reigning champions will now face France for the gold medal, continuing their pursuit of another international title. This development reinforces the U.S. team's dominant position in global women's basketball, setting the stage for a highly anticipated championship match.
