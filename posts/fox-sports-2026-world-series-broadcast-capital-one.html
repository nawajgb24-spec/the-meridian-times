FOX Sports Confirmed as Broadcaster for 2026 World Series, Capital One Returns as Presenting Sponsor

Major League Baseball (MLB) has confirmed that FOX Sports will serve as the exclusive national broadcaster for the 2026 World Series, with Capital One returning as the presenting sponsor. This announcement solidifies the long-standing partnership between MLB and FOX, which extends through the 2028 season, ensuring the championship series will be accessible to a broad audience across the United States. While specific game dates and times for 2026 will be detailed closer to the event, this confirmation provides clarity for fans, advertisers, and the sports media industry regarding the premier baseball event.
