The tournament structure mandates that teams represent their respective nations, chosen by national governing bodies such as USA Basketball for the United States. The United States has a rich history in international basketball, having won the FIBA World Cup five times, most recently in 2014. USA Basketball is responsible for assembling and training the national team, which typically features prominent NBA players, to compete on the global stage. This national representation is a cornerstone of FIBA competitions, making the concept of a city team competing anomalous. ### Timeline The most recent FIBA Basketball World Cup spanned from August 25 to September 10, 2023.
