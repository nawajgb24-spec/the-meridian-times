Alcaraz and Junior World No. 1 Miguel Share Court at ATP Finals

During the 2023 Nitto ATP Finals in Turin, Spanish tennis sensation Carlos Alcaraz engaged in a practice session with Brazilian junior World No. 1 Guto Miguel.
