Tacoma's Soundview Secures Tense Little League World Series Win Over Texas

Tacoma's Soundview Little League, representing the Northwest region, triumphed over Needville Little League of Texas in a critical 6-2 elimination game at the 2023 Little League World Series. The hard-fought victory, characterized by early lead, a mid-game rally from Texas, and a decisive late-game push, kept Soundview's championship aspirations alive. The win ignited celebrations in Washington and underscored the high stakes and emotional intensity of youth baseball at its pinnacle.
