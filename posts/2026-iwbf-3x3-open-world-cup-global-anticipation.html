### Introduction The International Wheelchair Basketball Federation (IWBF) is preparing for the highly anticipated 2026 IWBF 3x3 Open World Cup. As the countdown begins, the global wheelchair basketball community awaits further details on what promises to be a thrilling display of skill and athleticism. ### Background Wheelchair basketball has a rich history, evolving from a rehabilitation activity for injured veterans into a sophisticated and globally recognized para-sport. Governed by the IWBF, it is played by athletes with various physical impairments, providing an inclusive and competitive environment. The traditional 5-on-5 format has long been a staple of Paralympic Games and major championships. The 3x3 format, a condensed and faster version of basketball, emerged as a distinct discipline in able-bodied basketball before being adapted for wheelchair athletes. Its rapid-fire nature, single hoop, and shorter game duration make it highly accessible and spectator-friendly. The IWBF formally embraced 3x3 wheelchair basketball, recognizing its potential to expand the sport's reach and appeal to new audiences and participants. This adaptation retains the core elements of strategy, teamwork, and individual skill, but with an emphasis on quick transitions and continuous action. The format demands a different tactical approach and specific physical attributes from its players, distinguishing it from its 5-on-5 counterpart. Its growth has been a strategic focus for the IWBF in recent years. ### Timeline The conceptualization of 3x3 basketball as a distinct sport began to gain international traction in the early 2010s. The International Basketball Federation (FIBA) actively promoted the format, leading to its inclusion in various multi-sport events. The IWBF followed suit, initiating discussions and pilot programs for 3x3 wheelchair basketball in the mid-2010s. The first official IWBF 3x3 World Cup events were held in the late 2010s, establishing a precedent for this new championship. These initial tournaments proved successful, demonstrating the format's viability and popularity among athletes and fans. Since then, 3x3 wheelchair basketball has steadily integrated into the competitive calendar, with continental championships and other international tournaments contributing to its development. The announcement of the 2026 IWBF 3x3 Open World Cup represents a continuation of this trajectory, cementing its place as a premier event within the sport. ### Latest Developments The primary latest development is the official confirmation by the International Wheelchair Basketball Federation of the 2026 IWBF 3x3 Open World Cup.
