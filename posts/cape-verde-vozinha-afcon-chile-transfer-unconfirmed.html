Entering as underdogs in a group alongside continental giants Ghana and record-champions Egypt, Cape Verde defied expectations with a series of resilient and tactically astute performances. Their collective strength was complemented by individual brilliance, particularly from goalkeeper Vozinha. Born Josimar Dias in Mindelo, Vozinha has been a cornerstone of the national team for years. In the Round of 16 on January 29, Cape Verde faced Mauritania, securing a hard-fought 1-0 win to progress to the quarter-finals. Their run culminated on February 3 in a tense quarter-final clash against South Africa, which ended 0-0 after extra time. Vozinha made a crucial penalty save during the shootout, but Cape Verde ultimately lost 2-1, bringing an end to their inspiring tournament. ## Latest Developments Following the conclusion of AFCON 2023, the performances of several Cape Verdean players, including Vozinha, garnered significant attention from scouts and media.
