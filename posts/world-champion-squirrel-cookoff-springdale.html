World Champion Squirrel Cook Off Returns to Springdale

The highly anticipated World Champion Squirrel Cook Off is set to return, reportedly to Springdale, Arkansas, marking a significant event for culinary enthusiasts and the local community. Originating in Bentonville, the competition celebrates wild game cooking, sustainable hunting practices, and regional heritage. This article explores the event's history, its unique appeal, and the potential impact of its return to Springdale, as organizers prepare to announce specific dates and venue details for the upcoming competition.

Wilson's vision was to create a platform that not only celebrated the often-overlooked culinary potential of wild game, specifically squirrel, but also promoted ethical hunting practices and a deeper connection to regional food traditions. From its inception, the event was designed to challenge perceptions of squirrel as merely game meat, elevating it to a versatile ingredient capable of sophisticated culinary applications. Teams typically consist of amateur and professional chefs who compete to produce a variety of dishes, ranging from stews and gumbos to more avant-garde preparations like squirrel pâté or tacos. The cook-off's philosophy extends beyond mere competition, aiming to educate the public on nose-to-tail cooking, sustainable sourcing, and the rich cultural history of hunting in the Ozarks. It quickly evolved from a local gathering into a nationally recognized culinary spectacle, consistently drawing participants and media attention to Northwest Arkansas. ### Timeline: Evolution of a Unique Competition **Early 2010s:** Chef Joe Wilson, driven by a passion for wild game and local foodways, establishes the World Champion Squirrel Cook Off in Bentonville, Arkansas.
