England has secured third place in the World Cup after a captivating contest against France, widely hailed as an 'instant classic.' The Three Lions demonstrated resolute determination to hold off a formidable French challenge, earning their first bronze medal in recent memory. The match, played with unexpected intensity for a third-place playoff, provided a dramatic conclusion for both nations after their respective semi-final exits, culminating in a significant achievement for the English squad.
