Kathleen Marshall to Direct World Premiere of 'August Rush' Musical at Old Globe in 2027

Three-time Tony Award winner Kathleen Marshall has been announced as the director for the world premiere of 'August Rush: A New Musical,' set to debut at The Old Globe in San Diego during its 2027 season. The stage adaptation of the beloved 2007 film marks a significant collaboration, bringing Marshall's acclaimed directorial and choreographic expertise to a new musical based on a story celebrated for its themes of music, destiny, and family. The Old Globe, a nationally recognized theatre, continues its tradition of developing impactful new works.

San Diego, CA — Three-time Tony Award winner Kathleen Marshall will direct the world premiere of “August Rush: A New Musical” as part of The Old Globe's 2027 season, the institution announced recently. This highly anticipated production will bring the beloved 2007 film to the stage under the guidance of one of Broadway's most respected director-choreographers. Marshall’s involvement signals a significant artistic commitment to the new musical. Her reputation for staging critically acclaimed and commercially successful productions positions “August Rush” for a potentially robust launch at the renowned San Diego theatre. Kathleen Marshall is celebrated for her extensive career in musical theatre, distinguishing herself as both a director and choreographer. Her three Tony Awards for Best Choreography were earned for her work on revivals of “Wonderful Town” (2004), “The Pajama Game” (2006), and “Anything Goes” (2011). Beyond her Tony-winning achievements, Marshall has directed and choreographed numerous other Broadway productions, including “Nice Work If You Can Get It,” “Grease,” and “Little Shop of Horrors.” Her keen eye for character development, musicality, and dynamic staging has consistently garnered critical praise. “August Rush,” the original 2007 film, captivated audiences with its poignant narrative. Directed by Kirsten Sheridan, the movie tells the story of Evan Taylor, an orphaned musical prodigy who uses his extraordinary talent to search for his birth parents, whom he believes he can find through the music he creates. Starring Freddie Highmore as the titular character, alongside Keri Russell, Jonathan Rhys Meyers, and Robin Williams, the film explored themes of destiny, the power of music, and the unbreakable bonds of family.
