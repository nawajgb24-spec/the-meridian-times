In a commanding start to their World Cup campaign, the United States women's national basketball team secured a dominant 94-61 victory over China. The fixture was particularly notable for the highly anticipated World Cup debuts of collegiate phenoms Caitlin Clark and Paige Bueckers, both of whom delivered standout performances. Their impactful presence underscored the depth and evolving talent within the US squad, signaling a formidable challenge for opponents and an exciting new chapter for international women's basketball. The win firmly positions the US as a frontrunner while shining a spotlight on its emerging stars.
