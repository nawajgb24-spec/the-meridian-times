DARPA's Crucial Role in Combating New World Screwworm in U.S.

In 2016, the unexpected re-emergence of the New World Screwworm in the Florida Keys triggered an urgent response. The Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) played a pivotal, though lesser-known, role by providing rapid logistical support for the Sterile Insect Technique (SIT) campaign. This involved the expedited transport of millions of sterile screwworm flies, the output of specialized gamma irradiators (essential 'SIT radiators'), from Panama to the U.S. This critical intervention, in collaboration with the USDA and Florida state agencies, significantly accelerated eradication efforts, preventing a potentially devastating impact on wildlife and agriculture.
