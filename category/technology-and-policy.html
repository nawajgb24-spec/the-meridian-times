California Enlists Global AI Experts for Safety, 'Kill Switch' Development

California Governor Gavin Newsom has announced the recruitment of world-leading experts to implement his groundbreaking AI executive order, with a significant focus on advancing a 'kill switch' for advanced AI systems. This move solidifies California's position at the forefront of AI governance, aiming to proactively manage the profound risks and opportunities presented by artificial intelligence. The initiative seeks to balance rapid technological innovation with robust safety protocols, addressing concerns ranging from existential threats to societal disruption.
