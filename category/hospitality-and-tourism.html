IHG's Hotel Indigo to Launch on Central Coast

IHG Hotels & Resorts has confirmed the signing of Hotel Indigo Gosford, marking the lifestyle brand's entry into the Central Coast region of New South Wales. The 103-room boutique hotel, developed in partnership with Khedi Hospitality, is set to become a significant part of Gosford's urban revitalisation, offering a locally inspired experience for both leisure and business travellers. The project, initially slated for an early 2024 opening, aims to elevate the region's accommodation offerings.
