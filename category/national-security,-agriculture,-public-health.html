United States Sustains Vigilance Against Screwworm Threat

The United States maintains a robust, multi-agency defense against the New World Screwworm, a devastating livestock and wildlife parasite eradicated from the U.S. in 1966. Spearheaded by USDA APHIS and supported by a unified government response, efforts focus on a permanent barrier zone in Panama, rigorous surveillance, and swift action protocols, as demonstrated by the successful containment of the 2016 Florida Keys outbreak. This proactive strategy is vital for protecting the nation's agricultural economy, wildlife, and public health from reintroduction.
