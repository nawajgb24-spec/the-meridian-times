Edwards and Heymans Crowned Gold Medalists, Set World U20 Leads in Oregon

The World Athletics U20 Championships in Eugene, Oregon, concluded with two standout performances as Jaydon Edwards of Jamaica and Fynn Heymans of Belgium clinched gold medals and established new world U20 leads in their respective events. Edwards soared to a 17.55-meter triple jump, while Heymans powered through the 400-meter hurdles in 54.88 seconds, both performances also securing new championship records. Their dominant displays have marked them as rising stars in the global athletics arena.
