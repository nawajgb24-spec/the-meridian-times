Peachtree 10-U All-Stars Secure Coveted Spot in Cal Ripken World Series

The Peachtree 10-U All-Stars have achieved a significant milestone in youth baseball, qualifying for the prestigious Cal Ripken World Series. This accomplishment follows a dedicated season that saw the team navigate through various competitive district, state, and regional tournaments. The trip to Branson, Missouri, marks a rare opportunity for these young athletes to compete on a national stage, representing their community and showcasing years of hard work, skill development, and teamwork. The journey highlights the aspirations within youth sports and the profound community support that rallies behind local talent reaching such a pinnacle event.
