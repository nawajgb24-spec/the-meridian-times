Global Seasonal Outlook: Key Probabilities for September 2026 Emerge

The September 2026 Global Seasonal Outlook is a critical assessment for anticipating climate trends during the boreal autumn and austral spring. Organizations like the WMO and national meteorological services leverage advanced models to provide probabilistic forecasts for temperature and precipitation anomalies. This outlook factors in major climate drivers such as the El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO) and the Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD), offering vital information for strategic planning across agriculture, energy, and water management sectors worldwide. The current projections suggest varying regional probabilities, underscoring the dynamic nature of global climate systems.

Global Alarm as 'Super' El Niño Threatens Widespread Disruptions

A powerful El Niño event is gaining strength in the Pacific, raising alarms among scientists and international bodies about its potential to unleash a wave of extreme weather across the globe. Experts caution that many regions may be inadequately prepared for the widespread droughts, floods, heatwaves, and other climatic disruptions anticipated in the coming months, posing significant challenges to agriculture, infrastructure, and public health worldwide.
