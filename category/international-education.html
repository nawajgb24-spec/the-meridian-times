EMGS Secures PIEoneer Student Support Award 2026 for World-First Arrival Centre

Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS) has earned global recognition, winning The PIEoneer Student Support Award 2026 for its International Student Arrival Centre (ISAC). This accolade solidifies Malaysia's position at the forefront of international student welfare, acknowledging EMGS's pioneering, comprehensive approach to supporting students from the moment they land. The 'world-first' ISAC, a dedicated facility streamlining immigration, medical, and logistical needs, has been celebrated for setting a new benchmark in global education support and enhancing Malaysia's appeal as an international study destination.
