Publang Expands Digital Catalog with Five New BL Manhwa and Web Novel Titles at Anime NYC

Digital content publisher Publang has announced the acquisition of five new Boys' Love (BL) manhwa and web novel titles at Anime NYC, signaling a significant expansion of its offerings in a rapidly growing genre. This strategic move reinforces Publang's commitment to delivering diverse digital content to a global audience and highlights the increasing prominence of Korean serialized stories in the Western market. The announcement generated considerable interest among fans and industry observers.
