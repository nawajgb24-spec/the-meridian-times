Oregon Enters Day Five of Storm Response: Key Areas to Monitor

As Oregon enters the fifth day of recovery from a severe winter storm, three critical areas demand public attention: the ongoing struggle to restore power to thousands, the evolving flood situation and preliminary damage assessments, and the slow but vital process of reopening key transportation routes. Utility crews face persistent challenges, while state and local agencies coordinate efforts to support affected communities and mitigate further risks from receding floodwaters.
