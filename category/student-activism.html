Cornell YDSA's 'Students of the World Unite' Ignites Campus Dialogue

Cornell University's chapter of the Young Democratic Socialists of America (YDSA) has intensified its 'Students of the World Unite' initiative, aiming to foster international solidarity and draw connections between global conflicts and local activism. The campaign has spurred significant discussion on campus, addressing themes of anti-imperialism, human rights, and the university's role in global issues. This article examines the origins, trajectory, and impact of this movement on the Cornell community and the broader landscape of student activism.
