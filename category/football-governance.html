FA Chair Debbie Hewitt Demands FIFA Transparency on World Cup Sales, Balogun Transfer

FA Chair Debbie Hewitt has publicly urged FIFA to release critical documents concerning the sale of media and commercial rights for the 2030 and 2034 World Cups, alongside paperwork related to Folarin Balogun's transfer to AS Monaco. This significant call, coming from a member of the FIFA Council, intensifies pressure on football's global governing body to enhance financial transparency and improve governance, addressing long-standing concerns within the sport.

Kevin Lamour, Prominent FIFA Official and Critic of World Cup Plans, Departs Role

Kevin Lamour, a significant figure within FIFA, has resigned from his position, marking a notable exit for an official who publicly criticised President Gianni Infantino’s controversial World Cup expansion and frequency proposals. His departure casts a spotlight on ongoing internal debates and strategic directions within football's global governing body, prompting questions about unified vision and leadership moving forward.
