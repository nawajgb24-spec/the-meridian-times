Global Markets Retreat as AI Spending Concerns Emerge; Oil Lower

Global stock markets experienced a notable downturn, with technology-heavy indices leading the retreat, as investors began to re-evaluate the sustainability and immediate returns of substantial Artificial Intelligence (AI) investments. This shift in sentiment, questioning the hefty capital expenditures in AI infrastructure and development, prompted profit-taking across equity markets. Concurrently, international benchmark Brent crude oil settled lower, reflecting broader economic anxieties and projections of softening global demand.

ManpowerGroup Report: Progress in Scaling Global Talent

ManpowerGroup's 'Working to Change the World Report' details the evolving landscape of global talent, highlighting significant progress and ongoing strategies in building workforces at scale. The report emphasizes the critical role of continuous skill development, inclusive practices, and strategic collaboration to address persistent skills gaps and adapt to rapid technological advancements. It underscores a shift towards organizations actively creating talent, fostering human potential as a core business imperative.
