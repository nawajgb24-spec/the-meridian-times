Global Apprehension Mounts Over Potential Return of Trump's Canada Tariff Policies

The world watches with heightened apprehension as discussions intensify around the potential return of former U.S. President Donald Trump's protectionist trade policies, particularly those targeting Canada. Memories of the 2018 steel and aluminum tariffs, and Canada's swift retaliation, underscore the fragility of the critical bilateral trade relationship. While Trump is not currently in office, his campaign rhetoric about imposing universal tariffs has reignited fears of a new era of trade stand-offs, prompting leaders and industries on both sides of the border to consider the profound economic and diplomatic implications.
