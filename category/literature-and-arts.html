Newbery Honor Author Lauren Wolk '81 Ventures into Picture Books

Lauren Wolk, the acclaimed author and Newbery Honor recipient for 'Wolf Hollow', has expanded her literary repertoire by entering the world of picture books. Known primarily for her impactful middle-grade and young adult novels, Wolk's recent debut in this new genre with 'Beautiful', illustrated by Suzy Lee, marks a significant development in her career. This move offers insights into an author's evolving creative path and its potential influence on the broader landscape of children's literature, appealing to a younger audience while retaining her distinct narrative voice.
