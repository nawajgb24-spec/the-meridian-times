U.S. News & World Report Names Top South Carolina Cities for 2026

U.S. News & World Report has released its highly anticipated list of the 11 best cities to live in South Carolina for 2026, highlighting areas that excel in quality of life, value, job market strength, and desirability. Charleston secured the top spot, lauded for its unique blend of history and coastal living, despite higher living costs. Greenville followed closely, recognized for its robust job growth and urban revitalization. The report details a diverse landscape of desirable locations across the state, from bustling metropolitan centers to charming smaller towns, reflecting ongoing demographic shifts and economic development. These rankings offer insights for potential residents and local policymakers, underscoring the dynamic appeal of the Palmetto State.

Northern Kentucky, Ohio Cities Anchor World's Longest Yard Sale Route

The annual 127 Corridor Sale, widely known as the World's Longest Yard Sale, transforms a 690-mile stretch from Michigan to Alabama into an expansive treasure hunt. Cities across Ohio, particularly those along the US-127 route, serve as vital hubs, while the event's reach significantly impacts Northern Kentucky communities, drawing thousands of shoppers and vendors. This multi-state phenomenon, initiated in 1987 to boost rural tourism, continues to generate substantial economic activity and foster community engagement, offering a unique blend of commerce, culture, and roadside adventure for a four-day period each August.
