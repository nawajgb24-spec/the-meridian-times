Blessed Sheen's Baptismal Church to Anchor World's First Parish in His Name

St. Mary's Catholic Church in El Paso, Illinois, where Blessed Fulton J. Sheen was baptized, is set to become part of the world's first parish named in his honor. The Diocese of Peoria announced the formation of Blessed Fulton Sheen Parish, effective July 1, 2024, as part of a broader effort to consolidate churches. This development recognizes Sheen's global legacy while addressing contemporary challenges facing the Catholic Church through strategic mergers designed to optimize resources and pastoral care in a changing landscape.
