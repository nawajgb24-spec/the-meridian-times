Qatar 2022: A World Cup That Reshaped FIFA and Global Soccer

The 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar subjected FIFA to unprecedented scrutiny and instigated significant dialogue around human rights, labor practices, and ethical governance in global sports. From its controversial host selection to its execution amidst widespread protest and advocacy, the tournament presented profound challenges to FIFA's authority and commercial model. While a sporting spectacle and financial success for the organization, it catalyzed a critical re-evaluation of how major events are awarded and managed, leaving an indelible mark on the future trajectory of international football and sports diplomacy.
