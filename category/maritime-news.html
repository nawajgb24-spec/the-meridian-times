Columbia River: Decades-Old WWII Ship Finally Removed

Crews are completing the complex removal of a sunken World War II-era cargo vessel from the Columbia River, an operation spanning months and costing tens of millions of dollars. Submerged for nearly 80 years, the wreck posed significant environmental and navigational risks, prompting a multi-agency effort to dismantle and extract its remains. This article details the history of the vessel, the intricacies of the salvage operation, its impact on the region, and the broader implications for maritime heritage and environmental protection.
