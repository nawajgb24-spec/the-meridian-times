Masdor Plots Historic Upset Against Yoshinari at ONE SAMURAI 4

Malaysian Muay Thai contender Rifdean Masdor is set to challenge Japanese phenom Nadaka Yoshinari for a world title at ONE SAMURAI 4, declaring his intention to 'make history.' This highly anticipated bout pits Masdor's aggressive style against Yoshinari's legendary technical mastery, with the Malaysian fighter aiming for a significant upset that could redefine his career and elevate him onto the global stage. The fight represents a crucial moment for both athletes and for the continued growth of Muay Thai within ONE Championship.
