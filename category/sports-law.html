High Court Rejects Bid to Dismiss Rugby Concussion Lawsuits

A significant legal development in the world of rugby has seen World Rugby, the RFU, and the WRU lose their bid in the High Court to have concussion lawsuits dismissed. Over 200 former players are suing the governing bodies, alleging a failure to protect them from long-term brain injuries. This ruling ensures the case will proceed to a full trial, marking a pivotal moment in the ongoing debate over player safety and the sport's future liability.
