Assessing the Global Football Spectacle: Was the World Cup Worth the Cost?

The FIFA World Cup, a quadrennial global event, consistently ignites debates regarding its substantial costs versus its perceived benefits. While it undeniably delivers unparalleled sporting entertainment and significant revenue for FIFA, the 'worth' of hosting has become increasingly contentious. Recent tournaments, notably Qatar 2022, have brought to the forefront the immense financial investment in infrastructure, widespread human rights criticisms surrounding migrant labour, and environmental considerations. This article explores the multifaceted impacts—economic, social, ethical, and environmental—to provide a comprehensive perspective on whether the world's most popular football tournament justifies its complex price.
