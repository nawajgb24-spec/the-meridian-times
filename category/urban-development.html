The Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) has been recognized with the Mobility Award at the World Smart City Awards 2026, marking a significant milestone in its commitment to transforming urban transportation. Hong Kong's MTR: The World's Most Profitable Urban Railway

Hong Kong's Mass Transit Railway (MTR) stands as a unique outlier in the global public transport landscape, renowned for its consistent profitability. Unlike most urban subway systems worldwide that heavily rely on government subsidies, the MTR Corporation operates on an innovative 'Rail + Property' model. This strategy integrates railway development with commercial and residential property ventures, generating substantial revenues that not only cover operational costs but also fund expansions. This article examines the MTR's distinctive business approach, its historical evolution, financial performance, challenges, and its broader impact on both Hong Kong and the international transit industry, highlighting how it transformed public transport into a self-sustaining and lucrative enterprise.

25 Years On: Libeskind's Enduring Legacy at the World Trade Center Site

As New York City marks a quarter-century since the collapse of the World Trade Center, the architectural vision of Daniel Libeskind, though significantly transformed, continues to resonate through the reconstructed site. This article examines the journey from his 'Memory Foundations' master plan to the vibrant, multifaceted complex it is today, exploring the compromises, collaborations, and controversies that defined one of the most complex urban rebuilding projects in modern history. Libeskind's initial concept for memorialization and renewal laid the groundwork, even as political, economic, and security realities shaped its final form, leaving a legacy still under evaluation.
