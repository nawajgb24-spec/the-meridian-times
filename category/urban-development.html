Construction Underway for Manhattan's Towering New Jail

This ambitious project is central to the city's plan to close the controversial Rikers Island complex by 2027, aiming to replace it with more modern, humane, and geographically integrated borough-based facilities. Cleveland's 'People's Budget' Empowers Slavic Village Residents

Cleveland's Slavic Village is serving as a proving ground for a 'people's budget' initiative, a form of participatory budgeting designed to empower residents in allocating local funds.
