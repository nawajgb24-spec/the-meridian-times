Centenary Horizon: World Mission Sunday Embraces Innovation and 'Sheen Effect'

As World Mission Sunday approaches its significant centenary, a discernible shift towards innovative storytelling and renewed energy is transforming the global Catholic mission. This revitalization draws inspiration from the 'Sheen effect,' acknowledging Archbishop Fulton J. Sheen's pioneering use of media for evangelization, and adapts it to contemporary digital platforms. The article explores how traditional mission efforts are being re-imagined through modern communication strategies to engage a new generation and address global challenges.

Modern Martyrs: Vatican Emphasizes Enduring Witness Amid Global Persecution

The Catholic Church continues to highlight the profound significance of martyrdom in the contemporary world, underscoring that those who die for their faith serve as powerful reflections of Christ's light. While specific references to 'Pope Leo XIV' discussing new martyrs do not align with the established historical lineage of Roman Pontiffs, the Vatican, under Popes like Francis and John Paul II, has consistently drawn attention to the global phenomenon of Christian persecution and the enduring witness of modern-day martyrs. This article explores the Church's theological understanding of martyrdom, its processes for recognition, and the ongoing efforts to advocate for religious freedom in a world where faith-based violence remains a pressing concern.
