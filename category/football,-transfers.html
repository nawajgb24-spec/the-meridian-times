Manchester City Bolsters Goalkeeping Ranks with World Cup Winner

Manchester City has reportedly completed the signing of a World Cup-winning goalkeeper to serve as a back-up, a strategic move aimed at reinforcing squad depth and injecting invaluable experience into the team. While the identity of the player remains officially undisclosed at the time of reporting, the acquisition of such a high-profile figure underscores City's continuous pursuit of excellence and robust competition across all positions, particularly as they navigate multiple demanding competitions.
