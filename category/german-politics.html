Merz Vows to Press Ahead with Reforms After CDU's State Election Defeat

Friedrich Merz, leader of Germany's Christian Democratic Union (CDU), has affirmed his commitment to pressing forward with party reforms despite a significant electoral defeat in a recent state election. The setback, which has amplified internal scrutiny, underscores the challenges facing the CDU as it seeks to modernize its platform and reclaim lost ground in an increasingly fragmented political landscape. Merz's resolve indicates a pivotal moment for the party as it navigates structural changes and prepares for upcoming electoral contests, with implications for Germany's broader political trajectory.
