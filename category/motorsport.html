The Enduring Legend of the WRC's Announcing 'Jinx'

The World Rally Championship has long been a theatre of unpredictable drama, but few elements are as consistently discussed as the phenomenon known as the 'Marty Gallagher Jinx'. This refers to the perceived pattern where drivers often encounter misfortune shortly after WRC+ All Live commentator Marty Gallagher praises their performance or commanding position. What began as an anecdotal observation among dedicated fans has evolved into a celebrated, humorous tradition, shaping the unique character of live WRC broadcasts and fostering a distinct layer of engagement within the rally community.

BBC Secures Exclusive UK Broadcast Rights for WRC from 2027

The BBC has confirmed a multi-year agreement to become the exclusive UK broadcaster for the FIA World Rally Championship (WRC) starting from the 2027 season. This landmark deal will bring comprehensive WRC coverage to free-to-air platforms across BBC iPlayer, the BBC Sport website, and potentially linear television, marking a significant shift for the sport's accessibility in the United Kingdom. The move aims to substantially increase the championship's visibility and fan engagement, returning top-tier rallying to a wide public audience after a period primarily on pay-TV services. The agreement underscores a strategic effort by WRC Promoter to expand its global reach and by the BBC to diversify its sports content portfolio.
