The Enduring Legend of the WRC's Announcing 'Jinx'

The World Rally Championship has long been a theatre of unpredictable drama, but few elements are as consistently discussed as the phenomenon known as the 'Marty Gallagher Jinx'. This refers to the perceived pattern where drivers often encounter misfortune shortly after WRC+ All Live commentator Marty Gallagher praises their performance or commanding position. What began as an anecdotal observation among dedicated fans has evolved into a celebrated, humorous tradition, shaping the unique character of live WRC broadcasts and fostering a distinct layer of engagement within the rally community.
