Nicole Everage's SPS Internship: A Catalyst for Urban Innovation

Nicole Everage, a student at Meridian University, completed a transformative Student Public Service (SPS) internship with the Meridian City Department of Urban Planning and Resilience. Her work analyzing public transportation data not only contributed directly to the city's new transport strategy but also reshaped her career aspirations towards urban planning and smart city development. The experience exemplifies how targeted internships can unlock diverse opportunities and bridge academic knowledge with practical public sector needs.
