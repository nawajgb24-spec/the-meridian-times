North Texas Cities Embrace 2026 World Cup as Strategic Learning Opportunity

As Dallas prepares to host the 2026 FIFA World Cup, neighboring cities Fort Worth and Arlington are viewing the colossal event as an unparalleled learning experience. With AT&T Stadium in Arlington designated as a primary venue and Fort Worth poised to absorb significant visitor overflow, leaders across both municipalities are meticulously studying the logistical, economic, and public safety implications. This proactive approach aims to equip the region for successful World Cup execution and future major international events.
