Dollar Strength Amidst Geopolitical Tensions and Fed Rate Hike Bets

The US dollar has climbed to a two-month high, reflecting a confluence of factors including escalating expectations for Federal Reserve interest rate hikes and a risk premium embedded in global oil prices. An ongoing diplomatic stalemate between the United States and Iran is contributing to this oil price surge, which in turn reinforces inflationary pressures and the Federal Reserve's hawkish monetary policy outlook. This intricate interplay of monetary policy, energy markets, and geopolitics is shaping global economic sentiment and investment flows.
