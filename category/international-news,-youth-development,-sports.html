FIDE's Inaugural Youth Leadership Forum Cultivates Global Minds

The International Chess Federation (FIDE) recently convened its inaugural World Youth Leadership Forum (WYLF), an initiative designed to harness the strategic thinking inherent in chess to foster global youth leadership. Held from October 23-27, 2023, in a hybrid format centered in Budapest, Hungary, the forum brought together 100 young leaders from over 80 countries. Participants engaged in intensive workshops and discussions focused on ethical leadership, public advocacy, and project management, signaling a strategic shift for FIDE to extend its influence beyond competitive play into broader social and educational development.
