Cameroon Authorizes $390 Million World Bank Loan to Propel Economic Reforms

Cameroon has officially authorized a $390 million credit from the World Bank's International Development Association (IDA), marking a significant step in the nation's strategy to bolster economic reforms, enhance fiscal management, and strengthen climate resilience. This credit, approved by the World Bank in December 2023, is earmarked for the Second Inclusive and Resilient Growth Development Policy Operation (DPO), a program designed to foster sustainable and inclusive development across the Central African nation.
