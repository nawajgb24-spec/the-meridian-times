Shakira & Burna Boy's 'Dai Dai' Sets New Billboard Hot 100 Record for World Cup Anthems

The highly anticipated collaboration 'Dai Dai' by global superstar Shakira and Grammy-winning artist Burna Boy has officially become the highest-charting World Cup anthem in the history of the Billboard Hot 100. This landmark achievement underscores the track's immense global appeal, fusing Latin pop with Afrobeats, and highlights a pivotal moment for international music on the mainstream American charts. The song's unprecedented success on the Hot 100 surpasses previous records held by iconic World Cup tracks, including Shakira's own 'Waka Waka (This Time for Africa)', cementing 'Dai Dai's' place in sporting and musical history.
