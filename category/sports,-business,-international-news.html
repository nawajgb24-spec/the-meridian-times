World Cup Semifinal Ticket Prices See Late Drop, But at a Cost

As the FIFA World Cup semifinals approached, a noticeable decline in last-minute ticket prices emerged on various platforms. However, this apparent affordability was largely overshadowed by substantial associated costs and logistical hurdles, particularly the mandatory Hayya Card linked to high accommodation expenses in the host nation, Qatar. This report delves into the dynamics behind the price fluctuations and the complex reality for fans hoping to attend.
