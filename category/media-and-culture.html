Gardeners' World Faces Calls for Evolution Amid Public Debate

Long-running BBC programme Gardeners' World is at the centre of a public debate, with letters to national newspapers urging the show to 'turn over a new leaf'. Viewers are questioning how the beloved horticultural staple can balance its rich tradition with the evolving landscape of modern gardening, encompassing sustainability, diversity, and relevance to younger and urban audiences. This article explores the various perspectives shaping the discussion around the programme's future.
