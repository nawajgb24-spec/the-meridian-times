Mesa Library Retires Iconic Jimmy Eat World Card for 120th Anniversary Design

Mesa Public Library has opted to replace its popular Jimmy Eat World-themed library card with a new design commemorating the institution's 120th anniversary. The decision marks the end of an era for the distinctive card, which celebrated the Mesa-born rock band and had been a community staple since its introduction in 2014. The new card design features historical imagery, reflecting the library's enduring legacy and its commitment to community service.

Iowa Honors WWII Veteran Arthur Jenkins on His Centennial Birthday

Des Moines, Iowa – Arthur 'Art' Jenkins, a distinguished World War II veteran from Des Moines, marked a significant milestone this week, celebrating his 100th birthday surrounded by family, friends, and community well-wishers. Jenkins' century of life spans a transformative period in American history, from the Great Depression to his service in the U.S. Army in the European Theater, through decades of civilian contribution. His milestone celebration serves as a poignant reminder of the sacrifices and enduring legacy of the 'Greatest Generation,' whose numbers continue to dwindle. The event underscored the community's profound gratitude for his service and lifelong dedication, prompting reflections on the importance of preserving the stories of these historical figures.

Tampa Avenue Near Stadium Shut for Two Months Amid Major Infrastructure Overhaul

A critical section of Tampa Avenue, located near Camping World Stadium, has been closed to traffic for an estimated two-month period. The significant closure, which began on August 14, 2024, is part of an extensive storm drain improvement project and utility upgrade initiative spearheaded by the City of Tampa. Commuters, local businesses, and entities associated with the stadium are bracing for the impacts of the rerouting, with officials emphasizing the long-term benefits of the infrastructure investment aimed at improving drainage and preventing future issues.
