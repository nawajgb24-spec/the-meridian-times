Rare Snow Transforms New Zealand Cities, Skiers Conquer World's Steepest Street

An extraordinary cold snap has delivered a rare blanket of snow across several New Zealand cities, captivating residents and creating unusual scenes, most notably on Dunedin's Baldwin Street. The world's steepest residential street briefly became a makeshift ski slope, as locals embraced the unique weather event. This significant snowfall, unusual for urban centres, led to widespread transport disruptions and school closures while simultaneously offering a memorable spectacle.
