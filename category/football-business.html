Infantino’s $25 Billion World Cup Deal Collapse Underscores FIFA's Power Limits

FIFA President Gianni Infantino's ambitious $25 billion plan to sell commercial rights for new tournaments to a private consortium faced overwhelming opposition, primarily from UEFA, and ultimately collapsed. This comprehensive article delves into the proposal, the fierce resistance it met, its implications for global football governance, and what the failure reveals about the boundaries of FIFA's authority in a fragmented football landscape.
