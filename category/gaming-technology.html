'Deeply Odd State of Affairs': Tech Experts Decry Activision's 'Low Effort' Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold War PS5 Port

Activision's Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold War PS5 port faced significant backlash from tech experts and players alike for its confusing upgrade path, inconsistent performance, and perceived lack of next-generation enhancements. Digital Foundry's John Linneman famously dubbed it a 'deeply odd state of affairs,' highlighting dynamic resolution issues and a premium 'cross-gen tax' that felt unwarranted given the port's quality. This article delves into the controversy, its technical underpinnings, the wider implications for cross-gen development, and its lasting impact on player trust and industry standards.
