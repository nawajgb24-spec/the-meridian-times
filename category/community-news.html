Lawrence's Enduring World Cup Tradition Anchored by a Ping-Pong Table

In Lawrence, Kansas, a humble ping-pong table has become the unexpected centerpiece of a burgeoning World Cup tradition, symbolizing the community's profound connection to global football and the enduring power of shared experiences. This article delves into how a local custom evolved into a cherished ritual, bringing together residents across generations to celebrate the beautiful game and foster deep communal bonds.
