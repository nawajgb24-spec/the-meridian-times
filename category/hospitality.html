Wisconsin's American Club Recognized Among World's Elite Hotels

The American Club in Kohler, Wisconsin, has achieved significant international recognition, earning a place in Travel + Leisure's 2024 World's Best Awards. This historic luxury resort was named among the 'Top 100 Hotels in the World' and secured the 'No. 1 Resort in the Midwest' title. The accolade underscores the property's enduring commitment to exceptional service, its unique heritage, and its comprehensive array of world-class amenities, including renowned golf courses and a luxury spa. The ranking elevates Wisconsin's profile in the global luxury travel market and marks a notable achievement for a property located outside traditional major tourist hubs.
