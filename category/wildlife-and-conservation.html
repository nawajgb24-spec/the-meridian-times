Memphis Zoo Lions Receive Special Paintings for World Lion Day Enrichment

In celebration of World Lion Day, the Memphis Zoo implemented a unique behavioral enrichment program, presenting its African lions with special paintings crafted from non-toxic, animal-safe materials. This initiative not only offered novel stimulation for the pride but also served as a focal point for educating the public on critical lion conservation issues and the evolving standards of animal welfare in modern zoological institutions.
