World Maritime Day 2026: Bridging the Gap from Policy to Practice

The International Maritime Organization's World Maritime Day 2026, themed 'From Policy to Practice', spotlights the crucial journey of translating global maritime regulations into tangible, real-world actions. This article explores the significance of this theme, delving into the complexities of implementing international conventions on safety, environmental protection, and seafarer welfare. It examines the ongoing efforts by governments and the shipping industry to meet global standards, address challenges in decarbonization and digitalization, and ensure robust enforcement, highlighting the collective commitment required for a sustainable maritime future.
