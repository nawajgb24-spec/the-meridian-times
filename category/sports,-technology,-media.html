FIFA and TikTok Launch Creator Correspondents Program for World Cup 2026

FIFA and TikTok have announced a significant partnership, establishing the 'FIFA World Cup 2026™ Creator Correspondents' program. This initiative will empower a global network of TikTok creators to generate unique, fan-centric content throughout the lead-up to and during the tournament, which will be hosted across 16 cities in the United States, Canada, and Mexico. The collaboration represents a strategic move to engage a broader, digitally-native audience and redefine sports content creation through authentic, short-form video storytelling, offering perspectives beyond traditional media.
