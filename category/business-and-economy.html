India Poised to Become World's Third-Largest Hotel Market, Hilton Projects

India's hospitality sector is on a trajectory to become the third-largest hotel market globally, a significant projection recently highlighted by Hilton. This anticipated growth is fueled by a confluence of robust economic expansion, rising disposable incomes, a burgeoning domestic tourism segment, and strategic government initiatives. The forecast underscores India's increasing strategic importance for international hotel chains and investors.
