Bay Area Authorities Arrest 25 in Major Human Trafficking Crackdown

Bay Area law enforcement agencies, in a coordinated effort dubbed 'World Cup Operations,' have arrested 25 alleged human traffickers. The sweep targeted illicit networks across multiple jurisdictions, including San Francisco, Oakland, and San Jose, aiming to dismantle operations exploiting vulnerable individuals. Officials stated the initiative was part of broader preparations to combat increased criminal activity often associated with major international events, such as the upcoming 2026 FIFA World Cup. Suspects face charges ranging from human trafficking to pimping and pandering, with ongoing investigations and victim support efforts continuing.
