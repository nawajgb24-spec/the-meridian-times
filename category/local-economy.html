Bellingham's World Cup Economy: Bars Thrive, Hotels Lag

The 2022 FIFA World Cup presented a distinctly uneven economic picture for Bellingham, Washington. Local bars and pubs experienced a significant boost in customer traffic and sales as residents flocked to communal viewing events. In stark contrast, the city's hotels largely missed out on any World Cup-driven economic uplift, illustrating a localized benefit for the food and beverage sector that did not extend to the lodging industry.
