World School Milk Day Celebrated Globally Amid Focus on Child Nutrition

World School Milk Day is an annual international observance dedicated to promoting milk consumption in schools for children's health and development. Initiated by the FAO, the day sees schools worldwide engage in activities highlighting milk's nutritional benefits. While the event is widely celebrated, specific details regarding a celebration by 'Alice Shaw students' could not be independently verified through public records. The article explores the historical context, current initiatives, and future challenges of school milk programs, emphasizing their role in global child nutrition.
