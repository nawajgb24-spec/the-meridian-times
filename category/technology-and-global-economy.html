AI Boom Squeezing Out World's Cheapest Smartphones

The global surge in artificial intelligence capabilities is fundamentally reshaping the smartphone market, particularly at its most accessible end. As AI models, both cloud-based and on-device, demand increasingly powerful hardware, the cost of manufacturing new smartphones rises. This trend is leading to a significant reduction in the availability of ultra-affordable, entry-level devices crucial for digital inclusion in emerging economies. Manufacturers are shifting focus to higher-margin segments capable of supporting AI features, exacerbating concerns about a widening digital divide.
