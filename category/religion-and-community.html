Salt Lake Temple Renovation Nears Completion with 'Rejoice in Christ' Celebration

The Salt Lake Temple of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, after a four-year renovation, is concluding its public open house period. A significant 'Rejoice in Christ' cultural celebration, involving thousands of youth, recently marked a key milestone toward its rededication on September 15, 2024. The project preserved historical integrity while introducing critical seismic upgrades and modern amenities, drawing widespread public and congregational attention.
