New World Screwworm Threat: Vigilance Continues Decades After Eradication

The New World Screwworm, once eradicated from the United States, continues to pose a persistent threat, requiring constant vigilance and a robust international prevention program. While successful eradication efforts in the past pushed the pest out of North and Central America, a permanent barrier zone in Panama and vigilant domestic surveillance remain critical to preventing its re-establishment and safeguarding livestock, wildlife, and public health.
