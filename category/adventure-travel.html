Bend Resident Alex Miller Reflects on Starting Global Cycling Odyssey

Bend, Oregon, resident Alex Miller, 42, recently shared his reflections on the pivotal initial stages of his solo, self-supported cycling journey around the world. Having embarked in early 2021 and completed the major continental segments by late 2023, Miller's odyssey spanned 25,000 miles across five continents, driven by a quest for cultural immersion and personal limits. His current reflections provide insight into the meticulous planning, unexpected hurdles, and profound transformative experiences encountered during the genesis of his remarkable global expedition.
