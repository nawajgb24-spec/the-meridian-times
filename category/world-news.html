Their recent targeting of international shipping in the Red Sea, declared in solidarity with Palestinians, has drawn global attention and military responses, profoundly impacting maritime trade and regional stability. This article examines their origins, objectives, and the broad implications of their actions.

New Projection Offers Fresh Global View: The AuthaGraph Map

The AuthaGraph World Map, developed by Japanese artist and architect Hajime Narukawa, is challenging long-held conventions in cartography. Awarded Japan's Good Design Award in 2016, this innovative projection minimizes distortions in area, shape, distance, and direction more comprehensively than previous attempts. The map's unique method of rendering the spherical Earth onto a flat surface presents a balanced and less Eurocentric view, prompting educators and the public to reconsider established geographic perceptions and the inherent biases in traditional world maps.
