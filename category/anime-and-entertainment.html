Iceblade Sorcerer's Second Season Teases with Creditless Opening, Ending

The Meridian Times reports on the recent sharing of creditless opening and ending sequences for what is understood to be the second season of 'The Iceblade Sorcerer Shall Rule the World' anime. The article provides an overview of the series' background, examines the potential revelations within the new sequences, discusses public and industry reactions, and considers the broader implications for the series' future.
