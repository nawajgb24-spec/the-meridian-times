LA28 Aims for Climate-Positive Games Amidst Sustainability Push

The Los Angeles 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games are positioning themselves as a model for sustainability, with ambitious goals to be the first 'climate-positive' Games. By leveraging existing infrastructure and prioritizing renewable energy and waste reduction, LA28 seeks to significantly reduce its environmental footprint, drawing a stark contrast to the often criticized impacts of past major sporting events like the FIFA World Cup. This article explores the strategies, challenges, and broader implications of LA28's commitment to delivering a 'cleaner and greener' global spectacle.
