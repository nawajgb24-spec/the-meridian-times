Nikon Contest Scandal: Tiny Image, Massive Backlash

In 2015, a winning photograph in the Nikon Singapore Photography Contest sparked a global controversy. A submission by Chay Yu Wei, titled 'A 'Plane' Sight', was celebrated for its dramatic composition until eagle-eyed social media users discovered a minute, seemingly manipulated human figure within the frame. This revelation ignited a fierce online backlash, questioning the integrity of the image, the photographer's ethics, and the contest's judging standards. Nikon Singapore subsequently investigated, leading to the revocation of the prize and a broader discussion about digital manipulation in photographic competitions.
