UEFA Prepares Criminal Complaint Against FIFA President Infantino Amid World Cup Rights Controversy

UEFA is reportedly preparing a criminal complaint against FIFA President Gianni Infantino, reigniting a long-standing dispute over a controversial 2018 proposal to sell commercial rights for new tournaments. The move escalates tensions between global football's governing body and its European counterpart, centering on allegations of a lack of transparency and proper process surrounding a multi-billion dollar investment offer.

Franck Riehm Secures Re-election as President of World Bridge Federation

Franck Riehm has been re-elected as President of the World Bridge Federation (WBF), affirming his continued leadership of the global governing body for contract bridge. The decision underscores a commitment to continuity and stability within the organization as it navigates ongoing challenges and opportunities in the international sports landscape. Riehm, a veteran administrator with deep roots in the bridge community, is poised to lead the WBF through its next term, focusing on areas such as fair play, youth engagement, and the sport's global promotion.

UEFA Warns FIFA of Legal Action Over Stalled $25 Billion Competition Plan

The European football governing body, UEFA, has issued a stark warning to FIFA, threatening legal action over FIFA President Gianni Infantino's controversial and now stalled plan to sell commercial rights for two new global competitions. The multi-billion dollar proposal, which included an expanded Club World Cup and a Global Nations League, met fierce resistance from UEFA and other major stakeholders who raised concerns about transparency, the already congested football calendar, and the potential impact on existing tournaments. This development underscores persistent power struggles and fundamental disagreements within the highest echelons of international football governance.

IOC Declines Jurisdiction Over Infantino Complaint in Balogun Eligibility Case

The International Olympic Committee (IOC) has formally dismissed a complaint against FIFA President Gianni Infantino concerning his alleged interference in footballer Folarin Balogun's national team eligibility switch. Filed by human rights group FairSquare, the complaint claimed Infantino improperly influenced the process that allowed Balogun to represent the United States. The IOC stated the matter falls outside its remit, directing FairSquare to FIFA, the sport's global governing body. This decision highlights ongoing debates regarding jurisdictional boundaries in sports ethics and accountability at the highest levels of global sports administration.
