CalPrivate Bank Appoints Veteran Derek B. Rushing as Chief Credit Officer

CalPrivate Bank has named Derek B. Rushing as its new Executive Vice President and Chief Credit Officer. Rushing brings over 30 years of extensive experience from major financial institutions like City National Bank, Wells Fargo, and Union Bank. His appointment underscores CalPrivate Bank's commitment to strengthening its credit risk management framework amidst ongoing economic shifts and strategic growth initiatives.
