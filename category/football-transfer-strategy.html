The Strategic Allure: Could a Veteran World Cup Winner on a Free Transfer Stabilise a 'United' Defence?

This article explores the strategic considerations for a football club, often referred to as 'United', potentially looking to address defensive vulnerabilities by signing a veteran World Cup-winning defender on a free transfer.
