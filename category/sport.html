England's Enduring Triumph: Two Decades Since World Cup Glory

Twenty years on from their historic victory, England's 2003 Rugby World Cup triumph remains a landmark achievement in the nation's sporting history. The dramatic 20-17 extra-time win against host nation Australia, sealed by Jonny Wilkinson's iconic drop goal, not only brought the Webb Ellis Cup to the Northern Hemisphere for the first time in the professional era but also captivated a nation. This retrospective examines the journey to that pivotal moment, its immediate and long-term impact on English rugby, and the enduring legacy that continues to inspire.
