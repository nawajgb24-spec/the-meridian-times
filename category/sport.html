England Rugby League Reaches Crossroads After World Cup Exit

England's men's rugby league team experienced an early and unexpected exit from the 2022 Rugby League World Cup on home soil, falling to Samoa in the semi-finals. This defeat has ignited widespread debate, positioning the moment as a critical juncture for the sport's future in the nation.
