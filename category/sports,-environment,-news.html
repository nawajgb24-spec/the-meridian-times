Storms Forecast to Clear Wildfire Smoke Ahead of World Cup Final

A significant storm system is projected to arrive in the host city of the upcoming World Cup final, offering a crucial reprieve from persistent wildfire smoke that has plagued the region. While meteorologists anticipate the storm's winds and rain will significantly improve air quality for the high-stakes match, concerns remain regarding the dual challenge of potential storm-related disruptions and the broader implications of environmental factors on major global events.
