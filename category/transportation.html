NJ Transit Chief Praises World Cup Service Plans After Super Bowl Challenges

NJ Transit Executive Director Kevin Corbett has expressed strong confidence in the agency's preparations for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, highlighting a marked improvement in planning compared to the transportation difficulties experienced during Super Bowl XLVIII in 2014. The past event saw significant delays and overcrowding at MetLife Stadium, which will serve as the 'New York New Jersey Stadium' for the World Cup. Current efforts emphasize multi-agency collaboration, enhanced capacity, and optimized logistics to manage the unprecedented scale of the international tournament, aiming to avoid a repeat of prior public transit failures.
