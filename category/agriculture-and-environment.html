Global Fertilizer Study Maps Path to Boost Food, Cut Pollution

A landmark global study has pinpointed critical areas worldwide where optimizing fertilizer use can both significantly increase food production and drastically reduce environmental pollution. This first-of-its-kind research offers a comprehensive roadmap for sustainable agriculture, identifying regions where nutrients are underutilized, hindering crop yields, and areas where overuse leads to environmental damage. The findings provide crucial data for policymakers, farmers, and the industry to implement targeted strategies for a more efficient and eco-friendly global food system.
