Kelly Keel Appointed President and CEO of Texas Association of Business

Kelly Keel has officially taken the helm as the new President and CEO of the Texas Association of Business (TAB), the state's leading advocate for employers. Effective January 1, 2024, Keel's appointment marks a significant leadership transition for the influential organization. Her promotion from Executive Vice President of Government Affairs underscores a commitment to continuity and leverages her extensive experience in Texas policy and legislative strategy. The move is expected to reinforce TAB's mission of fostering a competitive business climate and driving economic growth across the state.
