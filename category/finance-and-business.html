Singapore Police Probe Iron Ore Trader Radiant World Amid Fraud Allegations

Singapore police have launched an investigation into iron ore trading firm Radiant World Pte Ltd over alleged financial irregularities and potential trade financing fraud. The probe, which came to light in March 2024, implicates multiple international banks with reported exposure reaching hundreds of millions of dollars. The incident highlights recurring challenges within the commodity trade finance sector in Singapore, prompting renewed scrutiny of risk management practices and raising concerns about the city-state's reputation as a trading hub.
