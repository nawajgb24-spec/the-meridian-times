Chicago Ranked World's Most Resilient City to Climate Change

Chicago has been recognized as the No. 1 city globally for its resilience to climate change, according to a recent international index. The ranking, conducted by Arcadis in collaboration with C40 Cities, highlights the city's strategic advantages, comprehensive planning, and ongoing infrastructure investments in preparing for future environmental shifts. This achievement underscores Chicago's long-term commitment to sustainability and adaptation, leveraging its access to freshwater and robust urban development strategies. However, maintaining this leading position will require continuous effort in addressing persistent climate-related challenges and ensuring equitable solutions across all communities.

Lima's Arid Resilience: Lessons in Urban Water Management

Lima, Peru, one of the driest capital cities globally, provides a crucial blueprint for sustainable urban water management in the face of extreme scarcity and climate change. With a population exceeding 11 million, the city's innovative strategies, including inter-basin water transfers, advanced wastewater treatment, and desalination, offer vital insights for other arid regions worldwide struggling with dwindling natural resources and increasing demand. This article explores Lima's journey from a city reliant on melting glaciers to a leader in diversified water solutions.
