Pokémon World Championship Draws 50,000 to San Francisco

The Pokémon World Championship 2024 concluded in San Francisco, attracting over 50,000 fans to the Moscone Center. This year's event saw the debut of 'Pokémon XP,' a new immersive fan experience alongside intense competitions across the Trading Card Game, Video Game Championship, Pokémon GO, and Pokémon UNITE. The championship reinforced Pokémon's global appeal and its significant presence in the competitive gaming landscape, generating substantial local economic activity and fostering a vibrant community spirit.
