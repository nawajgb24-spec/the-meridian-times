Arne Engels' Contract Scrutinized Amidst West Ham Interest and 'World Class' Acclaim

The contract situation of FC Augsburg midfielder Arne Engels is under increasing scrutiny following sustained reports of interest from Premier League club West Ham United. Biennial World Cup Plan: 'Worse Than Super League' – Ceferin

UEFA President Aleksander Ceferin strongly condemned FIFA President Gianni Infantino's proposal for a biennial World Cup, likening it to a threat 'even worse than the Super League.' The plan, championed by FIFA to hold the men's World Cup every two years, faced overwhelming opposition from major football stakeholders, including confederations, leagues, and player unions, citing concerns over player welfare, fixture congestion, and the dilution of the tournament's prestige. Despite initial strong advocacy, the proposal was effectively shelved by FIFA in early 2022 due to a lack of consensus, marking a significant victory for those advocating for the preservation of the traditional football calendar and structures.
