Tokyo Film Festival Unveils Diverse Competition Line-up for 36th Edition

The 36th Tokyo International Film Festival (TIFF) has announced its full competition line-up, featuring 15 films from across the globe. The selection includes the highly anticipated world premiere of Indonesian director Garin Nugroho's 'Andragogy' and the international premiere of Macedonian filmmaker Milcho Manchevski's 'Kaymak.' Under the leadership of Festival Director Shozo Ichiyama and with acclaimed director Wim Wenders presiding over the jury, TIFF aims to celebrate cinematic diversity, foster cross-cultural dialogue, and highlight both established masters and emerging talents within the global film landscape.
