Six Flags Great Adventure Unveils Record-Breaking Spinning Coaster

Six Flags Great Adventure has announced 'Flash: Vertical Velocity,' a new spinning launch roller coaster slated for the 2025 season. Manufactured by Intamin, the ride is projected to claim six world records, including being the tallest, fastest, and longest spinning coaster, as well as the tallest and fastest triple-launch coaster. The attraction aims to significantly enhance the park's thrill ride portfolio and redefine industry standards for spinning coaster technology.
