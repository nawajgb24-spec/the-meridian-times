Bay Area Operation Nets 25 Arrests in World Cup Human Trafficking Crackdown

A large-scale human trafficking operation in the San Francisco Bay Area, conducted in conjunction with the World Cup, has led to the arrest of 25 individuals. Federal and local law enforcement agencies collaborated to disrupt networks involved in sex trafficking and forced labor, focusing on vulnerabilities created by major international events. The multi-county effort highlights ongoing challenges in combating exploitation and the sustained commitment of authorities to protect vulnerable populations.
