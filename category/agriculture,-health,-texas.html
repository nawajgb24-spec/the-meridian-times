Texas Officials Warn of Screwworm Threat from Mexico Border

A recent Texas House Agriculture Hearing lauded the effectiveness of past New World Screwworm eradication efforts but underscored significant concerns regarding the ongoing threat of reintroduction from Mexico. State and federal agricultural and animal health officials emphasized the need for sustained vigilance, robust surveillance, and continued funding to protect Texas's vital livestock and wildlife populations from the devastating parasitic fly, which has been largely absent from the U.S. for decades but remains endemic in parts of Latin America. The 2016 Florida Keys outbreak served as a stark reminder of the speed with which the pest can re-establish itself and the intensive resources required for its elimination.
