Port Glasgow's Forgotten Legacy: Tracing a Shipyard's 'Bones' Across the Globe

A hidden chapter of Scottish industrial history reveals how the shipyards of Port Glasgow, notably Lobnitz & Co. and Ferguson Shipbuilders, built specialized vessels whose scattered wrecks now form a global maritime testament. These robust dredgers and workboats, exported worldwide, facilitated international trade and infrastructure development for over a century. The article explores the rise and evolution of these influential Clyde yards, the enduring legacy of their engineering prowess, and contemporary efforts to document the widespread 'bones'—the sunken or abandoned remnants of their creations—that lie in oceans and rivers across continents.
