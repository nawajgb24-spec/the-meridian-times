Komatsu Unveils State-of-the-Art Milwaukee Campus After Years of Anticipation

Komatsu Mining Corp. has officially opened its new $285 million South Harbor Campus in Milwaukee, concluding a multi-year development that transforms a former brownfield site into a hub for advanced manufacturing. The 60-acre facility consolidates Komatsu's P&H brand operations, featuring cutting-edge production lines, a vast solar array, and a commitment to sustainability and job retention in the city. The opening marks a significant milestone for both the global heavy equipment manufacturer and Milwaukee's economic future.

DM Vans: From Rifle to Global Recognition, 800 Conversions Strong

DM Vans, a custom van conversion company based in Rifle, Colorado, has reached a significant operational milestone, successfully delivering over 800 customized vehicles. This achievement underscores the company's substantial growth from a local enterprise to a prominent player in the burgeoning 'van life' and adventure vehicle market. The firm's commitment to bespoke craftsmanship and innovative design has positioned it as a leader, contributing significantly to the local economy while serving a clientele that extends well beyond Colorado's borders.
