FIFA President Infantino Lashes Out at Critics Amid Qatar World Cup Scrutiny

FIFA President Gianni Infantino delivered a defiant address on the eve of the 2022 World Cup in Qatar, strongly rebuking critics for what he termed 'hypocrisy' and 'spreading hate.' His remarks, which saw him declare 'Today I feel Qatari, today I feel Arab, today I feel African, today I feel gay, today I feel disabled, today I feel a migrant worker,' came amidst intense international scrutiny over Qatar's human rights record, particularly concerning migrant workers and LGBTQ+ individuals. The speech ignited further debate about the ethics of hosting major sporting events and FIFA's role in addressing social issues.
