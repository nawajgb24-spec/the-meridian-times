When Digital Worlds End: Players Confronting the Loss of Online Games

The shutdown of an online game, particularly a massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) or live-service title, represents more than just the loss of entertainment for its players. It marks the demise of virtual communities, shared histories, and digital identities, often evoking profound grief. This article explores the emotional landscape of players facing such closures, examining the reasons behind game shutdowns, the diverse ways communities mourn and attempt to preserve their digital heritage, and the broader implications for the video game industry and digital culture.
