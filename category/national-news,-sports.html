Dodgers Star Miguel Rojas Achieves US Citizenship, Expresses Immense Pride

Los Angeles Dodgers infielder Miguel Rojas, a Venezuelan native and World Series champion, has officially become a United States citizen. The veteran baseball player completed his naturalization process, marking a significant personal milestone. He conveyed deep pride in his new citizenship, underscoring his long-standing connection to the country where he has built a successful professional career.
