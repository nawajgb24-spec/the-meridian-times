Ringo Starr's Enduring Culinary Constant: The Two-Ingredient Sandwich

For decades, legendary Beatles drummer Ringo Starr has maintained an unwavering dietary ritual, consuming a simple two-ingredient sandwich. This consistent choice, typically featuring cheese and either Marmite or Vegemite, offers a unique glimpse into the personal habits of a global icon, contrasting sharply with the often-fluctuating culinary trends of celebrity culture. The longevity of this particular meal underscores a preference for routine and comfort amid a life lived in the international spotlight.
