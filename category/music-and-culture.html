The Enduring Echo: 'I Will Always Love You' and Whitney Houston's Legacy

The phrase 'We will always love you' resonates deeply in popular culture, primarily through Whitney Houston's iconic rendition of 'I Will Always Love You'. This article explores the song's journey from its country origins with Dolly Parton to its global pop dominance, examining its profound impact on music, its record-breaking success, and its lasting significance in the wake of Houston's untimely passing. It delves into the song's cultural footprint, its influence on the music industry, and the enduring public affection for Houston's unparalleled vocal artistry.
