Rennie's Firm Conviction: All Blacks to Uphold Core Identity at World Cup

Former Wallabies and Chiefs coach Dave Rennie has voiced a strong belief that the New Zealand All Blacks will adhere to their foundational attacking rugby philosophy during the upcoming Rugby World Cup. Rennie, a respected figure in international rugby, asserts that despite external pressures or recent performance fluctuations, the All Blacks will 'stick to their guns,' prioritizing their traditional expansive and high-tempo game. This perspective highlights the enduring strategic debate surrounding elite teams under tournament conditions and the balance between adaptation and adherence to core strengths.

World Rugby Explores US Six Nations Match, England Reluctant

World Rugby is reportedly considering plans to stage a Six Nations Championship match in the United States, a significant move aimed at expanding the sport's global footprint, particularly in the lead-up to the 2031 and 2033 Rugby World Cups hosted by the US. While the initiative could offer substantial commercial and promotional benefits, England's Rugby Football Union (RFU) is understood to be highly unlikely to participate, citing the immense financial and traditional value of its home fixtures at Twickenham. The proposal underscores World Rugby's ambition to balance the tournament's rich European heritage with the commercial imperative of global growth, presenting both opportunities and considerable challenges for the participating nations.
