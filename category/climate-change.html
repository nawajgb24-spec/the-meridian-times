UN Warns of Inevitable 1.5°C Overshoot, Mandating Carbon Removal Efforts

Recent reports from the United Nations confirm that the global average temperature is highly likely to exceed the critical 1.5°C warming limit established by the Paris Agreement. This stark warning underscores an urgent need not only for accelerated greenhouse gas emission reductions but also for the widespread deployment of carbon dioxide removal (CDR) technologies to prevent catastrophic climate impacts and eventually bring temperatures back down. The international community faces a dual challenge: drastically cutting current emissions while simultaneously developing and scaling methods to extract existing carbon from the atmosphere.
