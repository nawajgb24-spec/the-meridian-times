China's Datang Hami Fresnel Plant Begins Trial, Marks 100MW CSP Milestone

The Datang Hami Baimiaozi 100MW Concentrated Solar Power (CSP) plant, utilizing linear Fresnel technology, has commenced a 14-day trial operation in Xinjiang, China. Positioned as the world's largest single-unit Fresnel CSP project, this development signifies a notable advancement in solar thermal energy, particularly for China's renewable ambitions. The trial operation is a crucial step towards integrating the plant into the national grid and evaluating its performance, potentially paving the way for wider adoption of this specific CSP technology.
