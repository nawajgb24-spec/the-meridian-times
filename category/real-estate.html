Sydney's Housing Boom Ends: A Decade of Growth Reversed by Rate Hikes

After more than a decade of remarkable growth, Sydney's property market is experiencing a significant downturn. Fueled by historically low interest rates, robust population growth, and foreign investment, the city's housing prices soared, making it one of the most expensive urban centers globally. However, a series of aggressive interest rate hikes by the Reserve Bank of Australia, initiated in May 2022 to counter persistent inflation, has effectively halted the boom. This shift has led to declining property values, increasing mortgage stress for homeowners, and a reassessment of market dynamics across the industry, marking the end of an extraordinary era in Australian real estate.

Bay Area Leases See Emerging 'Work-From-Home Fee'

An emerging trend in the Bay Area rental market involves landlords adding a '$200 work-from-home fee' to lease agreements. This fee, reportedly designed to offset increased utility costs and wear-and-tear from remote work, is sparking debate among tenants, landlords, and legal experts regarding its justification and legality in an already high-cost housing region.
