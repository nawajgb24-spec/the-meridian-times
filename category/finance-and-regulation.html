Kalshi Insists it's Not a Sportsbook as World Cup Contracts Surge

Kalshi, a Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC)-regulated exchange, firmly maintains its identity as a derivatives trading platform, not a sportsbook, despite a significant uptick in event contract activity related to the 2022 FIFA World Cup. The company's unique positioning as the first federally regulated exchange for retail event contracts highlights a growing tension between financial derivatives and traditional sports betting, raising complex questions for regulators and market participants alike.
