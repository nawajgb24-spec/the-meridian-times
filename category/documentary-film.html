Sam Green's 'The Oldest Person in the World' Explores Longevity and Memory

Filmmaker Sam Green's live documentary, 'The Oldest Person in the World,' continues to captivate audiences with its unique exploration of extreme longevity, the human experience, and the complexities of memory. The film, often presented with Green narrating in person, delves into the life of Jeanne Calment, recognized as the oldest verified person in history, while also broadly examining humanity's fascination with extended lifespans and the challenges of documentation.
