Chinese AI Models Found to Censor Politically Sensitive Topics

Recent research confirms that popular free AI models developed in China exhibit significant content restrictions, systematically filtering or refusing to engage with topics deemed politically sensitive by the Chinese government. These include issues like the Tiananmen Square protests, human rights in Xinjiang, and Taiwan's political status. The findings highlight a unique approach to AI development driven by state policy, raising global concerns about algorithmic bias, information access, and the potential for digital censorship to profoundly shape the future of artificial intelligence.
