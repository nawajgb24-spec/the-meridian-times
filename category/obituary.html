WWII POW Ben Skardon, Bataan Death March Survivor, Dies at 101

Colonel Ben Hagood Skardon, a revered World War II Prisoner of War and an icon of resilience, passed away at the age of 101, eliciting a widespread outpouring of tributes. Skardon, who endured the horrific Bataan Death March and years in Japanese captivity, later became a beloved English professor and an enduring symbol of courage through his annual participation in the Bataan Memorial Death March. His death marks the passing of an extraordinary individual whose life illuminated the indomitable human spirit and the profound sacrifices of the Greatest Generation.

Mark Mortimer, 'Another World' Star, Dies at 59

Mark Mortimer, the acclaimed actor widely recognized for his long-standing portrayal of Dr. Malcolm Reed on the beloved daytime soap opera 'Another World,' has died at the age of 59. His passing marks a significant moment for the entertainment industry and a profound loss for his dedicated fanbase. Mortimer's career spanned decades, with his most iconic role making him a staple of daytime television and earning him considerable admiration. Tributes have poured in from across the globe, highlighting his talent, professionalism, and the indelible mark he left on the genre. The specifics regarding the cause of his death remain private, though reports indicate he passed away peacefully.
