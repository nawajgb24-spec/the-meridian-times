Retracing Ancient Routes: Modern Mariners Validate Pacific Wayfinding

Modern-day voyaging expeditions are meticulously retracing the formidable routes of ancient Pacific navigators, a testament to humanity's greatest feats of exploration. Using meticulously crafted replica canoes and traditional Wayfinding techniques, these journeys are not only validating centuries-old indigenous knowledge but are also galvanizing a profound cultural renaissance and advocating for urgent ocean conservation efforts. From the pioneering voyages of Hōkūleʻa to ongoing global expeditions, the legacy of Polynesian exploration continues to inspire and educate across the world.
