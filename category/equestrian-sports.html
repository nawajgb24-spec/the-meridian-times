Germany Poised for Dominance at 2026 FEI Jumping World Championship in Aachen

The 2026 FEI Jumping World Championship, set to unfold on home soil in Aachen, Germany, sees the host nation positioned as a formidable contender for the team title. With a rich history of equestrian excellence, a deep pool of world-class riders, and the unparalleled atmosphere of Aachen's hallowed grounds, expectations are exceptionally high. While no victory is guaranteed, the confluence of talent, experience, and the significant home advantage has led many within the equestrian community to view the German team as the leading prospect, despite strong international competition.
