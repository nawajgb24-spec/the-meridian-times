ASU Students Contribute to Record-Breaking Global AI Hackathon

Arizona State University (ASU) recently played a significant role in the 'Global AI Challenge 2024,' an unprecedented artificial intelligence hackathon that secured a new world record for participant numbers. Over a 48-hour period, ASU students joined thousands globally to innovate AI-driven solutions across various sectors, underscoring the university's commitment to advancing technology and fostering future leaders in AI development.

Tencent Cloud, UTM Pioneer Malaysian AI Talent Development

A significant collaboration between Tencent Cloud and Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) is underway, aiming to cultivate a new generation of Artificial Intelligence (AI) talent in Malaysia. This partnership is designed to bridge the critical gap between academic knowledge and the practical demands of the industry by immersing students in real-world AI challenges. The initiative positions Malaysia to accelerate its digital economy goals by building a skilled workforce capable of driving innovation across various sectors.
