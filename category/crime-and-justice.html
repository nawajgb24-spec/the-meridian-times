South Florida Authorities Make 178 Sex Arrests During World Cup Period

Law enforcement agencies across South Florida conducted a series of targeted operations during the global World Cup period in late 2022, resulting in 178 arrests for various sex-related offenses. The multi-agency effort aimed to combat commercial sex activity, including prostitution and potential human trafficking, often exacerbated by large events that draw increased tourism and temporary populations. These operations highlight ongoing challenges in the region concerning illicit sex trades and the proactive measures taken by authorities to address them.
