Seattle's Urban Stadium Model Paves Way for World Cup 2026 Success

Seattle's Lumen Field, strategically located in the heart of the city, played a pivotal role in the region securing a host city slot for the FIFA World Cup 2026. This article explores how the stadium's integration with urban infrastructure, robust public transit, and existing amenities offered a distinct advantage, contrasting with common logistical challenges faced by more remote venues. The city's successful bid highlights a growing trend towards walkable, accessible, and sustainably integrated sports complexes for major global events.
