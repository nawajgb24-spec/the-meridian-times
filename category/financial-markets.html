CME Group to Launch World's First Baseball Futures on October 12

CME Group, a global leader in derivatives, is set to introduce the world's first cash-settled futures contracts based on Major League Baseball (MLB) outcomes, launching on October 12. Regulated by the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), these innovative financial products aim to offer institutional investors and market participants new opportunities for hedging and speculation within a formalized, exchange-traded environment, building upon CME's existing sports derivatives portfolio.
