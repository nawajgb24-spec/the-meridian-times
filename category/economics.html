Global Food Prices Reach Three-Year High Amid Geopolitical Tensions and Climate Woes

World food prices climbed to a three-year high in July, according to the latest data, signaling renewed pressure on global food security and household budgets. The increase is largely driven by a combination of geopolitical developments, particularly the uncertainty surrounding the Black Sea Grain Initiative, alongside persistent adverse weather conditions in key agricultural regions and elevated production costs. This latest surge underscores the volatility of international commodity markets and the complex interplay of factors influencing the affordability and availability of staple foods worldwide.
