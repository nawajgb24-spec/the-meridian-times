Wisconsin Farm Kid to Global Dairy Innovator: Dr. Lowell Catron Honored

Dr. Lowell Catron, a veterinarian whose roots trace back to a Wisconsin dairy farm, has been recognized as the 2023 World Dairy Expo Industry Person of the Year. His career spans decades of groundbreaking work in bovine reproductive technologies, including the development of synchronized breeding programs and embryo transfer techniques. This article details his journey from a rural Wisconsin upbringing to becoming a pivotal figure in advancing dairy herd efficiency and genetics on an international scale.
