Portland Grapples with Hazardous Air as Wildfire Smoke Chokes the Region

Portland, Oregon, has faced severe air quality challenges on multiple occasions, temporarily registering as the city with the worst air quality globally. This recurring issue is primarily attributed to extensive wildfire activity across the Pacific Northwest, pushing Air Quality Index (AQI) levels into the 'hazardous' range. The events prompt widespread health advisories, impact daily life, and underscore the escalating environmental concerns linked to a changing climate and persistent drought conditions in the region.
