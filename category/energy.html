Tennessee Issues Landmark License for Commercial Fusion Plant

Tennessee state regulators have issued a pivotal license for a commercial fusion energy plant, marking a significant milestone in the global pursuit of clean and abundant power. This development positions Tennessee at the forefront of advanced energy innovation, opening new avenues for the private sector to develop and deploy fusion technology. The decision represents a crucial step beyond theoretical research, signaling the maturation of fusion energy as a viable future power source.

Qinghai's Delingha CSP Plant: A Landmark in Renewable Energy

Qinghai Province, China, hosts a significant concentrated solar power (CSP) facility, the CGN Delingha 50MW plant, which has emerged as a cornerstone of the nation's renewable energy ambitions. This pioneering project, operational since 2018, integrates a 9-hour molten salt thermal energy storage system, enabling it to provide stable power to the grid even after sunset. Its strategic location in the solar-rich Delingha Industrial Park underscores China's commitment to harnessing clean energy on a grand scale, contributing significantly to carbon emission reductions and demonstrating the viability of advanced CSP technology.
