FDA Issues Class I Recall for All Impella Heart Pump Controllers After Three Deaths

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has announced a Class I recall for all Impella heart pump controllers manufactured by Abiomed, a Johnson & Johnson MedTech company. This urgent action follows reports linking the controllers to three patient deaths and multiple serious injuries. The recall addresses a critical malfunction where purge fluid can leak and crystallize within the device, potentially causing the controller to shut down or fail, thereby compromising vital cardiac support. Healthcare providers are instructed to identify, quarantine, and return affected units immediately, emphasizing the severe implications for patient safety in critical care settings.
