Real-World Data Bolsters Teclistamab's Efficacy in Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma

Recent real-world evidence has further affirmed the significant efficacy of teclistamab, a bispecific T-cell engager, in patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (R/R MM). These findings, consistent with pivotal clinical trials, demonstrate deep and durable responses, including high rates of stringent complete response and minimal residual disease negativity, within a heavily pretreated patient population. The data reinforces teclistamab's role as an important therapeutic option for patients who have exhausted traditional treatment pathways, highlighting its potential to offer sustained disease control and improved outcomes in challenging clinical settings.

Medtronic Reports Positive Real-World Ablation Data

Medtronic, a leading global medical technology company, has announced positive real-world data concerning the performance of its cardiac ablation technologies. This evidence, drawn from broad clinical practice, reaffirms the safety and efficacy of its systems used to treat atrial fibrillation (AFib). The findings are expected to reinforce confidence among medical professionals and patients, further solidifying Medtronic's position in the competitive electrophysiology market.

Mount Sinai Health System hospitals have once again been recognized in Newsweek and Statista's 'World's Best Hospitals' rankings, with The Mount Sinai Hospital securing a top global position and consistently ranking No. 1 in New York.
