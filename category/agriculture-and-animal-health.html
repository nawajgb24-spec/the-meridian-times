Screwworm Vigilance: APHIS Sustains Battle Against a Persistent Pest

The New World Screwworm, once a devastating livestock pest in the Americas, was successfully eradicated from the United States in 1966 through the innovative Sterile Insect Technique. The U.S. Department of Agriculture's Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS) has since maintained a crucial barrier zone in Panama to prevent re-infestation. Despite decades of success, the 2016 Florida Keys outbreak served as a stark reminder of the ongoing threat, underscoring the necessity of continuous surveillance, international collaboration, and public awareness in safeguarding animal health and agricultural economies.
