Fry and Glamourdale Reclaim Individual Gold at 2026 World Championships

Charlotte Fry and her magnificent stallion Glamourdale have once again ascended to the pinnacle of individual dressage, reclaiming the coveted individual gold medal at the 2026 FEI World Championships. This triumph marks a powerful resurgence, reaffirming their unparalleled partnership and solidifying their legendary status in the sport, overcoming formidable challenges to assert their dominance on the global stage.
