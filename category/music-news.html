Metallica M72 World Tour 2027: No Official Presale Tickets or Dates Confirmed

Inquiries regarding presale tickets for a Metallica M72 World Tour in 2027 have surfaced among fans. However, official sources indicate that no such tour has been announced. The actual M72 World Tour, which supported the band's '72 Seasons' album, concluded its global run in late 2024, leaving future touring plans for 2027 currently unconfirmed by the band or its representatives.
