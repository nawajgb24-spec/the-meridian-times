Qatar World Cup: A Defining Test for FIFA and Catalyst for Soccer's Evolution

The 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar marked a pivotal moment for global soccer, placing unprecedented scrutiny on FIFA's governance and the human rights implications of mega-sporting events. Awarded amid controversy, the tournament spurred significant debate over migrant worker conditions, LGBTQ+ rights, and environmental impact. While operationally successful, its legacy is defined by a complex interplay of sporting achievement and ethical challenges, prompting ongoing discussions about future host selection and the sport's social responsibilities.
