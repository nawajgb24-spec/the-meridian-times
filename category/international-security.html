UNODC's Covert Role: Securing the World Cup Beyond the Pitch

The United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) played a crucial, often unheralded, role in safeguarding the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022. Through extensive capacity-building, technical assistance, and fostering international cooperation, UNODC supported Qatari authorities in countering transnational organized crime, illicit trafficking, and terrorism, ensuring the tournament proceeded without major security incidents. This article explores the strategies, challenges, and impact of UNODC's efforts in securing one of the world's largest sporting spectacles.

White House Official Confirms No Weaponized Drones Seized During FIFA World Cup in Qatar

A White House official has stated unequivocally that no weaponized drones were seized during the recent FIFA World Cup held in Qatar. This announcement serves to clarify the security landscape surrounding the high-profile global event, amid ongoing international vigilance concerning drone technology and its potential misuse. The statement underscores the extensive security measures implemented by Qatari authorities and international partners, while also implicitly addressing broader anxieties about emerging threats at large-scale public gatherings.
