Alert Bay's Historic World-Record Totem Pole Decommissioned After Five Decades

Alert Bay, British Columbia, has witnessed the dismantling of its iconic world-record totem pole, a landmark that stood for over 50 years. Erected in 1971 by Kwakwaka'wakw artists, the 173-foot pole became a powerful symbol of Indigenous culture and resilience. The decision to take it down was made by the 'Namgis First Nation due to natural deterioration and safety concerns. While the physical structure is gone, efforts are underway to preserve sections for future cultural purposes, reflecting the ongoing vitality of Kwakwaka'wakw art and storytelling.
