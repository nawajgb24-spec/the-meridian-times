Claims of Chinese Humanoid Robot Surpassing Bolt's 100m Record Remain Unverified

Reports have circulated globally regarding a Chinese humanoid robot allegedly breaking Usain Bolt’s 100-meter world record at a dedicated 'humanoid games' event. The Meridian Times investigated these claims and found that reliable, publicly available information does not corroborate such an achievement under conditions comparable to human athletic competitions. This article delves into the context of human sprint records, the current state of advanced humanoid robotics, the significant challenges in bipedal locomotion, and the critical importance of verification when comparing human and artificial athletic performance. While robotics continues to advance rapidly, the specific claim of a robot surpassing Bolt's 9.58-second record remains unconfirmed.
