Duplantis, Jefferson-Wooden, Sawe Headline 2026 World Athletics Athlete of the Year Nominees

World Athletics has announced that pole vault sensation Mondo Duplantis, sprint specialist Melissa Jefferson-Wooden, and long-distance formidable Sabastian Sawe will headline the nominees for the prestigious 2026 Athlete of the Year awards. This early recognition underscores their consistent dominance and groundbreaking performances across various disciplines, positioning them as leading contenders for the sport's highest individual honor. The nominations highlight a diverse array of athletic excellence, promising an exciting and competitive awards season for global track and field.

Ngetich Shatters Half Marathon World Record at Copenhagen 2026

Kenyan long-distance runner Agnes Jebet Ngetich has achieved a monumental feat at the World Athletics Road Running Championships Copenhagen 2026, setting a new women's half marathon world record. Clocking an astonishing 1:02:45, Ngetich decisively broke the previous mark, solidifying her status as a global athletics phenomenon. Her emotional dedication of the win to her mother resonated widely, underscoring a powerful personal triumph alongside athletic excellence. The record-breaking performance not only elevates Ngetich's career but also reaffirms Kenya's enduring legacy in elite road running.

Nairobi to Host 2029 World Athletics Championships in Historic African First

Nairobi, the capital of Kenya, has been awarded the prestigious World Athletics Championships in 2029, marking the first time the global event will be held on the African continent. The decision, announced by the World Athletics Council in Monaco, underscores Kenya's growing capability as a host for major international sports and its profound connection to the sport of athletics. This landmark achievement is expected to catalyze significant development and inspire a new generation of athletes across Africa.
