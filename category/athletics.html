Duplantis, Jefferson-Wooden, Sawe Headline 2026 World Athletics Athlete of the Year Nominees

World Athletics has announced that pole vault sensation Mondo Duplantis, sprint specialist Melissa Jefferson-Wooden, and long-distance formidable Sabastian Sawe will headline the nominees for the prestigious 2026 Athlete of the Year awards. This early recognition underscores their consistent dominance and groundbreaking performances across various disciplines, positioning them as leading contenders for the sport's highest individual honor. The nominations highlight a diverse array of athletic excellence, promising an exciting and competitive awards season for global track and field.
