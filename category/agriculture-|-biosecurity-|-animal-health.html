Pennsylvania Mobilizes Aggressive Stance Against Flesh-Eating New World Screwworm Threat

Pennsylvania is intensifying its biosecurity measures and surveillance protocols to proactively prevent the re-entry of the New World screwworm, a highly destructive parasitic fly, into the state. Despite its eradication from the U.S. decades ago, recent re-emergences, particularly in Florida, have prompted a heightened alert. The state's aggressive stance aims to safeguard its vital agricultural sector, protect livestock and wildlife, and mitigate the severe economic and ecological consequences that an infestation could bring, relying on robust partnerships and public vigilance.
