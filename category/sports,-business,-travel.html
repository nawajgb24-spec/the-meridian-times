World Cup Semifinals Spark Late Surge of High-Spending Fans Amidst Host City Travel Boom

As the World Cup approaches its pivotal semifinal stage, a distinctive trend has emerged: a delayed arrival of high-spending fan segments. While the host city has experienced a robust general travel boom since the tournament's commencement, key demographics known for significant expenditure are consolidating their trips around the climactic matches. This pattern presents both logistical challenges and unique economic opportunities, necessitating adaptive strategies from local businesses and urban planners to manage the intensified demand for premium services, accommodation, and transportation during the tournament's final week.
