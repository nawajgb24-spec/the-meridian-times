International Hockey Faces Boycott Threat Over Russia's Participation

Several leading national ice hockey federations, including those from Sweden and Norway, have pledged to boycott the IIHF World Championships and other international tournaments if Russia is permitted to return to competition. The potential boycott highlights the deep divisions within international sports regarding the involvement of Russian athletes amidst ongoing geopolitical conflicts, posing a significant challenge to the integrity and unity of global ice hockey events.
